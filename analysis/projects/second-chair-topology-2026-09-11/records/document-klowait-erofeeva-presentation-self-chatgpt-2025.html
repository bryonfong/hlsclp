Open topic: Legal workflows: Immediate gains create competence risks

Corpus framing of this source: AI automation, professional visibility and the presentation of competence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The presentation of self in the age of ChatGPT argues that AI changes not only professional tasks but also how workers display effort, competence and credibility. Drawing on sociological theory, a co-author’s auto-ethnographic account, illustrative cases and external research, the authors explain how supplementary automation can coexist with redundancy, concealed adoption and shifts in professional identity.
