Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Generative AI in legal aid: supported deployment, manageable use cases and direct-to-consumer safeguards

Corpus synthesis of this source: Chien, Kim and Austin present a field study and participating clinic experience in which access, training and responsive human support improved legal-aid professionals’ adoption of generative AI. They argue that carefully selected uses can raise productivity and support access to justice, while verification limits, cost, informed consent, unequal organic uptake, unauthorized-practice rules and direct-to-consumer quality require deliberate safeguards. They recommend further pilots, cross-sector partnerships, shared use-case evidence, well-resourced communities of practice and quality standards.
