Corpus framing of this source: Hands-on AI learning as a condition for preserving professional judgment

Corpus synthesis of this source: Getting Your Hands Dirty argues that lawyers must personally learn to use AI to combine established judgment with new capabilities. It acknowledges lasting automation and competitive threats while presenting direct experience as a way to preserve critical thinking and replace abstract fears with practical understanding. The passage offers experiential reasoning and predictions rather than measured evidence.
