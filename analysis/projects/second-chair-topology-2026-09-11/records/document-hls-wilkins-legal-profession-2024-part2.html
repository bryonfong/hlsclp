Corpus source-specific finding

Interdisciplinary problems are pushing lawyers toward an integrated solutions model.

Exact returned source excerpt

But the question is what does “thinking like a lawyer” mean in an age where lawyers are increasingly being asked to deal with complex interdisciplinary problems like sustainability and cybersecurity and the geopolitical risk of doing business in China — problems, as I indicated before, that are increasingly “legalized” and yet extend far beyond law.

Exact returned source excerpt

All these changes are pushing law from a “fee for service” model,” in which clients bring lawyers “legal questions,” and lawyers provide “legal answers” which the client then implements, to what I’ve been calling an “integrated solutions model.” In this model, neither the contours of the problem nor the possible array of solutions is strictly “legal” or fully understood by either the client or the lawyer.
