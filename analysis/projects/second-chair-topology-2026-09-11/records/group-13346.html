AI-training fair use: Lawfulness turns on specific copying and market substitution

Corpus synthesis: AI training is not categorically lawful or unlawful under copyright; the analysis depends on what is copied, at which stage, for what product purpose and with what substitutive effect. The exemplars vary between separating acquisition, retention, training and distribution, and weighing transformativeness, editorial or public-law uses, competition and market harm.
