Corpus framing of this source: Adverse selection can prevent insurance markets from clearing

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Market for 'Lemons': Quality Uncertainty and the Market Mechanism applies its automobile-market reasoning to insurance. It argues that applicants’ informational advantage can make higher premiums worsen the risk pool and potentially eliminate trade, presents historical survey findings as consistent rather than conclusive evidence, and distinguishes adverse selection from moral hazard.

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that difficulty distinguishing quality can obstruct trade, restrict insurance and exclude qualified minority workers.
