Corpus framing of this source: Margaret Hagan & Sateesh Nori on urgent, evidence-led deployment of generative AI for legal services

Corpus synthesis of this source: The discussion presents generative AI as a potentially immediate response to a severe justice gap, while treating implementation as an experimental, evidence-building process. The speakers identify user-facing information, pro bono copilots, intake and triage, paperwork automation, and litigant coaching as priority applications. They recommend judging these tools against current service failures rather than idealised alternatives, coordinating pilots across institutions and jurisdictions, and working with technology companies on legal-domain guardrails. They also acknowledge technological uncertainty, ethical risks, harmful deployment, and the digital divide.
