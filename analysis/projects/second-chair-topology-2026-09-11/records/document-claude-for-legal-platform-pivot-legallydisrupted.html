Corpus synthesis of this source: Zach Abramowitz examines whether lawyers building directly on Claude could challenge Harvey and Legora. He reports a Quinn Emanuel implementation and Freshfields partnerships, while framing large productivity gains and competitive displacement as possibilities rather than established outcomes.
