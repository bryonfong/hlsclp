Evidence for deployed AI: Proxy measures do not establish real-world effectiveness

Corpus synthesis: Benchmark scores, rankings, standalone accuracy, speed gains, audits, taxonomies and product inventories provide bounded evidence but cannot by themselves establish reliable task completion, workflow improvement, fairness, adoption or professional impact. The exemplars span legal, medical, coding and hiring systems, with results varying by task design, configuration, grading, user behaviour and institutional setting. Stronger claims require realistic end-to-end testing, appropriate outcome measures, verification and continuing lifecycle controls.
