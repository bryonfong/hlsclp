Delegated legal work: responsibility follows the provider chain without becoming strict liability

Corpus synthesis: Lawyers retain proportionate duties of diligence, supervision and client protection when legal work is delegated to AI systems or external providers, but a delegate’s error does not automatically establish discipline. The exemplars apply this principle differently to provider scrutiny and instructions, outsourcing charges and client consent, and cross-border risks involving confidentiality, conflicts and unauthorized practice.
