Corpus framing of this source: Fighting for Your Light: Anusia Gillespie on AI, Legal Innovation, and the Soul Toll of Law

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anusia Gillespie connects lawyer well-being with legal technology. She recommends using the Sol-Tol framework to monitor physical, mental and emotional health; argues that shifting drudgery to machines can create space for higher-level, mission-connected legal work; and attributes heightened professional enthusiasm to generative AI’s immediacy compared with conventional automation. She also describes trusted professional networks as a check on media hype. These contributions rest on her experience and observations rather than empirical evidence, and the supplied material contains no substantive response to the closing prediction question.
