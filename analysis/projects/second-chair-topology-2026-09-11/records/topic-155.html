Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Future of Jobs Report 2025: employer forecasts of technological change, job reallocation and skills needs

Corpus synthesis of this source: The report uses the World Economic Forum’s 2024 employer survey to describe expected transformation through 2030. It forecasts simultaneous job creation and displacement, rising demand for digital and human-centred skills, and widespread investment in training and automation. Its economy, region and industry profiles show substantial variation; these are employer expectations and plans, not measured effects of AI adoption.
