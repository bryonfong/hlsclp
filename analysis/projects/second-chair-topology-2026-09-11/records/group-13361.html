AI and labour-market adjustment: Headline exposure and employment counts obscure uneven worker outcomes

Corpus synthesis: Task exposure and aggregate employment measures do not by themselves establish job displacement, because employer capacity, adoption constraints, demand, redeployment and reskilling mediate how automation affects whole occupations. The evidence distinguishes effects on new entrants, displaced incumbents, wages, job quality and career progression, showing that stable or growing employment can coexist with lasting harm or occupational hollowing. Estimates remain sensitive to datasets, causal methods, implementation details and the extent to which projected adjustments actually occur.
