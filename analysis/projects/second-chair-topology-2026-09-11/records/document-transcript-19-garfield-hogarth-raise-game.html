Corpus framing of this source: Robert Hogarth on automated litigation valuation and AI-driven lawyer quality

Corpus synthesis of this source: Hogarth explains how Settle Index combines expected-value modelling with AI extraction from pleadings, argues that early financial modelling can prevent wasteful litigation expenditure, and predicts that broad LLM capabilities will raise the minimum expected quality of legal work. He bases these conclusions mainly on product experience and observed outputs, while acknowledging the continuing need for human judgment and supervision. His interlocutor separately frames AI as a possible support for, or competitive threat to, weaker lawyers and highlights the rapid progress of frontier models.
