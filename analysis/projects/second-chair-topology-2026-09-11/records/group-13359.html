Access to justice: Effective resolution requires more than lawyer supply

Corpus synthesis: The justice gap is defined by whether people can achieve fair, durable outcomes, not simply by the availability or price of legal assistance. The exemplars vary between identifying barriers such as resources, recognition, trust, language and capability, and comparing representation, lay assistance and procedural reform by their practical benefits.
