Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Access to What? — Rebecca L. Sandefur

Corpus synthesis of this source: Sandefur reframes access to justice as the achievement of lawful, just resolution rather than the provision of lawyers. Drawing on social-scientific studies and institutional examples, she argues for matching interventions to problems, reforming malfunctioning systems, broadening participation beyond the legal profession and investing in independent civil-justice research.
