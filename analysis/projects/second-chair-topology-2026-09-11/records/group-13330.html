Minimum-wage adjustment: Headline increases obscure real protection and aggregate effects

Corpus synthesis: The effects of minimum-wage increases depend on adjustment frequency, implementation delays, indexation rules, exceptions and workforce coverage, not simply the announced rate. The exemplars vary between assessing purchasing-power protection for low-paid workers, the comparability of adjustment systems across jurisdictions, and the limited contribution of minimum-wage changes to aggregate wage inflation.
