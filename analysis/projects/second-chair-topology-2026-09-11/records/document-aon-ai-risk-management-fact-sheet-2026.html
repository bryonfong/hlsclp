Corpus framing of this source: Aon: AI insurance clarity, measurable controls and proportionate governance

Corpus synthesis of this source: Aon’s Artificial Intelligence Risk Management 2026 fact sheet presents insurance as both protection against AI losses and an incentive for safer adoption. It combines directional litigation-to-coverage analysis, reported market evidence and forecasts with a practical governance roadmap, while acknowledging limited loss data, policy-specific exclusions and uncertainty over future harms.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI adoption has outpaced risk management, leaving substantial insurance ambiguity.

Exact returned source excerpt

As a result, to the extent that evolving AI perils might be covered by existing insurance policies, such coverage falls over ninety percent (90%) under “Silent AI” insurance.1 Silent AI coverage means that the underlying policy neither affirmatively covers AI nor excludes AI – the policies don’t mention AI.

Exact returned source excerpt

It took the Lloyd’s of London Market Association eighteen (18) years of coverage ambiguity to finally mandate that all policies must either affirmatively specify cyber coverage grants or include specific cyber exclusions (no more “Silent Cyber” insurance).
