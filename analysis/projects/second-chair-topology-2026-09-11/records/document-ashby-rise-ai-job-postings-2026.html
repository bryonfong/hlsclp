Corpus framing of this source: Ashby: The Rise of AI in Job Postings

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ben Perry’s Ashby report analyses more than 200,000 job postings and descriptions from 2021 through Q2 2026 to measure how often employers mention AI. It defines AI jobs broadly by relevant AI terminology in either titles or descriptions and reports variation by role, function and company size.

Corpus source-specific finding

The report uses a large, multi-year dataset and a broad text-based definition of AI jobs.
