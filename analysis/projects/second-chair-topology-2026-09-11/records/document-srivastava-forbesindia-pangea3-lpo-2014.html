Corpus framing of this source: Pangea3: legal outsourcing moves from cost reduction toward value creation

Corpus synthesis of this source: In this January 2014 interview, Pangea3’s managing director describes growing client acceptance, illustrates offshore teams’ substantive litigation support, and anticipates benefits from combining Pangea3’s services with Thomson Reuters’ content and software. The account relies on engagement examples and the executive’s expectations, rather than market-wide evidence.

Corpus synthesis of this source: In a 2014 Forbes India interview, Pangea3's managing director presents legal process outsourcing as a growing industry and an attractive career option for Indian attorneys.
