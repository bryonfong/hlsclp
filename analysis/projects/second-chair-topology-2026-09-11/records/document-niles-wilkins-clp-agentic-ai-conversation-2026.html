Corpus framing of this source: Sabastian Niles on trusted agents, human oversight and legal teamwork

Corpus synthesis of this source: Niles advocates governed, human-aligned AI agents and describes Salesforce’s efforts to embed them across workflows. He links anticipated business benefits to clear permissions, human oversight and workforce investment. The material offers executive experience and design recommendations rather than quantified evidence of outcomes; Wilkins contributes a delegation analogy that Niles endorses.

Corpus source-specific finding

Exact returned source excerpt

If you think about it, those categories—roles, tasks, access, guardrails—sound a lot like how we define human jobs and regulate behavior.

Combine technical controls with legal thinking about rules, standards and accountability.

We’re moving toward autonomous processes and agents that can responsibly reason, plan, and act on behalf of individuals, teams, and the company.
