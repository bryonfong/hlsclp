Corpus framing of this source: Brazilian judicial AI: rapid adoption, fragmented governance and rights-based oversight

Corpus synthesis of this source: The study maps AI deployment across Brazil’s courts, analyses selected systems and incidents, and reviews the evolving regulatory framework. It argues that caseload pressure has encouraged extensive but decentralised adoption, while limited transparency, uncertain human verification, high-risk applications and private-sector partnerships create potential risks to fundamental rights and judicial legitimacy. It treats Resolution CNJ No. 615/2025 as a substantial regulatory advance but questions implementation capacity and recommends empirical research, stronger public oversight, digital literacy and adaptive binding governance.
