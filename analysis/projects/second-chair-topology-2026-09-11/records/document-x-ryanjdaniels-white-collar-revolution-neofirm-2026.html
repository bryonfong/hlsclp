Corpus framing of this source: Ryan Daniels’s “Neofirm” thesis for AI-native professional services

Corpus synthesis of this source: Daniels argues that AI will favour a new corporate form combining practitioners with engineers, long-term R&D capital, outcome-based pricing and measurable quality. Drawing on Crosby’s experience, he presents this model as an alternative to partnerships that distribute annual profits, bill by the hour and buy external software. He predicts that increasingly capable LLMs, evaluations and agentic workflows will blur the boundary between professional services and software and materially redesign client service.
