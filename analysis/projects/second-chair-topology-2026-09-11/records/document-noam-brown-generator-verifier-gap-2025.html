Corpus framing of this source: OpenAI’s o1 team on inference-time scaling and reasoning

Corpus synthesis of this source: Noam Brown, Hunter Lightman and Ilge Akkaya present o1 as an empirically supported but still developing approach to improving reasoning by allocating more computation at inference time. They describe observed gains from longer thinking, explain why verifiable STEM tasks benefit, identify engineering and evaluation bottlenecks, predict broader research and software applications, and qualify long-run claims with diminishing returns and substantial uncertainty.

Corpus source-specific finding

The team presents inference-time compute as a new, relatively untapped scaling dimension that raises the prospective ceiling for AI beyond reliance on increasingly costly pre-training.
