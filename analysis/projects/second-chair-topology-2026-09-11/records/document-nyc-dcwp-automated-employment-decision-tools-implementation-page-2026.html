Open topic: Employment screening: Audits reveal bias without remedying it

Corpus framing of this source: DCWP guidance on automated employment decision tool requirements

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Automated Employment Decision Tools (AEDT): DCWP Implementation Page explains New York City's Local Law 144 compliance requirements, announces enforcement timing and provides complaint and educational resources. It presents agency implementation guidance, not empirical findings about whether audits reduce bias.

Corpus source-specific finding

Employers and employment agencies must meet audit, disclosure and notice conditions before using an AEDT.

Exact returned source excerpt

(“AEDT”) prohibits employers and employment agencies from using an automated employment decision tool unless the tool has been subject to a bias audit within one year of the use of the tool, information about the bias audit is publicly available, and certain notices have been provided to employees or job candidates.\ [Read Local Law 144 of 2021](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4344524&GUID=B051915D-A9AC-451E-81F8-6596032FA3F9&Options=ID%7CText%7C&Search=)\ [Read Rule](https://rules.cityofnewyork.us/rule/automated-employment-decision-tools-updated/).\ *Note: DCWP will begin enforcement of this law and rule on July 5, 2023.* ## FAQ Read [Automated Employment Decision Tools: Frequently Asked Questions](/assets/dca/downloads/pdf/about/DCWP-AEDT-FAQ.pdf) ## File a Complaint You can report an employer or employment agency that used an AEDT but did not: - Make sure the required bias audit was done - Post a summary of the results of the bias audit - Give required notices [Submit your complaint online](https://a866-dcwpbp.nyc.gov/consumer-complaint/file-complaint).
