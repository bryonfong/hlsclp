Corpus framing of this source: Lnu v. Blanche: lawyer responsibility for AI-assisted court filings

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Ninth Circuit holds that existing procedural and professional-conduct rules govern inaccurate filings regardless of whether generative AI, subordinates or lawyers produced the errors. It distinguishes permissible AI use from unchecked reliance, adopts a broad conception of hallucination covering both fabrications and inaccuracies, requires lawyers personally to read and verify cited authorities, and treats prompt correction and transparent disclosure as duties of candor. Applying those principles, it disciplines attorneys for filing fabricated and misrepresented authorities and for repeatedly giving false explanations afterward.
