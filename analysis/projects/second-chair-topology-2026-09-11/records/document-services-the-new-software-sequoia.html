Corpus framing of this source: Services: The New Software — Autopilot opportunities in outsourced intelligence work

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source identifies medical coding, claims adjusting, tax advisory, transactional legal work and managed IT as opportunities for AI systems that perform outsourced services directly rather than merely supply software tools. It bases the thesis on rules or standardised outputs, repeatable workflows, mature outsourcing, labour constraints and cross-customer or cross-jurisdiction data advantages. It names emerging providers in four categories but gives no explicit recommendation or implementation qualification.
