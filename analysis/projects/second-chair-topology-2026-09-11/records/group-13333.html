Credence services: Verifiability alone cannot secure appropriate treatment

Corpus synthesis: Evidence from theory, experiments and field settings indicates that making expert work observable—or lowering its price—does not necessarily prevent fraud, unnecessary service or unsuitable treatment. Outcomes depend more directly on incentives, liability and institutional safeguards, while reputation, trade and price provide inconsistent proxies for quality.
