Corpus framing of this source: The Promise and Peril of AI Legal Services to Equalize Justice

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ashwin Telang argues that AI legal services could reduce the US civil justice gap by making legal information and routine assistance cheaper and more accessible, but could instead deepen inequality through unequal access, cultural and racial bias, market concentration and displacement of human-centred policy. He recommends transparency, competition and supervised regulatory sandboxes rather than accepting low-quality technology as better than no assistance.
