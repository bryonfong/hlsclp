Corpus framing of this source: How AI is Transforming Access to Justice in India

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents AI-enabled grievance redressal and legal assistance as tools for closing systemic access-to-justice gaps in India. It uses Next-Gen CPGRAMS as its main institutional example and argues that AI’s significance lies less in speed than in redistributing practical access, reducing dependence on intermediaries and enabling more responsive public institutions.

Corpus source-specific finding

Next-Gen CPGRAMS reframes grievance redressal as a standards-based management system.

Exact returned source excerpt

To tap into its full potential, the government is rethinking CPGRAMS, not just as a portal for filing grievances but as a framework for managing them efficiently through a system of standards and protocols.

Exact returned source excerpt

The next phase, Next-Gen CPGRAMS, is being developed as an open-source Digital Public Good to set standards and protocols for grievance redressal.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI is expected to improve grievance routing, prioritisation, accessibility and policy insight.

Exact returned source excerpt

AI will play a key role in improving how grievances are filed, ensuring they reach the right department, and helping officials prioritise cases.

Exact returned source excerpt

Language translation features will make the system more accessible, while AI-generated dashboards will surface trends that indicate areas for policy intervention and process improvement.

#### **Keeping People At The Centre** AI in law, particularly in India isn’t just about automation or efficiency—it’s about bridging systemic gaps that have long kept justice out of reach.

Exact returned source excerpt

Arming truckdrivers with legal information to negotiate with the police, farmers trying to claim entitlements, or citizens navigating government grievance systems—each of these interactions reflect deep system challenges.

Exact returned source excerpt

Exact returned source excerpt

The introduction of AI to bridge our systems of justice is not just an infusion of technology; it’s a fundamental shift in power.
