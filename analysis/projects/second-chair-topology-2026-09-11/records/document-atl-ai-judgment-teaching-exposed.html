Corpus framing of this source: Teaching legal judgment explicitly through AI-supported reasoning

Corpus synthesis of this source: Olga V. Mack argues that AI exposes weaknesses in legal training rather than replacing legal judgment. Drawing on Product Law Hub classroom pilots with the AI coach Frankie, she reports benefits from contextual explanations and explicit modeling of reasoning, then recommends that law schools and firms teach these skills deliberately.

Corpus framing of this source: AI as a diagnostic and instructional aid for legal judgment

Corpus synthesis of this source: Olga V. Mack argues from Product Law Hub classroom pilots that AI does not displace legal judgment; instead, it reveals shortcomings in how legal education and law-firm training develop that judgment. She presents contextual explanation—especially links between legal analysis and practical consequences—as the feature associated with stronger student engagement and learning.
