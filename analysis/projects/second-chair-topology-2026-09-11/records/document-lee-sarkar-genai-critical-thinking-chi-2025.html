Corpus framing of this source: GenAI shifts critical thinking toward oversight, with confidence shaping reported engagement

Corpus synthesis of this source: Lee and colleagues’ CHI 2025 study analyses knowledge workers’ self-reported GenAI use. It finds widespread reductions in perceived cognitive effort alongside new demands for verification, integration and stewardship. The authors distinguish these associations from causal evidence of skill loss and recommend tools and training that preserve human judgement and accountability.
