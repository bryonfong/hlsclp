Creative diversity: Individual gains can homogenise groups

Corpus synthesis: Separates output quality from collective diversity and treats personas as preliminary mitigation rather than a proven solution.

This record retains the provider’s full available topic findings and source perspectives. It does not claim a new independent analytical review. Positions refer to this topic’s proposition: “Redirects” is not simple opposition, and “Unclear” is not support.

6 distinct stance-bearing works · Builds on: 1 · Supports: 4 · Unclear: 1

Native topic findings

  1. AI can improve individual creative output while narrowing collective diversity; the two outcomes require separate assessment.
  2. Homogenisation is not a uniform finding: exposure settings and input design may change the direction of diversity effects.
  3. Diversified AI personas offer preliminary mitigation evidence, not a demonstrated solution to the quality–diversity trade-off.
  4. Extensive retention of AI text does not establish passive copying or eliminate human creative contribution.
  5. Creation tasks look more promising for human–AI collaboration than predefined-choice decisions, but gains are not guaranteed.
  6. Practical deployment should test individual quality, collective distinctiveness and longer-term effects separately.
