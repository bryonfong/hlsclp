Corpus framing of this source: Legal Cheek discussion: contested forecasts for AI, paralegals and legal work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Legal Cheek frames AI-driven legal-job displacement as an open question, while its anonymous commenters supply sharply conflicting predictions rather than systematic evidence. Their contributions range from wholesale professional replacement to narrower junior-headcount reductions and task transformation. Counterarguments stress hallucinations, accountability, professional indemnity, negotiation and real-world legal judgment. A litigant-in-person reports that AI already handles preliminary research and correspondence but not higher-stakes stages, and predicts pressure on hourly billing for rapidly generated documents.

Corpus source-specific finding

Legal Cheek presents paralegal displacement as a prediction to scrutinise, not as its own settled conclusion.
