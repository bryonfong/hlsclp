Adaptive medical devices: Planned modification requires lifecycle regulatory control

Corpus synthesis: AI-enabled device changes can remain within regulatory control when authorized prospectively through defined boundaries, testing and risk controls, with fresh review for changes outside that scope. The exemplars vary in emphasis between modification plans and a broader governance framework incorporating classification, representative testing and public records, while cautioning that device inventories alone are not complete clinical evidence.
