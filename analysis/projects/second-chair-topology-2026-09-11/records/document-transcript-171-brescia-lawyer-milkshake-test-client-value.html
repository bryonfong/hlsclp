Corpus framing of this source: Ray Brescia on verification, hallucinations and legal AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Brescia argues that generative AI can accelerate and reduce the cost of legal work, but lawyers must independently verify every authority and proposition before relying on it. He uses inherited briefs, hallucination incidents and the risk of judicial repetition to explain why verification is a basic competence obligation, while predicting continued client pressure to use AI-generated legal material.
