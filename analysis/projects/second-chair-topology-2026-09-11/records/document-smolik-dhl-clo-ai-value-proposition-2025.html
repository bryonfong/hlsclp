Corpus synthesis of this source: Mark A. Smolik, Chief Legal Officer of DHL Supply Chain Americas, argues that AI should augment rather than replace legal professionals. Drawing on earlier technological transitions and current law-department adoption, he predicts that AI and structured data will automate routine work, improve decisions and service economics, strengthen buyers’ leverage, and return time to legal professionals.
