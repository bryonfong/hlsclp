Corpus framing of this source: Governing AI through auditable processes under persistent assurance limits

Corpus synthesis of this source: AI Governance and the Verification Gap: A Framework for Law and Policy Under Computational Intractability argues that limits on comprehensive model assurance create parallel problems for liability, oversight and enterprise risk. It proposes complementary technical testing, enforceable process audits, risk-tiered liability and research into meaningful human oversight. Its contribution is a claimed formal justification for process governance, not the invention of that approach.

Exact returned source excerpt
