Corpus synthesis of this source: In Harvey Power Users: The Champions Network — Alex Bazin at Lewis Silkin, Bazin offers qualitative observations and practical recommendations for legal-AI adoption. He distinguishes senior lawyers’ capacity for creative use from associates’ high-volume applications and advocates spreading effective practices through workflow-aware champions and peer examples. The article’s closing synthesis emphasises organisational support rather than individual expertise alone.
