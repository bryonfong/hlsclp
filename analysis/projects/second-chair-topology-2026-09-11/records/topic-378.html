Native topic findings

  1. The central question is whether cheaper legal assistance delivers effective remedies, not merely more information or filings.
  2. Federal filing studies associate the generative-AI era with more self-representation, but do not establish that AI caused the increase.
  3. More polished complaints and greater participation do not yet demonstrate better legal outcomes.
  4. Lower drafting costs may transfer work to courts rather than reduce total institutional burdens.
  5. Provider augmentation offers a distinct pathway, but practical examples do not establish population-level access gains.
