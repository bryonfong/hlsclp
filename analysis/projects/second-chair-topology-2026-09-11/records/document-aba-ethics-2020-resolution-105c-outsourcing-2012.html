Corpus framing of this source: Outsourcing preserves lawyers’ duties of competence, supervision and accountability

Corpus synthesis of this source: ABA Commission on Ethics 20/20, Resolution 105C: Outsourcing sets out an ethical framework for using outside lawyers and nonlawyer assistance. Its rule and comment text addresses provider selection, confidentiality, client consultation, supervision, technological competence and jurisdictional limits; it offers normative requirements and guidance rather than empirical findings or predictions.
