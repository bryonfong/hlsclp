Corpus framing of this source: Court adoption, disclosure and transparency for generative AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bridget McCormack and Jen Leonard examine why courts struggle to adopt generative AI, when judicial use may warrant disclosure, and how transparency expectations may evolve as AI becomes embedded in routine tools. McCormack attributes much judicial concern to public-sector resource and training constraints, argues that operational improvement need not erase litigants’ voices, questions a permanent disclosure rule analogous to revealing clerks or research tools, but recommends broad transparency during the current transition. Leonard reports concerns about preserving litigants’ own language and supports context-sensitive disclosure, literacy and training.
