Corpus framing of this source: Lynda Shely on Arizona ABS licensing, professional safeguards and regulatory experimentation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Shely describes Arizona’s nonlawyer-ownership regime as a closely regulated experiment that attracts investment and technology expertise while reserving legal practice to lawyers. She reports licensing and disciplinary experience, explains operational safeguards, anticipates further protections for professional independence and contrasts developments elsewhere. She speaks personally, not for Arizona’s Supreme Court or its ABS committee.
