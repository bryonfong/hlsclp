Corpus framing of this source: Canaries, Interest Rates, and Timing: More on the Recent Drivers of Employment Changes for Young Workers

Corpus synthesis of this source: Erik Brynjolfsson, Bharat Chandar and Ruyu Chen revisit their “Canaries in the Coal Mine” findings to test whether interest rates better explain declining entry-level employment in AI-exposed occupations and whether the timing fits an AI-related account. They reject interest rates as a strong explanation, refine the estimated onset to 2024 under firm-time controls, document a growing and unreversed employment decline through October 2025, and stress both data limitations and the need for continued monitoring.
