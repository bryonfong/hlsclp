Corpus framing of this source: Metacognition's Law: Thinking About Thinking in the Age of AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that effective cognitive work with AI depends less on outperforming machines in speed or efficiency and more on navigating ambiguity, understanding human thought and reflecting on how one thinks. It presents this as an emerging observation and a qualified prediction rather than supplying empirical evidence.
