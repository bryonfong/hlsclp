Corpus synthesis of this source: The source reports a gap between EvenUp’s automation proposition and former employees’ experience of extensive human correction and completion work. It contrasts those accounts with EvenUp’s defence of a deliberately hybrid human-AI model, its CEO’s claim of continuing improvement, and favourable assessments from a customer and investor. It uses the dispute to illustrate the broader difficulty of translating generative-AI ambitions into reliable workplace performance.
