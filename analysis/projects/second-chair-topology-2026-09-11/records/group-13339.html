Platform infrastructure: Openness and convenience can entrench private gatekeepers

Corpus synthesis: Platforms can enable external innovation and convenient interconnection while retaining concentrated control through their architecture and incentives. The exemplars vary in emphasis: one focuses on governance and unresolved collective costs, while the other treats data and network effects as sources of infrastructural power rather than features of a neutral marketplace.
