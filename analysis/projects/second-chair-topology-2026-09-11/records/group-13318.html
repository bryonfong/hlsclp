AI governance implementation: Formal oversight must become enforceable lifecycle control

Corpus synthesis: The exemplars frame effective governance as an implementation problem requiring auditable evidence, continuing evaluation, clear responsibility and enforceable safeguards throughout deployment. They contrast the EU’s differentiated risk rules with Japan’s voluntary, innovation-first approach and proposals for competing private compliance methods. In procurement, courts, auditing and insurance, they caution that adoption, nominal human oversight or coverage cannot substitute for substantive control, while leaving institutional independence, cross-border consistency and assurance quality unresolved.
