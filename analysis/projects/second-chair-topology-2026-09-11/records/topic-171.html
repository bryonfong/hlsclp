Native topic record

Collaborative transformation: Cohesive networks preserve gatekeeping

Corpus synthesis: Explains renewal through varied boundary-crossing configurations whose central actors could recruit newcomers while retaining power.

This record retains the provider’s full available topic findings and source perspectives. It does not claim a new independent analytical review. Positions refer to this topic’s proposition: “Redirects” is not simple opposition, and “Unclear” is not support.

Declared lineage: Cross-domain institutional evidence: Innovation and market correction remain conditional on incentives, gatekeeping and measurement · Cross-domain innovation networks: Mobility and open anchors generate new forms without displacing incumbents

5 distinct stance-bearing works · Supports: 5

Native topic findings

  1. The accounts explain industry transformation through collaboration rather than wholesale incumbent replacement.
  2. Boundary-crossing changed research, governance and financing, but firms adopted different mixtures.
  3. Organizational diversity became productive when anchors connected participants and shared central roles.
  4. The sequence and configuration of collaboration mattered more than collaboration volume alone.
  5. A cohesive network elite could recruit newcomers while retaining gatekeeping power.
  6. Institutional influence did not imply superior profitability, independence or survival.
  7. Collaboration depended on unequal resources and public support, not self-sufficient start-ups.
  8. Network resilience remained conditional on discovery, funding and continued access to newcomers.
  9. The evidence supports a historically bounded explanation, not a universal cluster-building recipe.

Corpus synthesis of this source: In “An Open Elite: Arbiters, Catalysts, or Gatekeepers in the Dynamics of Industry Evolution?”, the authors use biotechnology alliance data from 1984–2004, historical accounts and subsequent developments to explain industry transformation through collaboration rather than wholesale incumbent replacement.
