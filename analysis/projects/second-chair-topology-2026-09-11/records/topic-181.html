Native topic record

Innovation networks: Open anchors can sustain elites

Corpus synthesis: Explains organizational change through community ties, mobility and institutional feedback rather than technology or financial incentives alone.

This record retains the provider’s full available topic findings and source perspectives. It does not claim a new independent analytical review. Positions refer to this topic’s proposition: “Redirects” is not simple opposition, and “Unclear” is not support.

Declared lineage: Cross-domain institutional evidence: Innovation and market correction remain conditional on incentives, gatekeeping and measurement · Cross-domain innovation networks: Mobility and open anchors generate new forms without displacing incumbents

11 distinct stance-bearing works · Builds on: 3 · Supports: 3 · Unclear: 5

Native topic findings

  1. Organisational innovation depends on relationships and institutional choices, not technological breakthroughs alone.
  2. Diversity generates regional innovation when community-oriented anchors connect organisations and support mobility.
  3. Boundary crossing creates new uses for existing practices, not merely wider diffusion.
  4. An open innovation network can sustain a powerful elite without necessarily becoming closed.
  5. Institutional rules can produce commercialisation through unintended feedback rather than financial incentives alone.
  6. Lasting institutional influence does not establish commercial superiority or preservation of scientific openness.
  7. The practical lesson concerns enabling relationships, not replicating a universal development recipe.
