Corpus framing of this source: When Does Verification Pay Off? A Closer Look at LLMs as Solution Verifiers

Corpus synthesis of this source: Lu et al. systematically compare LLM verification across self-, intra-family and cross-family settings, covering base and reasoning post-trained models. They introduce verifier gain, validate it against rejection sampling, relate verification performance to solver–verifier similarity, model strength, post-training and task type, and derive design recommendations while noting measurement and verifier limitations.

Corpus source-specific finding

The study broadens verification analysis beyond self-verification and introduces verifier gain as its principal metric.

Exact returned source excerpt

To the best of our knowledge, our work is the first systematic study of solver–verifier interactions across self-, intra-family, and cross-family regimes, spanning both base and reasoning post-trained models.

Exact returned source excerpt

Verifier accuracy alone provides an incomplete picture of verifier use- fulness at test time.
