Corpus framing of this source: Legal-tech adoption after the large-language-model inflection point

Corpus synthesis of this source: In “A Deep Dive into Legal Tech Investment with TLTF's Zach Posner,” Posner argues that compelling large-language-model demonstrations made AI strategically unavoidable for legal businesses, even before performance was fully established. He advocates firm-wide strategy and change management for major implementations while treating individual lawyers as useful judges of workflow-specific tools. He anticipates near-term pressure on revenue from lower-level work but sees firms beginning to embrace the technology. He also stresses that less conspicuous incremental workflow products remain a major investment opportunity. Separately, host Greg Lambert reports buyer stress caused by the volume of solutions entering the market.
