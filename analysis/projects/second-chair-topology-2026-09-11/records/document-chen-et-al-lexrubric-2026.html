Corpus framing of this source: LEXRUBRIC: fine-grained evaluation for open-ended Chinese legal tasks

Corpus synthesis of this source: The authors introduce a diagnostic benchmark that uses expert-written, weighted atomic rubrics to evaluate open-ended Chinese legal answers across legal consultation and judicial examination. They argue that conventional leaderboards and standardized-answer benchmarks obscure material differences in accuracy, completeness, safety, reasoning and task-specific robustness. Their evaluation of 18 models finds substantial remaining difficulty, especially on a constructed hard subset, while cross-judge comparisons support the ranking method's reliability. They limit the benchmark to evaluation rather than deployment assurance and recommend human review for consequential legal uses.
