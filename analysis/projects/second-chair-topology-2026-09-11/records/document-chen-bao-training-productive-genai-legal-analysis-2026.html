Corpus synthesis of this source: The study tests whether targeted training changes law students’ adoption and productive use of DeepSeek during an issue-spotting examination. It distinguishes increased adoption from improved effectiveness conditional on adoption, finding that training expands use and is associated with higher grade-point performance, while access alone produces no average performance gain. The authors qualify the causal mechanism and external validity, recommend integrating human–AI collaboration into professional education, and call for field replications, longitudinal research and stronger identification designs.
