Corpus framing of this source: The Landscape of Civil Litigation in State Courts

Corpus synthesis of this source: The National Center for State Courts uses a large, multi-jurisdictional case-level dataset to challenge high-stakes, trial-centred accounts of state civil litigation. It depicts a system dominated by modest-value contract, debt, housing and small-claims matters; administrative dispositions; limited formal adjudication; substantial self-representation; and costs and delays that impede access to justice. It qualifies cross-court and historical comparisons because court data and definitions vary, then calls for stronger court-led case management and operational reform.
