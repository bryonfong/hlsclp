Corpus framing of this source: Mitch Zuklie on technology, client sophistication and distributed legal-service delivery

Corpus synthesis of this source: Orrick chair Mitch Zuklie argues that law firms increasingly compete through technology and, potentially, proprietary data; sophisticated in-house legal operations now exert greater pressure on firms to improve value and internal decision-making. He also describes Orrick’s no-headquarters model as a way to maintain industry-relevant local presences while assembling matter teams globally, and says digitisation has eliminated costly administrative work and accelerated associates’ substantive experience.
