Corpus framing of this source: Lone Star Legal Aid’s problem-led, human-supervised approach to AI chatbots

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ashley Oborn and Sourav Mohan describe Lone Star Legal Aid’s development of three internally built chatbots to centralize legal information, expand service capacity and support public referrals. They present AI as an assistive tool rather than a substitute for people, emphasize expert-curated data, source citations and constrained responses, and explain the organizational resources, buy-in and realistic timeline required. Host Cat Moon adds contextual figures and observations, while the guests supply the project’s principal claims and implementation lessons.
