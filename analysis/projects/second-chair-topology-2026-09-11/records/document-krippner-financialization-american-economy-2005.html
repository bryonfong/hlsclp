Corpus framing of this source: Krippner: Measuring financialization through US corporate accumulation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Greta R. Krippner’s 2005 paper documents a post-1970s shift toward financial sources of US corporate income and profits. It develops complementary measures, tests accounting and organizational objections, and proposes implications for research on corporate control and state autonomy. Corpus framing of this source: The Financialization of the American Economy

Corpus synthesis of this source: Krippner’s principal contribution is an aggregate empirical framework for establishing the existence, timing and magnitude of US financialization. She argues that financial activities increasingly generate income for non-financial firms and that finance has become a privileged site of accumulation. She uses this perspective to move beyond unresolved bank-control debates and to propose implications for corporate behaviour, labour and the welfare state, while emphasizing measurement biases, alternative explanations and the need for firm-level research.
