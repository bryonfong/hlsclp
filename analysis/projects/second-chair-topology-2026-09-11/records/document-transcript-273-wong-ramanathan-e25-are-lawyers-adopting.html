They attribute faster in-house adoption to cost pressure, corporate investment and executive direction; emphasise clear strategy, function-specific tools and trusted content; and describe both workforce implications and competitive risks. The host adds a broader case for AI as an individual assistant, creative thought partner and talent-retention tool, while warning that vendor surveys should be triangulated rather than accepted in isolation.
