Corpus framing of this source: XpertBench: expert-derived professional tasks with calibrated rubric-based evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source introduces XpertBench, a 1,346-task benchmark drawn from practitioner workflows across seven domains, and ShotJudge, a one-shot expert-calibrated LLM judging method. It argues that authentic professional tasks, granular expert rubrics and hierarchical quality control produce a more professionally meaningful evaluation than knowledge-retrieval benchmarks or uncalibrated LLM judges. On a 245-task gold subset, it reports stronger human-expert alignment for ShotJudge and differentiated performance among frontier models, while qualifying domain-level findings where sample sizes are limited.

Corpus source-specific finding

The source argues that professional LLM evaluation should move beyond short-answer metrics and knowledge retrieval toward authentic, open-ended practitioner work assessed through granular rubrics.
