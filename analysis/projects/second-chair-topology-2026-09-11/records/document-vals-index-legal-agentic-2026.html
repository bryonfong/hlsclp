Open topic: Lease reasoning: Payment risk survives supplier failure

Corpus framing of this source: A legal-research benchmark sample on Virginia finance-lease obligations

Corpus synthesis of this source: Vals Index — Continuous Independent Agentic Benchmark (Legal Sector: Legal Research Bench + Independent HLAB Runs) presents a public equipment-leasing task and agent-generated legal analysis, rather than an empirical finding about legal-AI performance. The displayed response concludes that the corporate lessee must continue paying despite defective trailers and the manufacturer's bankruptcy, while pursuing supplier-side remedies. Its reasoning combines Virginia statutes, contractual terms and cited legal authority; these are the displayed response's conclusions, not separately established findings by the benchmark publisher.

Corpus source-specific finding

The publisher presents one representative task, not multiple independent legal views.

Exact returned source excerpt

Below are the agents’ full answers to a single representative Business & Commercial question, one of the benchmark’s public sample tasks: a Virginia equipment-leasing dispute that turns on UCC Article 2A finance-lease rules.

Exact returned source excerpt

Switch tabs to compare how each model framed its analysis; models are ordered by overall benchmark accuracy.

Corpus source-specific finding

The response classifies the transaction as a non-consumer finance lease.

Exact returned source excerpt

A "finance lease" requires three things, all satisfied here: - **(i) The lessor did not "select, manufacture, or supply the goods."** Client picked the model and negotiated the refrigeration specs directly with Manufacturer; Lessor merely bought the units.

Exact returned source excerpt

✔ - **(ii) "The lessor acquires the goods… in connection with the lease."** Lessor purchased the Trailers from Manufacturer for $85,000 each specifically to lease them to Client.

Corpus source-specific finding

Acceptance makes rent independent of equipment defects under both statute and contract.

Exact returned source excerpt

Client accepted the Trailers — it took delivery and operated them for roughly four months before the compressor failures (acceptance under §§ 8.2A‑515/‑516).

Exact returned source excerpt

Client cannot stop paying — the statutory and contractual "hell or high water" rule **Statutory rule — § 8.2A‑407.** "In the case of a finance lease that is not a consumer lease the lessee's promises under the lease contract become **irrevocable and independent upon the lessee's acceptance of the goods**" (subsection (1)).

Corpus source-specific finding

The response places warranty liability and manufacturer-insolvency risk on the supplier side, not the financier.

Exact returned source excerpt

Virginia law **expressly excludes finance leases from the implied warranties** of merchantability (§ 8.2A‑212) and fitness (§ 8.2A‑213), the Lessor made **no express warranty** (§ 8.2A‑210; the Lease contains none), and the Lease channels equipment claims to Manufacturer.

Exact returned source excerpt

2A‑209(1), "the benefit of a supplier's promises… and of all warranties, whether express or implied, under the supply contract, extends to the lessee to the extent of the lessee's leasehold interest under a finance lease… but is subject to the terms of the warranty and of the supply contract and all defenses or claims arising therefrom." Manufacturer's two‑year warranty on the mechanical and refrigeration components therefore runs through to Client, and Client retains any independent rights it has against Manufacturer (§ 8.2A‑209(4)).

Corpus source-specific finding

The response acknowledges validity defenses but finds none available on the stated facts.

Exact returned source excerpt

No applicable escape hatch on these facts The hell‑or‑high‑water rule does **not** bar defenses going to the **formation or validity of the lease itself** — e.g., fraud in the inducement or unconscionability (*C & J Vantage*, recognizing such carve‑outs; see also Va.

Exact returned source excerpt

But nothing in the facts suggests any wrongdoing by Lessor: Lessor delivered/paid for conforming trailers and **affirmatively gave Client the complete supply contract disclosing Manufacturer's warranty terms before signing.

Corpus source-specific finding

The response recommends continued payment or a negotiated workout alongside bankruptcy and third-party claims.

Exact returned source excerpt

**Consequence of stopping payment.** Ceasing payments puts Client **in default**, exposing it to acceleration, repossession, and a deficiency claim plus the Lessor's other remedies under the Lease and §§ 8.2A‑523 *et seq.* Withholding rent is not a permissible self‑help remedy here.

Exact returned source excerpt

### Practical bottom line Client should **keep paying Lessor** (or negotiate a workout) and direct its defect remedies to **Manufacturer's bankruptcy estate** and any other responsible third parties.

GDP.

Corpus source-specific finding

Legal Research Bench tests multi-step research resembling junior-associate and paralegal work.

Exact returned source excerpt

The benchmark tests whether models can conduct the kind of multi-source research that junior associates and paralegals routinely perform: finding relevant statutes and case law, applying precedent to a fact pattern, and synthesizing across jurisdictions or regulatory regimes.

Exact returned source excerpt

The tasks span eight practice areas: Administrative / Regulatory, Business & Commercial, Civil Litigation, Constitutional / Civil Rights, Criminal, Family, Health, and Immigration.

Corpus source-specific finding

Vals uses held-out research questions while opening only a small sample and the harness.

Exact returned source excerpt

### Dataset The dataset comprises **413 expert-authored, peer-reviewed questions**, each paired with a gold-standard answer, authoritative legal sources, and a detailed grading rubric.

Exact returned source excerpt

It is divided into three parts: Public (5 open-source samples), Private Validation (200 samples available for license), and Test (208 samples).

Corpus source-specific finding

Strict scoring exposes omissions that partial-credit results conceal.

Exact returned source excerpt

Experts include only items that a correct answer must** contain; each rubric item is a required element, not an optional one.

Exact returned source excerpt

Our primary metric is **all-pass**: a question counts as correct only if the response satisfies every* rubric item: 100% if all checks pass, 0% otherwise.

Corpus source-specific finding

Research scores depend on an LLM judge, which Vals validates against expert agreement.

Exact returned source excerpt

- courtlistener_search: searches the CourtListener database for case law, statutes, and legal documents - web_search: general web search for secondary sources, regulatory text, and commentary - retrieve_information: queries over previously fetched documents to extract specific passages - parse_html_page: downloads and parses an HTML page from a URL - submit_final_result: submits the agent’s final answer for evaluation ### Grading All responses are graded by GPT 5.4 as a judge against each task’s rubric (see [Scoring](#scoring) for the all-pass metric and rubric structure).

Exact returned source excerpt

We selected this judge because its grades align more closely with a majority vote of 3 human expert reviewers than any single human reviewer does with that majority: GPT 5.4 agrees with the majority vote 87.4% of the time, compared to an average human baseline agreement of 83.4%.

Corpus source-specific finding

Conflicting legal authority creates a consistent performance weakness.

Exact returned source excerpt

- Reconciling conflicting authority is the most reliable failure mode: every model scores lower on questions that require synthesizing across jurisdictions, courts, or regimes than on those that don’t, a drop of 6 to 17 points per model and 11 points when pooled.

Exact returned source excerpt

Health and Administrative / Regulatory score highest on average; Family and Immigration are by far the hardest.

Exact returned source excerpt

Among the top three, Opus 5 is both the most accurate and the least expensive, while Fable 5 is the fastest.
