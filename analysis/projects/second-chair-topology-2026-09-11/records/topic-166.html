Native topic findings

  1. The cluster centres on who legal research represents and how participant composition limits broader claims.

  3. AI survey and pilot populations differ in ways that matter for interpreting their reach.
  4. Career data show distinct racial and ethnic employment patterns, but changing respondents complicate longitudinal interpretation.
