Licensing and insurance under uncertainty: Protective institutions remain incomplete and can exclude

Corpus synthesis: Licensing and medical insurance respond to information gaps that ordinary market exchange cannot fully resolve. Licensing may provide quality signals while restricting competition and substitutes; insurance contracts remain limited by adverse selection, moral hazard, trust and oversight, without making information gaps the proven cause of every coverage failure. The exemplars favour comparing imperfect institutional arrangements over assuming that deregulation or more complete contracting offers a simple remedy.
