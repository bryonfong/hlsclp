Corpus framing of this source: M-25-22: Performance-based, accountable federal AI procurement

Corpus synthesis of this source: M-25-22 sets requirements and recommendations across the federal AI acquisition lifecycle. It treats efficiency gains as an opportunity contingent on fit-for-purpose systems, competition, testing and risk management—not as an empirically demonstrated result. Its contribution is an operational procurement framework grounded in existing federal authorities and M-25-21, rather than a study of AI performance.

Corpus framing of this source: OMB M-25-22: Competitive, performance-based federal AI procurement with lifecycle safeguards

Corpus synthesis of this source: OMB’s April 3, 2025 memorandum sets requirements and recommendations for acquiring trustworthy AI quickly and cost-effectively. It replaces M-24-18 and connects procurement to M-25-21 risk management. Its contribution is a policy framework, not empirical evidence of AI’s benefits: OMB argues that competition, cross-functional expertise, measurable outcomes and continuing oversight can improve government efficiency while protecting public trust.

Corpus framing of this source: M-25-22: Federal AI Acquisition Across the Contract Lifecycle

Corpus synthesis of this source: OMB presents a prescriptive, lifecycle-wide framework for acquiring AI systems and services rather than an empirical evaluation. It argues that AI can improve government efficiency, but agencies must define clear requirements, test fitness for purpose, monitor performance and cost, manage rights and risks, and preserve competition and public trust. The memorandum grounds these measures in existing federal law and policy, qualifies them by agency and use-case coverage, and assigns implementation actions to agencies, GSA, OMB and the Chief AI Officer Council.
