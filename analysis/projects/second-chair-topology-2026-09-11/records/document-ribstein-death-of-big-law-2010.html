Corpus framing of this source: The Death of Big Law: structural fragility and alternatives to associate-driven growth

Corpus synthesis of this source: Larry E. Ribstein’s 2010 article argues that Big Law’s difficulties expose a fragile business model, not merely a recession. Drawing on economic theory, reported employment data, lateral-hiring research and firm-failure accounts, he links institutional decline to weakened reputational incentives and predicts smaller firms or businesses built around firm-owned legal products. His argument concerns large law firms’ economic viability, not the disappearance of lawyers or legal demand.
