Corpus framing of this source: Provision-extraction results favour tailored RAG configurations and follow-up prompting

Corpus synthesis of this source: The RAG Report: Large Language Models in Legal Due Diligence compares in-context and RAG configurations with two third-party extraction and review tools. Its experiments show that model choice, prompting and provision type materially affect extraction performance. The authors distinguish observed tool failures from their tentative explanation that non-adjustable system settings caused some failures.
