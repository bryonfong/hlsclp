Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: CalibratedRubric: task-adaptive, probabilistic rubric-bank construction for open-ended LLM evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Chen and co-authors propose CalibratedRubric, a framework combining task-specific scoring, posterior measurability filtering and IRT-based information-aware rubric selection. They present theoretical arguments against strict unanimity and binary variance filters, then test the components across financial, healthcare, general and legal benchmarks. Their evidence supports more compact, uncertainty-aware evaluation and conditional agreement gains, while they limit stronger claims about multidimensional validity, judge bias, graded scoring and safety-sensitive objectives.
