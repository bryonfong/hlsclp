Corpus framing of this source: Making firm knowledge discoverable and executable by AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ryan McClead recommends structuring firm knowledge for AI as well as human use. He argues that AI can automatically invoke firm-designed skills and execute workflows without users knowing those skills or steps, while preserving users’ ability to override them. He illustrates the proposed approach with contract review rather than presenting measured implementation results.

Corpus framing of this source: Ryan McClead on AI-assisted legal work and executable knowledge

Corpus synthesis of this source: McClead argues that AI can sharply accelerate expert work but still requires knowledgeable human judgment, bounded workspaces and purpose-built deterministic tools where exactness matters. Drawing on his experience writing a book with Claude, he describes a shift from traditional knowledge management and manual prompting toward AI-accessible, executable workflows, while qualifying these benefits because generative systems remain probabilistic.
