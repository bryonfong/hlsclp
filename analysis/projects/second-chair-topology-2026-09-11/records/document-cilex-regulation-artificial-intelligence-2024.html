Corpus framing of this source: CILEx Regulation’s approach to AI-assisted legal work

Corpus synthesis of this source: CILEx Regulation (CRL) supports AI adoption under continued human judgement, lawyer accountability and proportionate oversight. It expects near-term regulation to retain existing responsibility structures while CRL coordinates with other regulators, updates its regulatory framework, addresses education and training risks, and explores AI for its own operations.
