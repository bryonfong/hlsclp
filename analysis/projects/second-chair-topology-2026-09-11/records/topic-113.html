Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Employer forecasts of AI-driven job redistribution and changing skills, 2025–2030

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Future of Jobs Report 2025 synthesises employer expectations of technological and wider economic change. It projects simultaneous job creation and displacement, rising demand for technical and human-centred skills, and extensive workforce retraining. Its economy, region and industry profiles show substantial variation, including contrasting forecasts for lawyers. These are survey-based forecasts and employer plans, not evidence of realised AI displacement.

Corpus source-specific finding

The report bases its outlook on a large employer survey, not observed future employment outcomes.
