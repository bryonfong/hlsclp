Corpus framing of this source: Professions as institutional agents and changing models of knowledge work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Scott proposes an institutional account of professions: professionals create and apply frameworks that govern social life, while the category of profession itself changes with occupational structures and scholarly assumptions. He develops this theoretical contribution through a review of competing sociological models, historical examples and secondary workforce estimates.

Corpus framing of this source: Professional authority and its transformation through knowledge, organizations and markets

Corpus synthesis of this source: In Lords of the Dance: Professionals as Institutional Agents, Scott offers a historical and theoretical synthesis of how professions construct authority and how technological, organizational and market changes reshape their work.
