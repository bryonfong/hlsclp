Corpus framing of this source: Legal Aid of North Carolina’s tiered, AI-assisted modernised-intake model

Corpus synthesis of this source: Legal Aid of North Carolina and its technology partners present a human-centred, phased model for expanding access under severe intake-capacity constraints. The model combines an online portal, the Leah AI assistant, automated intake and system integration, continued human support, case-management roles, and a vetted referral network. They offer early operational evidence from disaster response but emphasise that the wider system remains under development and that its effect on case volumes has not yet been evaluated.
