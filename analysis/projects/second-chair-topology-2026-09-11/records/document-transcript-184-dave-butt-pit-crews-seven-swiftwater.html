Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Swiftwater on business-aligned legal, risk and investigation operations

Corpus synthesis of this source: Mirat Dave and Danish Butt argue that legal, risk, compliance and investigation teams should operate as service functions and business enablers rather than reactive control functions. They recommend aligning performance measures and investment cases with enterprise value, standardising processes before applying technology, using investigation data preventively, and adopting a deliberate long-term AI strategy. They predict that AI-enabled investigation platforms will move from recording completed work to facilitating the work itself, while qualifying that vague mandates, poor data readiness and uncoordinated tool adoption may limit AI’s impact.
