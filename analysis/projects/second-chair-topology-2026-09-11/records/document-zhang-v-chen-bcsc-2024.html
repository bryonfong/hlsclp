Corpus framing of this source: Zhang v. Chen: Costs, substantial success and counsel’s use of ChatGPT

Corpus synthesis of this source: The decision applies a qualitative substantial-success test to a parenting application, distinguishes the application’s central issue from secondary relief, and explains why the circumstances did not warrant special costs against counsel. It also records professional guidance requiring lawyers to verify AI-generated materials and retain responsibility for their accuracy.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court frames costs as discretionary and assesses substantial success qualitatively across the disputed matters, using roughly 75% success as a guide while weighting issues by significance.
