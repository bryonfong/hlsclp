Corpus framing of this source: Regional and linguistic barriers to AI adoption in journalism

Corpus synthesis of this source: Generating Change links unequal AI adoption to Western-focused investment, language barriers and uneven local resources. Respondents illustrate these constraints through newsroom experiences; the report expresses cautious hope, rather than a demonstrated finding, that generative AI could narrow regional disparities.

Corpus source-specific finding

Corpus framing of this source: Newsroom AI adoption: efficiency gains, uneven capacity and contested consequences

Corpus synthesis of this source: Generating Change reports newsroom experiences and expectations from April–July 2023. It finds widespread experimentation but uneven organisational impact, with opportunities to redirect journalistic effort alongside risks to accuracy, employment, independence and smaller newsrooms’ viability. The report advocates cautious, journalism-led adoption; its non-representative survey records practitioner perceptions rather than establishing industry-wide effects.

Corpus source-specific finding

The evidence captures practitioners’ experiences, not a representative global estimate.

Exact returned source excerpt

The surveys and interviews were conducted between April and July, 2023.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey was supplemented with interviews, and conversations at journalism conferences.
