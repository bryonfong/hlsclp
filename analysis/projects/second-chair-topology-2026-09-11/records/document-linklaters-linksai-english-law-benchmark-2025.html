Corpus framing of this source: The LinksAI English law benchmark (Version 2): limits, context and future testing

Corpus synthesis of this source: The benchmark argues that rapid improvements in general-purpose LLMs have not made them dependable sources of legal advice on critical-reasoning tasks. It distinguishes benchmark-style legal questions from the less structured objectives clients typically bring to lawyers, compares some benchmark retrieval with ordinary search, preserves a role for human legal-service delivery, and commits to retesting significant future model iterations. It reports, without adopting as its own conclusion, Elon Musk’s prediction that AI may eventually eliminate the need for jobs.

Corpus framing of this source: The LinksAI English law benchmark (Version 2)

Corpus synthesis of this source: A February 2025 benchmark of six general-purpose LLMs on 50 English-law questions. Senior lawyers assessed substantive accuracy, citations and clarity. The report finds substantial improvement in Gemini 2.0 and OpenAI o1 over earlier models, but concludes that none is reliable for unsupervised English-law advice, especially where critical reasoning, factual application or clause interpretation is required.
