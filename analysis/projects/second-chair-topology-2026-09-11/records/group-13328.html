Task-level automation: Economic adjustment limits job loss while redistributing work and rewards

Corpus synthesis: Automating particular tasks need not eliminate whole occupations because productivity, demand growth, work reallocation and the creation of new tasks can sustain employment. The resulting adjustment may still redistribute work, wages and expertise premiums across workers and firms, potentially widening inequality. Adoption constraints, verification requirements and difficult residual tasks also reduce projected productivity gains when tools are scaled.
