Corpus synthesis of this source: Meredith Kildow describes legal-sector AI adoption as client-driven, increasingly directed from corporate leadership and concentrated in document review, investigations, data reduction and contracts. She argues that clients face a fragmented vendor landscape while simultaneously being pressed to demonstrate efficiency, return on investment and cost reduction. She recommends aligned delivery teams and hands-on experimentation to identify workable, matter-specific applications. The supplied material also contains separate host framing about “innovation theater” and job-displacement fears, plus an embedded Percipient promotion; these are not treated as Kildow’s positions.
