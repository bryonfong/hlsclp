Native topic findings

  1. Minimum-wage protection depends on adjustment procedures, not simply the size of nominal increases.
  2. Adjustment frequency and implementation delays create separate risks to purchasing power.
  3. Automatic indexation is not a uniform mechanism, and an unfixed schedule need not mean absent protection.
  4. Wage-setting authority varies, but the supplied material does not establish a dispute or rank governance models.
  5. Jurisdictional coverage and statistical construction limit direct cross-country comparisons.
