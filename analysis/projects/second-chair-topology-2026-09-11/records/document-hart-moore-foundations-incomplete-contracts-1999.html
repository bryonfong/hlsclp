Corpus framing of this source: Foundations of Incomplete Contracts: renegotiation, describability and contractual incompleteness

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source develops a mechanism-design foundation for incomplete contracts. It identifies inability to commit against renegotiation as the central constraint, then qualifies the apparent irrelevance of ex ante describability by constructing settings where describability determines whether parties can attain the first-best or do no better than a null contract. It also distinguishes obligational gaps from incompleteness caused by unverifiable or costly-to-describe contingencies, examines institutional obstacles to preventing renegotiation, and proposes further work connecting nonspecifiable asset uses to ownership rights.
