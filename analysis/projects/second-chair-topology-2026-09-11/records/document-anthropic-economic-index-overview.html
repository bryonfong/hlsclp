Corpus synthesis of this source: The Anthropic Economic Index describes observed Claude.ai usage, distinguishing task automation from collaboration and grouping conversations by occupational tasks, location and use context. Its dashboard provides descriptive usage evidence, with explicit limits on interpreting task categories as users’ jobs.
