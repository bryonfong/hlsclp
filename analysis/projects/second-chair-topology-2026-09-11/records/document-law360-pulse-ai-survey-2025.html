Corpus framing of this source: Law360 Pulse 2025 AI Survey: Adoption, Benefits and Risks in U.S. Law Firms

Corpus synthesis of this source: Law360 Pulse reports a descriptive survey of 390 U.S. private-practice lawyers conducted in late 2024 and early 2025. It finds sharply expanded generative-AI adoption and generally positive or neutral sentiment, especially among users, while documenting substantial concerns about reliability, ethics, confidentiality, malpractice and junior-lawyer development. It also identifies differences by usage, seniority, gender and firm size, and cautiously predicts consolidation in a fragmented legal-AI market. The supplied material makes no express recommendation.
