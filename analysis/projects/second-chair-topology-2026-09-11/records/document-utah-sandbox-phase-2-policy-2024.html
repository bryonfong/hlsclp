Corpus framing of this source: Utah Sandbox Phase 2 prioritises underserved Utah consumers and targeted oversight

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Utah Supreme Court and Office of Legal Services Innovation explain why Phase 2 narrows the regulatory Sandbox to service models with greater potential to meet unmet legal needs in Utah. They cite resource pressures, apparent misuse of authorisations and data-collection difficulties, while proposing transition protections and more focused oversight. The materials describe regulatory experience and expected benefits, not demonstrated reductions in the justice gap.

Open topic: Regulatory sandboxes: Conditional entry tests consumer risk

Corpus framing of this source: Utah narrows its legal regulatory Sandbox to innovation serving underserved Utahns

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Utah Supreme Court’s September 2024 letter and the Innovation Office’s Phase 2 page present a targeted revision of the Sandbox, not its abandonment. The Court ties participation to meaningful access-to-justice benefits in Utah, explains regulatory and data-collection problems behind the change, and sets exit rules and limited transitional accommodations. It predicts a much smaller Sandbox focused on more innovative service models.

Corpus source-specific finding

The Court retains the Sandbox as an experiment in expanding access without increasing consumer harm.

Corpus framing of this source: Utah Legal Regulatory Sandbox Phase 2: narrowing participation around innovation and access to justice

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Utah Supreme Court explains why it intends to reduce the Sandbox’s population of low-innovation, ABS-only entities and concentrate oversight on service models with stronger potential to expand access to legal services. It identifies resource, authorization, market-presence and data problems; proposes limited protections for affected lawyers and provisional status for qualifying unlaunched models; and plans to redesign complaints and data processes after narrowing the Sandbox.
