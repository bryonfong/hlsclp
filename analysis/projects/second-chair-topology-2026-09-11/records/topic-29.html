The Role of Deliberate Practice in the Acquisition of Expert Performance

Academia & research · Foundations (pre-2015)

Corpus framing of this source: Deliberate practice, accumulated expertise and the limits of sustained effort

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Role of Deliberate Practice in the Acquisition of Expert Performance links musicians’ expertise to accumulated, improvement-oriented practice rather than total musical activity alone. Ericsson, Krampe and Tesch-Römer combine diaries, retrospective histories and piano performance tests with a broader literature review. They distinguish their predictive findings from unresolved causal explanations and argue that effective practice requires recovery, motivation and access to training resources.
