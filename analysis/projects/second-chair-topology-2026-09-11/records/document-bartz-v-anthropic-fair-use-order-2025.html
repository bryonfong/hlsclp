Corpus framing of this source: Bartz v. Anthropic: separate fair-use rulings for training, digitisation and pirated libraries

Corpus synthesis of this source: The June 23, 2025 order treats LLM training, replacement digitisation of purchased books and acquisition of pirated library copies as distinct uses. It finds the training and replacement digitisation fair, but rejects a training-based justification for retaining a general-purpose pirated library. Its reasoning distinguishes protected expression and markets from learning, format changes and competition that copyright does not reserve to authors.

Corpus framing of this source: Bartz v. Anthropic: separate fair-use treatment for LLM training, format conversion and pirated libraries

Corpus synthesis of this source: The order treats Anthropic’s conduct as several objectively distinct uses rather than one end-to-end AI-training project. It grants summary judgment that training specific LLMs and replacing purchased print books with one-for-one digital copies were fair uses, but holds that building and retaining a general-purpose library from pirated books was not. It limits the ruling to the developed record and leaves other downstream copying unresolved.
