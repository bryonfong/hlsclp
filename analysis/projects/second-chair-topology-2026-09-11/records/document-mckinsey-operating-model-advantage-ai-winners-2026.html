Corpus framing of this source: The Operating Model Advantage: Why AI Winners Are Rewiring Their Organizations

Corpus synthesis of this source: McKinsey argues that widespread AI deployment has produced limited enterprise-wide financial impact because most companies add tools to existing workflows and hierarchies rather than redesigning their operating models. It presents organizational rewiring—especially workflow, governance, talent, data and decision redesign—as the basis for sustained AI advantage, supports the argument with surveys, economic indicators, historical analogy and company examples, and recommends a staged transformation led by CEOs.

Corpus source-specific finding

Widespread deployment has not translated into broad financial impact because companies typically bolt AI onto unchanged organizations.

# The Operating Model Advantage: Why AI Winners Are Rewiring Their Organizations *Christoph Schmitz, Dago Diedrich, Gianmarco Cilento, Steffen Fuchs — McKinsey & Company (2026)* The AI revolution** has arrived, generating high activity but—to date—minimal financial benefits.

Exact returned source excerpt

Virtually all major companies have deployed chatbots, analytics dashboards, and AI-enabled tools across niche parts of their business, yet few have achieved meaningful enterprise-wide impact.

Corpus framing of this source: Organizational redesign as the basis for durable AI advantage

Corpus synthesis of this source: Christoph Schmitz and colleagues at McKinsey argue that AI creates enterprise value when companies redesign coordination, workflows, governance and talent—not merely install tools. Their 2026 article combines survey associations, reported economic indicators, historical analogy and company examples with recommendations for business-led transformation. It distinguishes documented results from projected gains and treats organizational capabilities as a means to build competitive advantages, not as value in themselves.

Corpus source-specific finding

Broad AI access shifts the proposed source of advantage from tools to organizational design.

Exact returned source excerpt
