Corpus framing of this source: AI Is Killing The Billable Hour. The Real Question Is What Comes Next.

Corpus synthesis of this source: Stephanie Corey and Ken Callander argue that AI is accelerating a broader structural shift away from hourly billing toward well-scoped, non-hourly value-based pricing. They explain hourly billing as a historical mechanism for transferring uncertainty to clients, identify operational requirements and benefits of fixed fees, and recommend granular scoping, competitive RFPs and value-oriented metrics. They draw on conversations with in-house leaders, reported experience from value-based engagements and Rita McGrath’s disruption framework, while qualifying AI as an accelerator rather than the underlying cause.
