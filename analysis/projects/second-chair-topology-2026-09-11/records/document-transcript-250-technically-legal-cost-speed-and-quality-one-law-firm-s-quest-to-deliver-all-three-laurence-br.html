Corpus framing of this source: Laurence Brown on workflow redesign, AI risk and professional responsibility

Corpus synthesis of this source: Brown argues that legal AI creates its greatest value through workflow redesign rather than as a simple chat tool embedded in existing software. He accepts confidentiality, governance and billing risks, but contends that regulation and a conservative patent profession can overemphasise caution. His own position is that firms should control genuine risks while considering whether failure to use AI may itself disadvantage clients.
