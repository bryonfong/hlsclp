Corpus framing of this source: Wolters Kluwer’s Suzanne Konstance on Trust, Compliance, and the Next Phase of Legal AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Konstance argues that legal AI creates value only when it rests on authoritative, current, contextualized content and remains subject to expert scrutiny. She presents Wolters Kluwer’s editorial processes, customer co-design, internal AI investment and selective content licensing—including its Harvey relationship—as ways to improve accuracy and practical usefulness. She predicts that agentic systems will shift professionals from individual execution toward active supervision, increasing rather than reducing the need for legal proficiency, verification and accelerated training.
