Corpus framing of this source: General Catalyst’s AI-enabled services investment thesis

Corpus synthesis of this source: Marc Bhargava presents an investment strategy built around partially automating service workflows while retaining human professionals for complex or sensitive work. He argues that this model can increase capacity, reduce outsourcing and materially improve service-business margins. Nathan Labenz separately frames services as a much larger market than software and raises the possibility that AI will also generate substantial consumer surplus.
