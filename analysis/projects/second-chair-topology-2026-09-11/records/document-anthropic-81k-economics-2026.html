Corpus framing of this source: Claude users report productivity gains alongside displacement fears

Corpus synthesis of this source: In “What 81,000 people told us about the economics of AI” (April 22, 2026), Anthropic researchers Maxim Massenkoff and Saffron Huang connect users’ reported experiences with observed Claude usage. The findings concern self-reports and correlations, not demonstrated displacement effects.
