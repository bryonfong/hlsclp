Corpus framing of this source: BearisterAI’s pre-demo claims for public-defender evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: BearisterAI’s 2026 written response presents BearisterAI 1.0 as a privacy-focused legal AI product with citation-constrained outputs, non-LLM case-law search, and isolated handling of malformed files. The submission consists principally of vendor assertions rather than attached technical evidence or test methodology. It qualifies reproducibility by changes in law, describes SOC 2 Type II certification as work in progress, and identifies ongoing recall research. The programme separately disclaims endorsement and frames the exercise as education and evaluation.
