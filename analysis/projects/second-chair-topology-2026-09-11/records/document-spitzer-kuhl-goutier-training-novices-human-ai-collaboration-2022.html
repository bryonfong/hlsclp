Corpus framing of this source: AI-mediated transfer of expert knowledge to train novices

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper develops a preliminary conceptual framework for using human–AI collaboration (HAIC) to preserve task-specific expert knowledge and train novices when subject-matter experts are unavailable or costly to use as trainers. It frames this as a research agenda rather than reporting completed experimental findings, grounding its proposal in literature on organizational knowledge, tacit knowledge, explainable AI and complementary human–AI performance.
