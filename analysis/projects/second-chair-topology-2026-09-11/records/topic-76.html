Answering the Call of Automation: How the Labor Market Adjusted to Mechanizing Telephone Operation

Academia & research · Generative shock (2023-2024)

Corpus framing of this source: Telephone automation: lasting losses for incumbent operators, occupational adjustment for later cohorts

Corpus synthesis of this source: Answering the Call of Automation: How the Labor Market Adjusted to Mechanizing Telephone Operation distinguishes the costs borne by displaced telephone operators from the outcomes of subsequent cohorts. Using historical census, company and newspaper data, the authors find sharp operator-job losses but no detectable decline in later cohorts’ employment. They document age-specific occupational adjustment and test competing explanations, while acknowledging identification and statistical-power limits.
