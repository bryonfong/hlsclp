Corpus framing of this source: Testing disruption theory rather than treating it as a universal prescription

Corpus synthesis of this source: Kirk Kardashian’s 2015 Tuck article reports Andrew King’s empirical critique of Clayton Christensen’s disruptive-innovation theory. It contrasts the theory’s managerial influence with limited testing, highlights weak case conformity in an expert survey, and presents King and Baljir Baartartogtokh’s recommendation to retain useful elements alongside classical strategic analysis.
