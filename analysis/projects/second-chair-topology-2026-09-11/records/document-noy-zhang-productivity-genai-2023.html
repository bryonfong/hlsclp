Corpus framing of this source: Experimental Evidence on the Productivity Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence

Corpus synthesis of this source: Noy and Zhang use a preregistered randomized experiment and a two-week follow-up to estimate ChatGPT’s effects on mid-level professional writing. They report faster, higher-quality output, compressed performance inequality, major changes in task structure, and evidence that ChatGPT substitutes for effort more than it complements human skill.
