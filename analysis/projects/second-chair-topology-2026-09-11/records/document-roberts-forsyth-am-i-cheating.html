Corpus framing of this source: AI-assisted writing: preserving learning and authorship beyond the cheating question

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anthea Roberts and Miranda Forsyth (2025) frame AI-assisted writing as a challenge to learning, authenticity and creative control rather than simply a question of cheating. They use reflective questions and historical analogies, not empirical findings, to explore risks and opportunities, and recommend human-directed collaboration with AI.
