Cross-domain institutional evidence: Innovation and market correction remain conditional on incentives, gatekeeping and measurement

Corpus synthesis: These exemplars use historical, theoretical and cross-sector evidence to challenge claims that openness, observability, professional regulation, stronger platforms or better AI performance will automatically improve markets or displace incumbents. Outcomes instead depend on incentives, governance, network position, uncertainty and the assumptions used to measure effects; institutions that enable innovation or protection may also preserve exclusion and concentrated control. The materials vary in whether the principal constraint is institutional design, causal evidence or the unavoidable incompleteness of competing market and regulatory arrangements.

This is a declared corpus family. Membership describes the corpus hierarchy; it does not imply causal relationships or relative importance. Source counts below are deduplicated within this group and may overlap with other groups.
