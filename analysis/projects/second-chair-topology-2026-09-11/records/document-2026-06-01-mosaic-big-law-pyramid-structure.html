Corpus framing of this source: Big Law’s shift toward smaller junior cohorts and lateral hiring

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Big Law Pyramid Is Turning Into a Cylinder argues that AI capabilities and profit incentives are pushing firms away from large entry-level cohorts toward experienced lateral hires. The author distinguishes growing megafirms from firms reducing recruitment, predicts unequal effects on graduates, and expects hiring to remain top-heavy until firms redesign training.

The author describes a shift from a staffing pyramid toward a cylinder, with important exceptions.
