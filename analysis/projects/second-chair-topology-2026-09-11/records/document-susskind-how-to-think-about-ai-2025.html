Corpus synthesis of this source: The reviewer presents Richard Susskind’s book as a conceptual guide for lawyers confronting AI. The review endorses Susskind’s challenge to legal exceptionalism, his vocabulary for distinguishing forms of technological change, and his structured treatment of risk.
