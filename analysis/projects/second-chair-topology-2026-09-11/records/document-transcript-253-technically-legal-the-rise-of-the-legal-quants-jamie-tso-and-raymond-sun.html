Corpus framing of this source: The Rise of the Legal Quants (Jamie Tso & Raymond Sun): Liquid Legal Markets and Human-Centred AI Adoption

Corpus synthesis of this source: Tso and Sun predict that AI will make specialised legal services more liquid, productised and directly accessible through client-facing tools and agent-callable interfaces. They argue that practical value depends less on tool availability than on people who combine technical understanding, first-principles reasoning and real-world application. They present LegalQuants as a way to support and reward this scarce talent, while the interviewer qualifies the vision by observing that such technically capable “unicorns” remain a small subset of the legal profession. The discussion leaves open whether legal services will converge on one neutral marketplace or remain fragmented across provider-controlled platforms.
