Native topic findings

  1. Courts are converging on controlled, assistive AI use rather than blanket prohibition or autonomous adjudication.
  2. Judicial integrity, rights and public confidence provide the governing standard.
  3. Human responsibility, direct engagement and verification are the main safeguards, but their limits remain contested.
  4. Concrete harms support caution, while claimed benefits remain mostly prospective or unquantified.
  5. Operational governance extends beyond output accuracy to security, privacy, training data, staff supervision and scope.
  6. Governance must remain adaptive and locally tailored because technologies, uses and impacts differ.
