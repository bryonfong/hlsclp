Corpus framing of this source: The Cues That Matter: Screening for Quality Signals in the Ex Ante Phase of Buying Professional Services

Corpus synthesis of this source: Pemer and Skjølsvik use screening theory and interviews with experienced organizational buyers to explain how clients assess professional-service quality before an assignment. They develop a hierarchical taxonomy of resource, delivery and relational quality; distinguish shortlist-qualifying signals from interaction-based signals of excellence; qualify conventional claims about reputation and trust; and propose practical and research uses for the framework.
