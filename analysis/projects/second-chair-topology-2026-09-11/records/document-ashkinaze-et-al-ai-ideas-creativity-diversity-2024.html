Corpus framing of this source: Passive AI exposure diversifies collective ideas without raising individual originality

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ashkinaze and co-authors contribute a large-scale, dynamic experiment that separates exposure to AI-generated ideas from disclosure of their source. They find that high exposure increases collective diversity and slows convergence over time, but does not improve individual originality. AI-source labels affect adoption differently across self-perceived creativity groups, while the task-difficulty result remains speculative. The authors frame off-the-shelf LLMs as potential idea banks that may interrupt group convergence, subject to substantial measurement, sampling and design limitations.
