Open topic: Employment screening: Audits reveal bias without remedying it

Corpus framing of this source: New York City mandates audits and disclosure for automated employment screening

Corpus synthesis of this source: New York City Local Law 144 of 2021 establishes requirements for automated tools used in hiring and promotion: independent bias audits, public audit summaries, advance notice and data transparency. The supplied source is statutory text, not an empirical assessment or prediction of these measures’ effectiveness.

Corpus source-specific finding

The law covers tools that substantially assist or replace discretionary employment decisions.

Exact returned source excerpt

The term “automated employment decision tool” means any computational process, derived from machine learning, statistical modeling, data analytics, or artificial intelligence, that issues simplified output, including a score, classification, or recommendation, that is used to substantially assist or replace discretionary decision making for making employment decisions that impact natural persons.

Exact returned source excerpt

The term “employment decision” means to screen candidates for employment or employees for promotion within the city.

Employers and employment agencies must secure a recent independent bias audit and publish its summary before using a covered tool.

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied excerpt establishes a new section of New York City’s administrative code governing automated employment decision tools and defines which computational processes fall within, or outside, that category.

Chapter 5 of title 20 of the administrative code of the city of New York is amended by adding a new subchapter 25 to read as follows: Subchapter 25 Automated Employment Decision Tools § 20-870 Definitions.
