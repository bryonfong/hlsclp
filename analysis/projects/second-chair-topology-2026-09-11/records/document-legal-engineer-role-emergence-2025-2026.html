Corpus framing of this source: Legal AI hiring shortages prompt high salaries and internal talent development

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied material from “The emergence of the 'legal engineer' role (job postings and commentary, 2025-2026)” consists of Roy Strom’s Bloomberg Law column of 2 July 2026; the other named inputs are not captured. Strom uses job advertisements and interviews to describe demand for experienced legal AI leaders, competition for scarce talent, and alternatives based on peer coaching and legal education. Advertised efficiency targets and interviewees’ predictions are not demonstrated outcomes.
