Corpus framing of this source: AI Action Plan for Justice

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Ministry of Justice presents a phased programme for embedding generative and non-generative AI across internal administration, frontline justice services and public-facing support. It combines reported pilot results and operational case studies with planned scaling, while making governance, human oversight, evaluation, workforce engagement, infrastructure, privacy and transparency conditions of adoption.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI should augment staff and redirect capacity toward higher-value, human-centred work.

Exact returned source excerpt

#### 2.1.1 Equip every staff member with a secure AI assistant General-purpose AI chat assistants, such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, and government-built alternatives, can support staff in a wide range of everyday tasks: from drafting emails and summarising documents to managing inboxes, redacting information, and generating reports.

Exact returned source excerpt

Following successful pilots across HR, policy, communications, probation and HMCTS enabling functions, we observed significant time savings and improved user experience for our workforce, particularly for staff with neurodiverse conditions, accessibility needs, or low digital confidence.

Corpus source-specific finding

Semantic search and cited knowledge retrieval can reduce time spent locating operational information.

Exact returned source excerpt

#### 2.1.2 Accelerate insight with AI-powered search and knowledge retrieval Justice system staff often rely on large volumes of unstructured information, including operational procedures, policy documents, case records or legal precedents but traditional search tools struggle to surface what’s most relevant.

Exact returned source excerpt

Staff were spending time experimenting with different keywords to locate case details, leading to frustrating “search flurries.” To solve this, MOJ Data Science introduced semantic search in the Probation Digital System (launched June 2025), powered by a Large Language Model (LLM).

Corpus source-specific finding

Public-facing AI could improve navigation, triage and access to justice, but final adjudication should remain human.

Exact returned source excerpt

We are working with DSIT as part of HMT’s Transformation Fund to test and deploy AI applications in MOJ call centres with the aim of streamlining case handling and enhancing service delivery.

Exact returned source excerpt

Chatbots could also help citizens understand their needs or navigate civil or family law to resolve their dispute without going to court, receive criminal injuries compensation guidance, or understand prison visitor rules.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI can support policy analysis, but officials should retain responsibility for judgment and values.

Exact returned source excerpt

### 2.5 Improve policy and strategic decision making through AI We will explore and pilot AI tools that support smarter, faster and more inclusive decision making across policy and strategy functions.

Exact returned source excerpt

Machine-learning models can help identify patterns in complex evidence bases, including social, economic and environmental data, to flag emerging risks or highlight groups likely to be affected by proposals.

Corpus source-specific finding

Non-generative AI remains central to risk assessment, evidence extraction and data integration.

Exact returned source excerpt

### 2.6 Improve decision making through non-generative AI While generative AI is capturing much of the spotlight, there are many applications where other forms of AI are better suited.

Exact returned source excerpt

For example, actuarial tools maintained by MOJ Data Science routinely support human-led assessments of reoffending risk and rehabilitation programme allocation.

Corpus source-specific finding

Governance, ethics, transparency and security are prerequisites for deployment.

Exact returned source excerpt

### 1.2 Implement robust governance to steer and track AI progress Robust governance is essential to ensure AI solutions are safe, transparent, lawful and aligned with our ethical commitments and strategic priorities.

Exact returned source excerpt

We have established an AI Steering Group that brings together senior leaders from across the Ministry of Justice, including policy, data, digital, security, people, legal, HM Prison & Probation Service (HMPPS), HMCTS, risk, and communications, to oversee AI initiatives and manage risks.

Corpus source-specific finding

Projects should be measured continuously and stopped, changed or scaled according to results.

Exact returned source excerpt

### 2.7 Evaluate success regularly and adjust our approach as we go The success of all AI solutions will be evaluated using clear, quantifiable metrics, ensuring alignment with department goals.

Exact returned source excerpt

For each project, metrics will be defined at the outset and success will be continually assessed with the help of AI Operations, enabling us to stop, pivot, or scale projects as necessary.
