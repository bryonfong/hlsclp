Corpus framing of this source: Hierarchies, Specialization, and the Utilization of Knowledge in Legal Services

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source develops and empirically tests a theory that legal-service hierarchies help exploit increasing returns from acquired knowledge. It links larger business-client markets to greater specialization, more hierarchical offices and higher associate-to-partner leverage, distinguishes these patterns from firm-size effects, and finds weaker or absent relationships for individual-client practices and non-lawyer staffing. It cautiously extends the analysis to other knowledge-intensive work and identifies questions for further research.
