Corpus framing of this source: BetterBench: a lifecycle framework for assessing AI benchmark quality

Corpus synthesis of this source: BetterBench develops and applies a systematic quality-assessment framework for public AI benchmarks. It combines literature and stakeholder interviews, scores 24 foundation-model and non-foundation-model benchmarks across 46 lifecycle criteria, identifies substantial weaknesses in implementation, reproducibility and statistical reporting, and supplies a checklist and living assessment repository. The authors frame the framework as minimum quality assurance rather than a test of construct validity or suitability for a specific use case.

The authors operationalise benchmark quality across the full lifecycle.
