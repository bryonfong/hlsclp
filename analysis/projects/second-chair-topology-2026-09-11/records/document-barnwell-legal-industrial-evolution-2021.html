Corpus framing of this source: Industrialising legal work for exponential demand

Corpus synthesis of this source: Legal Evolution argues that machine-enabled business growth, expanding information and regulation, and broader stakeholder accountability are increasing legal work’s volume, complexity and required speed. Incremental cost control will not close the resulting capacity gap; legal organisations need coordinated, system-level transformation that standardises interfaces, captures knowledge, conserves expert time and builds composable, machine-assisted workflows. The source presents its projections and staffing proposal as directional or tentative rather than precise.

Corpus source-specific finding

Legal demand is entering a secular acceleration that incremental savings cannot absorb.
