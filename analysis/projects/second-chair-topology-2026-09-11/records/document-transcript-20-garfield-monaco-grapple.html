Corpus framing of this source: Alex Monaco on Garfield and consumer legal automation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Drawing on roughly six months of using Garfield and prior experience instructing law firms, Monaco argues that AI can compress client intake and document preparation, outperform traditional firms on responsiveness and cost, and improve access to legal processes. He still allows that human lawyers may currently write better and understand cases more fully, but treats today's human verification as an interim stage and predicts broader automation without humans in the loop.
