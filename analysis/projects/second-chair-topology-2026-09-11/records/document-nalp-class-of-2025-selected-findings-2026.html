Corpus framing of this source: NALP: Strong Class of 2025 outcomes mask contracting hiring and an uncertain structural shift

Corpus synthesis of this source: Jobs & JDs: Employment for the Class of 2025 — Selected Findings combines Danielle A. Taylor’s employment and salary analysis with Nikia L. Gray’s commentary. NALP reports resilient graduate outcomes despite widespread hiring declines, distinguishes employment shares from job counts and salary composition from pay cuts, and treats generative AI as a possible contributor to future structural change rather than an established cause of the observed contraction.
