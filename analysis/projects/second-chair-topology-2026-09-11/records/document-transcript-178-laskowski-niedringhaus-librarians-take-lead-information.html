Builds onWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Law Librarians Take the Lead: The Future of AI and Legal Information

Corpus synthesis of this source: Cas Laskowski, Taryn Marks and Kris Niedringhaus argue that legal information professionals should help shape legal AI through information architecture, curation, critical literacy, training and coordinated infrastructure. They propose a community-led initiative for sharing resources and expertise, stress that AI’s effects depend on intentional design and implementation, and predict that librarians will become trusted partners across the information lifecycle. They qualify that ambition by acknowledging uncertain institutional readiness, the initiative’s evolving form and its dependence on sustained volunteer effort.
