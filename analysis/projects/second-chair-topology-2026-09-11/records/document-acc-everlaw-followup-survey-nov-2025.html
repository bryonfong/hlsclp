Corpus framing of this source: ACC/Everlaw survey: GenAI promises business value, but legal departments lack proof

Corpus synthesis of this source: The ACC/Everlaw release reports strong expectations that GenAI will improve in-house efficiency and demonstrate business value, alongside limited outcome measurement and infrastructure gaps.
