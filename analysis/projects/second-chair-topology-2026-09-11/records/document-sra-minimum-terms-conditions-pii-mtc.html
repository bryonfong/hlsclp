Corpus framing of this source: SRA minimum indemnity terms: mandatory coverage, claimant protections and limited insurer recourse

Corpus synthesis of this source: The SRA Minimum Terms and Conditions of Professional Indemnity Insurance sets regulatory requirements rather than presenting empirical findings or predictions. The supplied extracts establish the scope and minimum value of cover, restrict insurers’ ability to withdraw protection, specify permitted exclusions and reimbursement rights, and govern claims handling and policy continuity. They contain no express assessment of AI-related risks.

Corpus framing of this source: Minimum professional indemnity insurance terms for legal practice

Corpus synthesis of this source: The SRA provisions define core professional indemnity coverage, limit it where international sanctions apply, establish the priority of mandatory special conditions, and permit reimbursement provisions concerning specified misconduct and restricted post-cessation activity.

Corpus source-specific finding

Professional indemnity coverage extends to civil liability and related defence costs arising from actual or alleged breaches of duty in legal work or duties incidental to private legal practice.

Exact returned source excerpt

liability of the insurer to indemnify any insured against civil liability or related defence costs arising from any actual or alleged breach of duty in the performance of (or failure to perform) legal work or failure to discharge or fulfil any duty incidental to the insured firm's practice or to the conduct of private legal practice.

Corpus source-specific finding

Coverage and benefits cease to the extent that providing them would expose the insurer to specified international trade sanctions or restrictions.

Exact returned source excerpt

International trade sanctions The insurer shall be deemed not to provide cover and shall not be liable to pay any claim or provide any benefit under the insurance to the extent that the provision of such cover, payment of such claim or provision of such benefit would expose the insurer to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under United Nations resolutions or the trade or economic sanctions, laws or regulations of the European Union, United Kingdom, Australia or United States of America.

Corpus source-specific finding

Insurers and insured firms may agree general policy conditions, but mandatory special conditions under clause 4 prevail in any conflict.

Exact returned source excerpt

As agreed The insurance may contain such general conditions as are agreed between the insurer and the insured firm, but the insurance must provide that the special conditions required by clause 4 prevail to the extent of any inconsistency.
