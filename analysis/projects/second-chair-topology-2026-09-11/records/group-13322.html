Benchmark validity: Workplace realism strains reproducible scoring

Corpus synthesis: Professional and agent benchmarks must distinguish technical reproducibility from workplace suitability; preference ratings and protocol scores are not equivalent to professional correctness or competence. The exemplars vary in emphasis across contamination control, controlled environments, diagnostic attribution, cost and uncertainty accounting, representation, and separate validation of automated judges and self-correction.
