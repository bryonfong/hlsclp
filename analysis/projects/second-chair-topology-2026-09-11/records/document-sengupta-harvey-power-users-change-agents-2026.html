Corpus framing of this source: Harvey Power Users: The New Generation of Legal Change Agent

Corpus synthesis of this source: Reena SenGupta argues in a 2026 Harvey company blog that proficient Harvey users achieve unusually large productivity and fulfilment gains and can become internal change agents by redesigning workflows and influencing colleagues. She bases the argument chiefly on an RSGI-Harvey study of 40 customers, including 29 law firms, while acknowledging self-defined user categories, absent formal ROI frameworks, unquantified cost savings and variation in whether heavy users champion broader change.

Corpus source-specific finding

A lot of that change has happened in the last five years, and particularly in the last 18 months.
