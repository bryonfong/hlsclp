Corpus framing of this source: Ayinde / Al-Haroun: verification, professional responsibility and sanctions for AI-generated legal material

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Divisional Court anticipates an important role for AI in litigation but requires authoritative verification, effective supervision and compliance with existing professional duties. It draws on the Ayinde proceedings, professional guidance and overseas cases to explain the risks, distinguish misconduct from unresolved questions about AI use, and warn that both practitioners and professional leaders face scrutiny.
