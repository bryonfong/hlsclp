Corpus framing of this source: Gabriela Isturiz on AI competition, professional-services economics and venture access

Corpus synthesis of this source: Isturiz argues that AI lowers the difficulty of building technology while intensifying competition, making customer value, positioning and go-to-market execution more important than technical novelty. She predicts commodity pricing for AI-enabled routine work but premium pricing for qualified expert review, with professional-services margins increasingly depending on agentic workflow and operating-cost reductions. She also calls for faster technology-evaluation practices inside firms and intentional action to address women founders’ limited access to venture funding.
