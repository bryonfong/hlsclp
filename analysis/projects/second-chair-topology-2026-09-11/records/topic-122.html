Native topic record

Hiring audits: Disclosure cannot guarantee nondiscrimination

Corpus synthesis: Explains NYC coverage, demographic reporting and employer responsibility while denying any entitlement to a nonautomated alternative.

This record retains the provider’s full available topic findings and source perspectives. It does not claim a new independent analytical review. Positions refer to this topic’s proposition: “Redirects” is not simple opposition, and “Unclear” is not support.

Declared lineage: Evidence for deployed AI: Proxy measures do not establish real-world effectiveness · Automated hiring audits: Transparency exposes bias without ensuring fair screening

4 distinct stance-bearing works · Supports: 4

Native topic findings

  1. New York City regulates automated employment screening through mandatory audits, disclosure and notice, rather than a guarantee of nondiscrimination.
  2. Coverage depends on the influence of automated outputs, not simply whether a human makes the final decision.
  3. Audits standardise demographic disparity reporting while allowing qualified methodological flexibility.
  4. Vendors may arrange audits, but employers retain responsibility and must assess auditor conflicts.
  5. Transparency and information rights do not create an entitlement to an alternative selection process.
  6. The record documents regulatory refinement, not a contest between independently evidenced positions.
