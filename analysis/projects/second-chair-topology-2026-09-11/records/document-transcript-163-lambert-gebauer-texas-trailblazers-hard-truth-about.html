Corpus framing of this source: Texas Trailblazers and the Hard Truth About AI in Legal Work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Greg Lambert and Marlene Gebauer use conference discussions and practical examples to argue that legal AI’s immediate constraints lie less in raw capability than in workflow design, human verification, pricing incentives, professional skills and organisational readiness. They distinguish their own conclusions from speakers’ reported claims, qualify analogies between lawyers and software engineers, and recommend governed experimentation, client dialogue and value-focused pricing.
