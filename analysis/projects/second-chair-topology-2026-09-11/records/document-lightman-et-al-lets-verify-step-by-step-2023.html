Corpus framing of this source: Let’s Verify Step by Step: Process Supervision for Mathematical Reasoning

Corpus synthesis of this source: Lightman and colleagues compare step-level process supervision with final-result outcome supervision for reward models that rank generated solutions to MATH problems. They report stronger selection performance from process supervision, attribute the advantage to more precise credit assignment, test active learning and synthetic supervision, examine out-of-distribution generalisation, release PRM800K, and qualify the findings with baseline, comparability, contamination and active-learning limitations.

Corpus source-specific finding

Process supervision outperformed outcome supervision in mathematical reasoning experiments.

Exact returned source excerpt

We conduct our own investigation, finding that process supervision significantly outper- forms outcome supervision for training models to solve problems from the challenging MATH dataset.
