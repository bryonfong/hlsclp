Corpus framing of this source: Foundation-model ownership, resource barriers and concentrated power

Corpus synthesis of this source: On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models argues that concentrated ownership of models and data can concentrate economic power and restrict opportunity. It identifies resource disparities as a persistent barrier to community alternatives, while noting grassroots counterweights. The supplied excerpt offers qualitative reasoning and examples, rather than quantified evidence, and separately frames foundation models as operating-system-like components for security.

Corpus framing of this source: Foundation models as a sociotechnical infrastructure with concentrated risks and opportunities

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bommasani, Liang and the CRFM author team frame foundation models as broadly adaptable but incomplete technological foundations. They analyse how scale produces emergent capabilities, homogenisation, systemic security exposure and concentrated economic power, while emphasising unresolved knowledge gaps and the need for interdisciplinary research. The supplied record identifies the work as a wide-ranging arXiv preprint rather than a peer-reviewed journal article.

Corpus source-specific finding

Defines a new AI paradigm and broad analytical scope

Exact returned source excerpt

## Abstract AI is undergoing a paradigm shift with the rise of models (e.g., BERT, DALL-E, GPT-3) trained on broad data (generally using self-supervision at scale) that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks.

Exact returned source excerpt

We call these models foundation models to underscore their critically central yet incomplete character.

Corpus source-specific finding

Argues that scale creates emergent capabilities and systemic downstream risk

Exact returned source excerpt

Though foundation models are based on standard deep learning and transfer learning, their scale results in new emergent capabilities, and their effectiveness across so many tasks incentivizes homogenization.

Exact returned source excerpt

Homogenization provides powerful leverage but demands caution, as the defects of the foundation model are inherited by all the adapted models downstream.

Corpus source-specific finding

Highlights unresolved understanding and recommends interdisciplinary research

Exact returned source excerpt

Despite the impending widespread deployment of foundation models, we currently lack a clear understanding of how they work, when they fail, and what they are even capable of due to their emergent properties.

Exact returned source excerpt

To tackle these questions, we believe much of the critical research on foundation models will require deep interdisciplinary collaboration commensurate with their fundamentally sociotechnical nature.
