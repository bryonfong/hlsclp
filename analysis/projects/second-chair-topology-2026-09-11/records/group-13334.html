Digital legal practice: New media change safeguards, not core duties

Corpus synthesis: Existing professional duties continue to govern lawyers’ handling of digital documents, communications and technology-mediated work, but their application depends on the risks and features of each medium. The exemplars vary across file access and delivery, metadata, confidentiality, privilege, notification and technological safeguards, while distinguishing broad ownership claims from the minimum materials and protections lawyers must provide.
