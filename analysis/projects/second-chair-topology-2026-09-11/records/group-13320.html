Cross-domain innovation networks: Mobility and open anchors generate new forms without displacing incumbents

Corpus synthesis: The exemplars explain institutional renewal through boundary-crossing careers, collaborative communities and reciprocal network development rather than through technology or investment alone. Evidence from biotechnology, banking and regional innovation systems varies in the importance assigned to sequencing, leadership and incumbent sponsorship. These mechanisms can produce new organisational forms and regional advantages while leaving elite gatekeeping intact, although the conditions for wider transformation remain unresolved.
