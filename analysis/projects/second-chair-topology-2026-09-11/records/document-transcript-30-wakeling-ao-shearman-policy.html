Corpus framing of this source: David Wakeling: Turn fragmented AI principles into coordinated deployment rules

Corpus synthesis of this source: David Wakeling — A&O Shearman (AI regulation) argues that businesses need practical, internationally coordinated AI guidance rather than overlapping abstract principles. Drawing on client work and banking-regulation experience, he recommends a common framework, detailed standards, aligned timetables and mutual recognition, with continuing revision as evidence develops.

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: David Wakeling — A&O Shearman: From fragmented AI rules to mutual recognition

Corpus synthesis of this source: Wakeling argues from the perspective of a business deploying AI that proliferating, partly overlapping regulatory initiatives do not give deployers enough practical certainty. Drawing on A&O Shearman’s cross-jurisdictional deployment decisions and his experience of banking regulation, he advocates detailed, iterative, multistakeholder standard-setting that could ultimately support harmonisation and mutual recognition.
