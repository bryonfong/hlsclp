Corpus framing of this source: Vivienne Ming on designing AI augmentation and human–machine workflows

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ming argues that organisations should distinguish full automation from genuine augmentation, redesign work around human–AI co-creation, and preserve humans’ comparative strength in uncertain and novel problems. She supports this position with a forecasting exercise in which AI outperformed unaided participants, while a minority who actively challenged and collaborated with the AI approached prediction-market performance. She also identifies missing augmentation benchmarks, weak organisational adoption, and an unresolved pipeline problem for developing junior workers.
