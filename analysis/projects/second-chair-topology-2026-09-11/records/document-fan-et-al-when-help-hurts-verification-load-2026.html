Corpus framing of this source: AI coding interfaces: immediate gains alongside accumulating verification costs

Corpus synthesis of this source: When Help Hurts: Verification Load and Fatigue with AI Coding Assistants reports a controlled coding study that separates interface design from model capability. The authors find immediate performance benefits from AI assistance but rising fatigue across repeated tasks, propose a behavioral verification-load measure, and recommend adapting interfaces to task complexity and user experience. They qualify their mechanism and crossover analyses as observational or exploratory.
