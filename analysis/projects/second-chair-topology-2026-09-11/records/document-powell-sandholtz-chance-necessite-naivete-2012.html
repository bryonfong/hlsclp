Corpus framing of this source: How early biotechnology firms invented an organizational form across science, finance and commerce

Corpus synthesis of this source: Chance, Nécessité, et Naïveté: Ingredients to Create a New Organizational Form explains the emergence of dedicated biotech firms through historical case studies. The authors distinguish science-centered and commerce-centered variants, trace how founders and investors repurposed existing practices, and argue that these experiments transformed universities and industry without ensuring the survival or commercial superiority of the pioneering firms.
