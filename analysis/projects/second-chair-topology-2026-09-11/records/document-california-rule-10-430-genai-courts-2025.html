Corpus framing of this source: California court rules and standards for judicial generative-AI use

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source establishes a two-part governance framework, effective September 1, 2025, for generative-AI use by California court staff and judicial officers. Rule 10.430 imposes requirements, while Standard 10.80 provides guidance for judicial officers acting within their adjudicative role. The framework addresses discrimination, verification, hallucinated output, harmful content, public disclosure, confidentiality and compliance with existing legal and ethical duties. It offers normative safeguards rather than empirical evidence or predictions.

Corpus framing of this source: California court rules governing generative AI use

Corpus synthesis of this source: Rule 10.430 and Standard 10.80 establish a statewide governance framework for generative AI in California courts. They require courts that permit its use to adopt policies, prescribe safeguards for court-related work, set standards for judicial officers acting adjudicatively, define covered systems and personnel, and preserve courts’ discretion to impose stricter limits or prohibit use entirely.
