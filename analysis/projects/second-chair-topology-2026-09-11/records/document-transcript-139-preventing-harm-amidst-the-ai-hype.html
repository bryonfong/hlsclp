Corpus framing of this source: Legal-data scale and complementary models for useful legal AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that specialised legal AI cannot depend on legal text alone. Because legal corpora are small relative to general-purpose model training data, systems will continue to rely substantially on the open internet. It further contends that legally useful products need complementary data models—especially models representing relationships among cases—and identifies proprietary or inaccessible legal information as a development constraint.

Corpus source-specific finding
