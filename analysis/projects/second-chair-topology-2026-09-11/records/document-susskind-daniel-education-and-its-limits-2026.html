Open topic: Automation education: Adaptability helps but cannot suffice

Corpus framing of this source: Education as the best near-term response to automation, but not a complete solution

Corpus synthesis of this source: In Education – And Its Limits, Daniel Susskind distinguishes barriers to taking available jobs from a possible future shortage of work. He advocates redesigning education around uncertain technological change while arguing that skills provision cannot by itself address geographic mismatch, occupational identity, political power or the loss of purpose associated with paid work.

Builds onWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Education under technological uncertainty—and its limits

Corpus synthesis of this source: The lecture argues that AI belongs to a decades-long expansion of technological capability, but makes attempts to identify “future-proof” skills increasingly unreliable. It recommends preserving basic skills, teaching critical AI use, and adopting personalised and simulated learning. It also limits these prescriptions: education may help people adapt where work exists, but cannot by itself answer a structural shortage of paid work, provide socially recognised contribution outside employment, or control technology companies’ growing political power.
