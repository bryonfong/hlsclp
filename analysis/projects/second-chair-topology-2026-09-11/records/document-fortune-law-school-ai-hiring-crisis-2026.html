Corpus framing of this source: Law-school demand rises as experts warn of weaker entry-level hiring

Corpus synthesis of this source: The article contrasts strong recent law-graduate employment with warnings that rising enrolment, weak hiring elsewhere and possible AI substitution could worsen future prospects. It reports Mike Spivey’s oversupply concerns and Jen Leonard’s prediction of a weaker post-recession hiring recovery, while noting Spivey’s qualification that AI has not yet affected firms’ hiring processes.
