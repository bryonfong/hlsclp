Corpus framing of this source: Carta–Avantia as a possible model for software-led legal services

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source treats Carta Law as a notable private-capital development and explores whether software companies could acquire ALSPs to build AI-first law firms. It presents this broader trajectory as a possibility, not an established trend, and highlights regulatory conditions, acquisition fit and conflicts with existing software customers.

Corpus source-specific finding

Exact returned source excerpt

Exact returned source excerpt
