Corpus framing of this source: Goldman Sachs on AI’s prospective US labour-market effects

Corpus synthesis of this source: Goldman Sachs Research argues that AI is already causing limited, sector-specific labour displacement, while its broader effects will unfold mainly through firms’ adoption over roughly a decade. Its base case combines substantial worker displacement with a modest rise in aggregate unemployment, but warns that a faster transition would have larger economic effects. It also expects productivity gains, help filling labour gaps and job creation tied to AI infrastructure.
