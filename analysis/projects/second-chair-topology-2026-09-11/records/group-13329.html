AI adoption leadership: User outcomes and controlled trials must precede transformation claims

Corpus synthesis: These exemplars frame adoption as an accountable organisational practice grounded in customer problems, user feedback, provider collaboration and human oversight. They distinguish demonstrated workflow assistance from forecasts of embedded or autonomous transformation, making trust conditional on evidence rather than branding. Their emphasis varies from verification and distributed expertise to judgment, advocacy and pricing pressure, while employment and revenue effects remain unresolved.
