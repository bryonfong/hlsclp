Corpus synthesis of this source: Worledge and colleagues combine a Los Angeles debt-collection audit with a controlled evaluation of their Default Assistant. They report faster, more accurate human review, while treating the results as a proof of concept rather than evidence of established courtroom effectiveness. Their contribution centres on verifiable case-file citations, persistent over-reliance, narrowly scoped fairness testing and the need for realistic deployment studies.
