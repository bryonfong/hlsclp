Toward a New Model of the Corporate Attorney-Client Relationship, the author argues that strategic alliances better explain large companies’ relationships with outside law firms than a simple principal–agent model. The 2010 article combines historical scholarship, corporate purchasing research, interviews and company examples to trace the rise and limits of in-house control, emerging partnerships, and their implications for pricing, competition and professional independence.
