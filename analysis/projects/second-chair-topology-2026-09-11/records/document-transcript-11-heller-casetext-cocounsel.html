Corpus framing of this source: Jake Heller — From Idea to $650M Exit (Casetext / CoCounsel)

Corpus synthesis of this source: Heller argues that successful AI applications derive defensibility from sustained integration and quality-control work, while durable revenue depends on intensive customer adoption rather than pilots or software features alone.
