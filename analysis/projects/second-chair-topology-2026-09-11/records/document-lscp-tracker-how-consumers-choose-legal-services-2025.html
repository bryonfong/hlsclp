Corpus framing of this source: Tracker Survey 2025: How Consumers Are Choosing Legal Services

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Legal Services Consumer Panel reports high and broadly stable confidence in providers’ handling of client money, identifies lower confidence among several demographic and matter-type groups, and finds that regulated status leads consumers’ stated provider-selection criteria in 2025.

Corpus source-specific finding

Confidence in providers’ safeguarding of money remained high and stable.

Exact returned source excerpt

92% of respondents were confident that the legal service provider they used would keep their money safe, a similar proportion to that reported in 2024 (91%), 2023 (90%) and 2022 (91%).

Corpus source-specific finding

Confidence varied across demographic groups and types of legal problem.

Exact returned source excerpt

This figure was lower for those aged 18 to 24 (84%), and those from ethnic minorities (90%).

Exact returned source excerpt

It was also lower for those using services for housing problems as a tenant (88%), benefits and tax credits (83%) and neighbourhood disputes (84%).

Corpus source-specific finding

The survey treats consumer decision-making evidence as important to market assessment and consumer empowerment.

Exact returned source excerpt

Consumers’ decision making For both providers and regulators, understanding what influences consumers' decision making is key to assessing whether the market is responding to consumers’ needs and empowering consumers with the information they need to make informed decisions.

Corpus source-specific finding

Regulated status was the leading stated choice factor in 2025.

Exact returned source excerpt

For the first time in 2025, consumers were asked about how important a provider being regulated was in their decision-making.

Exact returned source excerpt

This was the most important factor, selected by nine in ten (89%).

Corpus source-specific finding

Established commercial and service attributes also remained important.

Exact returned source excerpt

Reputation, price, specialism and speed remain key factors, as seen in previous years.
