# Legal Tech Insights: Sheppard Mullin's Legora selection and AI roadmap *Legal IT Insider — Legal IT Insider (2025)* # Legal Tech Insights: Sheppard Mullin’s Legora selection and AI roadmap December 4, 2025 * - [Latest News](https://legaltechnology.com/category/latest-news/) Legal IT Insider spoke with Sheppard Mullin’s chief knowledge and innovation officer [Kate Cain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kate-cain-476187/) about the selection of Legora and how it fits within the Am Law 100 law firm’s wider generative AI strategy and roadmap.* In November, Am Law 100 law firm Sheppard Mullin announced a key milestone in its multi-year innovation roadmap – a partnership with European-founded GenAI provider Legora.

[*](https://www.harvey.ai/contract-intelligence?utm_medium=referral&utm_source=legal-it-insider&utm_content=display&utm_campaign=2026-q2--pmm--contracting) An early adopter of CoCounsel back in its pre-Thomson Reuters Casetext days, Sheppard Mullin is not a stranger to GenAI technology and has been keeping an eye on the legal AI assistant market, with chief knowledge and innovation officer Kate Cain observing: “Earlier this year, we were starting to hear from more and more clients expressing an interest in the kinds of projects that Legora and tools like it are really well suited for and decided that the technology itself had matured enough that it made sense for us to take a look at.” While the Legora partnership is the firm’s newest milestone, it is part of a broader effort to modernise how its lawyers work, evaluate AI tools, and embed innovation across practice groups.
