Corpus framing of this source: Gyi Tsakalakis on AI-enabled intake and the enduring value of human connection

Corpus synthesis of this source: Tsakalakis argues that AI can improve legal intake, analysis and drafting support, but firms still win consumer trust through responsiveness, reputation and human connection. He grounds the intake problem in his firm’s consumer research, Clio reports and secret-shopping observations; predicts that automated inquiry handling will become inexpensive table stakes; and recommends offering consumers both automated and human channels, auditing intake, and requiring human verification of AI output. He discloses investments in CaptureNow and Text-to-Chat.
