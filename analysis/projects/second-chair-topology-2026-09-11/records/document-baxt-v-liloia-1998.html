Corpus framing of this source: Baxt v. Liloia: Ethical Rules, Civil Liability and Discovery Sanctions

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Supreme Court of New Jersey separates attorney discipline from civil liability. It holds that a Rules of Professional Conduct violation does not independently create a civil claim against an adversary’s lawyer, while allowing relevant rules to inform an otherwise recognised duty or standard of care. Although the alleged concealment did not support tort damages, the court strongly condemns the attorneys’ discovery conduct and directs the plaintiffs toward discovery sanctions and recovery of reasonable expenses.
