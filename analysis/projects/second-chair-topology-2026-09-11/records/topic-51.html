Native topic findings

  1. Reliable model evaluation depends on the metric and statistical assumptions, not simply on collecting more observations.
  2. Mixed supervision creates a trade-off between flexible weighting and sharper uncertainty bounds.
  3. Practical plug-in weighting remains distinct from the assumptions supporting the sharper theorem.
  4. Coverage-profile thresholds can make rankings statistically unstable, even with minimum support probabilities.
  5. Additional structural assumptions restore specific ranking guarantees, but do not justify unrestricted score-based comparisons.
