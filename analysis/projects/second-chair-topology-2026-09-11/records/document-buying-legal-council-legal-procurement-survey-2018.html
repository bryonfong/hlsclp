Corpus framing of this source: 2018 Buying Legal Services Survey — Insights Into Legal Procurement

Corpus synthesis of this source: Buying Legal Council presents legal procurement as an increasingly professional, active and data-driven function that reduces legal spend and can improve provider outcomes. Drawing on a survey of 153 legal procurement professionals, it links stronger results to category tenure and collaboration with in-house legal and legal operations, documents broader adoption of procurement tools and provider consolidation, and identifies further growth opportunities in project management, pre-matter scoping and alternative providers. It treats the findings as indicative trends rather than representative estimates.

Corpus source-specific finding

The survey provides indicative evidence about legal-procurement practices, not representative market estimates.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey was completed by 153 legal procurement professionals and comprised 33 mostly multiple-choice questions and focused on purchasing decisions, cost control, analyses, and trends.

Exact returned source excerpt

The 2018 study was conducted from mid-December 2017 through early February 2018.

The Council argues that legal procurement has shifted legal spend from relationship-led purchasing toward active, business-driven management.
