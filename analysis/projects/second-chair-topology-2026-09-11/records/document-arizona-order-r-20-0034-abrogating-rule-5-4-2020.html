Corpus framing of this source: Arizona’s ABS framework couples nonlawyer participation with lawyer-led ethical safeguards

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Arizona Supreme Court’s R-20-0034 rulemaking supplies regulatory requirements and explanatory commentary, rather than empirical findings or predictions. Its principal contribution links licensed nonlawyer ownership and management to lawyer-led compliance, proportionate supervision, professional independence and misconduct reporting. Related provisions address outsourced assistance, practice sales and the boundaries of law-related services. The supplied amendment text interleaves old and revised wording, so isolated legacy provisions should not be treated as a clean statement of the resulting rules.
