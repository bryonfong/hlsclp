Corpus framing of this source: The Legal Industry's Shared AI Evaluation Framework

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents a buyer-oriented framework and toolkit for structured legal-AI procurement. It evaluates output robustness alongside security, privacy, vendor, adoption and cost considerations; applies progressively greater scrutiny across pre-demo screening, demonstrations and real-workflow pilots; and supports multiple stages of the procurement lifecycle. A multidisciplinary steering committee shapes the criteria, while the supplied static capture raises—but does not answer—questions about scope, governance, standards alignment, vendor participation and maintenance.
