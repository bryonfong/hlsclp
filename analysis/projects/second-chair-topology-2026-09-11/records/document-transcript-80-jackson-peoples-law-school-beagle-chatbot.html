Corpus framing of this source: Drew Jackson & Chris McGrath on Beagle+: a scalable, bounded public-legal-information chatbot

Corpus synthesis of this source: Drew Jackson and Chris McGrath present People’s Law School’s Beagle+ as a retrieval-augmented-generation chatbot designed to deliver tailored, plain-language legal information at scale while avoiding legal advice. They describe its reach, operating safeguards, human-review process and limits, and recommend referrals to people, cross-jurisdictional knowledge sharing and collaboration with large platforms.
