Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Employer forecasts of AI-driven job, skill and workforce transformation to 2030

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Future of Jobs Report 2025 synthesises employer expectations from the 2024 survey, rather than observed AI employment effects. It projects simultaneous job creation and displacement, rising demand for technical and human skills, and widespread retraining and automation. Its economy, region and industry profiles show substantial variation, including mixed forecasts for lawyers.

The report provides a broad employer-survey forecast, not a causal measurement of AI displacement.
