Corpus framing of this source: Verifiability as the organising principle of AI automation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Andrej Karpathy presents AI as a new computing paradigm and argues that verifiability now predicts which tasks will automate and improve fastest. He contrasts this with conventional computing’s dependence on tasks that humans can specify explicitly, explains verifiable-task progress through optimisation and repeated machine practice, and uses the framework to account for uneven capabilities across task types.
