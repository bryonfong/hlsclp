Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Future of Jobs Report 2025: uneven occupational growth and changing skill needs

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied profiles report employers’ expectations for business transformation, occupational growth and skills through 2030. They show strong demand for AI-related roles and skills, frequent declines in clerical roles, and substantial variation across industries and geographies—including contrasting outlooks for lawyers. These are survey-based outlooks, not observed displacement or evidence that AI alone causes the projected changes. Many excerpts omit profile names or contain truncated tables, limiting attribution and interpretation.
