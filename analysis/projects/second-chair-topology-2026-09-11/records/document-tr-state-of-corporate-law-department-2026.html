Open topic: Buyer transformation: Legal departments may capture AI gains

Corpus framing of this source: Legal departments must turn operational and AI gains into demonstrable business value

Corpus synthesis of this source: Thomson Reuters Institute’s open summary of its 2026 State of the Corporate Law Department Report describes a gap between general counsel’s assessment of legal’s contribution and other executives’ perceptions. It reports resource pressures and growing technology adoption, then argues that departments must demonstrate business outcomes rather than efficiency alone. The supplied material contains the summary article, not the full report.

Corpus source-specific finding

GCs and other executives report sharply different perceptions of legal’s contribution.

Exact returned source excerpt

Indeed, when asked how they view legal’s contribution to the rest of the business, 86% of GCs surveyed said they viewed the legal function as a significant contributor.

Exact returned source excerpt

However, only 17% of other C-Suite executives said the same — and 42% said legal contributes little or not at all.

Corpus source-specific finding

Resource constraints persist while GCs anticipate continued outside-counsel spending pressure.

Exact returned source excerpt

Nearly half of GCs cited staffing and resource constraints as the top barrier they face to delivering additional value.

Exact returned source excerpt

As of the fourth quarter of 2025, more than one-third (36%) of GCs said they expect to increase overall spend on outside counsel over the next year, while only 20% said they plan to decrease their spend.

Corpus source-specific finding

Departments have modernised operations and increasingly prioritise technology.

Exact returned source excerpt

GCs have adjusted their hiring practices to conform with the modern corporation, taking new ways of working into account.

Exact returned source excerpt

Corpus source-specific finding

AI availability and efficiency gains have outpaced measurement of business results.

Exact returned source excerpt

Exact returned source excerpt
