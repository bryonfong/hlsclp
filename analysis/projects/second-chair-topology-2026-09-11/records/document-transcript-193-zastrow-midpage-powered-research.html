Corpus framing of this source: Otto von Zastrow on AI-enabled legal research and MidPage

Corpus synthesis of this source: Von Zastrow argues that stronger AI and newly producible case-law datasets have lowered the historical data barrier to competing with Lexis and Westlaw. He presents MidPage’s proposition search, interface, natural-language filtering and research repository as ways to make litigation research faster, more precise and easier to organise. He predicts broader data competition, API access and AI-assisted research of much greater depth, while qualifying that high-quality data maintenance remains difficult and recommending constrained use of ChatGPT to reduce unsupported answers.
