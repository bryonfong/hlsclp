Corpus framing of this source: How People Use ChatGPT: consumer adoption, work patterns and decision-support value

Corpus synthesis of this source: Chatterji and colleagues’ September 2025 working paper analyses consumer ChatGPT usage through July 2025. It documents expanding non-work use, writing-heavy work use and demographic convergence, and argues that advice and decision support offer an important route to economic value. Corpus framing of this source: How People Use ChatGPT: Usage, Work Activities and Economic Value

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper uses internal consumer ChatGPT message data and privacy-preserving automated classification to describe who uses ChatGPT, for what purposes and with what apparent interaction quality. Its main contribution is empirical: it documents rapid growth led by non-work use, identifies information, guidance and writing as dominant activities, distinguishes decision support from task production, maps work use to O*NET activities and interprets ChatGPT primarily as a decision-support tool in knowledge-intensive work. It also reports sampling boundaries and substantial variation in classifier reliability.
