Corpus framing of this source: Matter of Weber — AI-generated evidence, fiduciary-duty proof and damages

Corpus synthesis of this source: Jonathan G. Schopf’s 2024 Saratoga County Surrogate’s Court decision rejects all objections to a trustee’s interim accounting and addresses an expert’s use of Microsoft Copilot. The court distinguishes generative from assistive AI, requires counsel to disclose AI-generated evidence, and holds that such evidence should face a court-defined Frye hearing before admission because reliability and general acceptance cannot be assumed.

The dispute concerned retention and mixed use of trust property and resulting damages.
