Corpus framing of this source: Garcia v. Character Technologies: AI speech, product liability and surviving tort claims

Corpus synthesis of this source: At the motion-to-dismiss stage, the court declined to classify Character A.I.’s LLM output as protected speech, treated the app as a product only for claims based on design defects rather than its ideas or expressions, and allowed most pleaded claims to proceed. It dismissed the intentional-infliction-of-emotional-distress claim, preserved jurisdictional discovery, and repeatedly qualified its conclusions as pleading-stage determinations based on allegations that discovery could disprove.
