Corpus framing of this source: Harvey’s CEO on secure collaboration, selective automation and faster lawyer development

Corpus synthesis of this source: Connie Loizos’s November 2025 TechCrunch interview presents Winston Weinberg’s account of Harvey’s growth and strategy. He describes current legal-AI uses and infrastructure constraints, prioritises cross-organisation permissions, and predicts that selective automation and AI tutoring can shift firms toward developing partners faster. The article reports company growth claims; Weinberg’s capability, training and pricing forecasts remain prospective rather than demonstrated outcomes.
