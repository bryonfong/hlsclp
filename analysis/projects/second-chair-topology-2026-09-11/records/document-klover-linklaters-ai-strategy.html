### Laila A cornerstone of this proprietary core is Laila**, the firm’s homegrown GenAI chatbot.7 Critically, Laila is built using Microsoft’s secure Azure OpenAI service, which ensures that all prompts and their corresponding responses are kept within a secure, Linklaters-controlled environment.7 This addresses one of the most significant barriers to AI adoption in the legal sector: the risk of exposing confidential client data to public models.9 Laila serves as a secure, internal alternative to public tools like ChatGPT, and its high rate of adoption, currently handling over 60,000 prompts per week, indicates its successful integration into the daily work of the firm’s personnel.10 This high level of engagement also makes Laila an invaluable platform for testing new use cases and evolving its features based on real-world user feedback.7 ### AI Sandbox To accelerate the development of such tools, Linklaters has launched the **AI Sandbox**, a structured initiative designed to rapidly build and prototype GenAI solutions.10 The Sandbox functions as the firm’s innovation engine, providing a formal pathway to turn promising ideas into working prototypes.
