Corpus framing of this source: UNESCO’s framework for human-supervised, rights-respecting judicial AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Guidelines for the Use of AI Systems in Courts and Tribunals proposes lifecycle governance for courts and individual judicial professionals. It treats AI’s efficiency and access benefits as potential gains, not grounds for replacing judicial reasoning, and translates human-rights principles into oversight, contestability, verification, inclusive design and training recommendations.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI should assist judicial work without replacing independent human judgment.

Exact returned source excerpt

AI is envisaged not as a replacement for judicial reasoning, but as an assistive tool to expand access to information, enhance efficiency, and support equitable justice—always under meaningful human su- pervision and ethical review.
