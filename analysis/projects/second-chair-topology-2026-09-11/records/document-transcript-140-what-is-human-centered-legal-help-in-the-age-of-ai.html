Corpus framing of this source: Human-centred legal help: build connected pathways before adopting AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Amanda Brown presents a human-centred access-to-justice model in which technology helps people move among information, self-help and human assistance rather than serving automatically as the endpoint. She describes LANAP’s connected resource infrastructure, cautions against unstrategic AI adoption and replacement, and treats AI personas and changes in service-provider models as plausible but unsettled developments. Host Cat Moon separately endorses preserving the human nature of justice as legal technology advances.
