Corpus framing of this source: The Forms of Capital

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents capital as convertible forms of accumulated social energy. It argues that economic capital provides direct access to some goods and services, while others require social relationships and obligations. It also explains how delay, attention and personalization transform exchange into gratitude and obscure its monetary character, despite appearing wasteful from a narrowly economic perspective.
