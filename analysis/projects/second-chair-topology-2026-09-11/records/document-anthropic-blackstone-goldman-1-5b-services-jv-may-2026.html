# Anthropic takes shot at consulting industry in joint venture with Wall Street giants *Nick Lichtenberg — Fortune (2026)* # Anthropic takes shot at consulting industry in joint venture with Wall Street giants By Nick Lichtenberg, Business Editor — May 4, 2026, 10:37 AM ET Anthropic announced Monday that it has partnered with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs to launch a new AI-native enterprise services company — one that puts the Claude maker in direct competition with the world’s largest consulting firms for the lucrative business of corporate AI transformation.

Exact returned source excerpt

The venture, backed by approximately $1.5 billion in committed capital, is designed to embed Anthropic’s engineers and models directly into the core operations of mid-size businesses, according to the _Wall Street Journal_ citing people familiar with the matter.

Corpus source-specific finding

The article argues that embedded engineering and investor networks give the venture an advantage over conventional consultants.

Exact returned source excerpt

The new firm is a standalone entity with Anthropic engineering resources embedded directly within its team, according to the official press release, a structure that mirrors Palantir’s forward-deployment model and undercuts traditional consultants by combining implementation capability with ownership of the underlying model.

Exact returned source excerpt

Alongside the three founding partners, the joint venture has drawn backing from General Atlantic, Leonard Green, Apollo Global Management, Singapore’s sovereign wealth fund GIC, and Sequoia Capital, giving it a built-in client pipeline across hundreds of portfolio companies.

Corpus source-specific finding

Executives present the venture as a response to scarce implementation talent and unmet demand.

Exact returned source excerpt

In an implicit indictment of the existing consulting model, Blackstone President and COO Jon Gray said the firm aims to break down “one of the most significant bottlenecks to enterprise AI adoption,” namely the scarcity of engineers who can implement frontier AI systems at speed.

Exact returned source excerpt

“Enterprise demand for Claude is significantly outpacing any single delivery model,” Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao said.
