Corpus framing of this source: RULERS: fixed rubrics, evidence grounding and calibration for reliable LLM judging

Corpus synthesis of this source: Hong and co-authors frame rubric-based LLM evaluation as transferring human criteria into a stable, auditable scoring protocol. They propose RULERS, a three-phase framework that locks a task-level rubric bundle, requires structured and verifiable textual evidence, and calibrates model-derived signals to human score distributions. Experiments across four benchmarks and four frozen LLM backbones support stronger human-score alignment, distributional stability and robustness than tested baselines, while the authors qualify these findings with cost, transfer, rubric-quality, calibration-data, model, fairness, privacy and misuse limitations.
