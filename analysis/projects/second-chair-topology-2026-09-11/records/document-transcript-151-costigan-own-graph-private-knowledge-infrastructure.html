Corpus framing of this source: Private AI sovereignty and adversarial first-draft workflows

Corpus synthesis of this source: Stephen Costigan presents Atlas AI as private knowledge infrastructure that keeps firm data and related artefacts inside the firm’s environment while accessing models under zero-data-retention terms. He argues that this architecture makes data flows directly demonstrable to governance stakeholders and predicts that firms will therefore entrust sensitive matters to private AI. Separately, he adopts Greg Lambert’s “red-pen” framing and describes a copilot workflow in which an adversarial system challenges a cited first draft before a lawyer edits it and exercises judgment. The excerpt offers architectural explanations and predictions rather than empirical performance evidence.
