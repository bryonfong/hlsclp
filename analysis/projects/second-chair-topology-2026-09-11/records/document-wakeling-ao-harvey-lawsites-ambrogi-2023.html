# As Allen & Overy Deploys GPT-based Legal App Harvey Firmwide, Founders Say Other Firms Will Soon Follow *Bob Ambrogi — LawSites (LawNext) (2023)* As Allen & Overy Deploys GPT-based Legal App Harvey Firmwide, Founders Say Other Firms Will Soon Follow | LawSites Yesterday, [Allen & Overy](https://www.allenovery.com/en-gb/global), one of the world’s largest law firms, [announced](https://www.allenovery.com/en-gb/global/news-and-insights/news/ao-announces-exclusive-launch-partnership-with-harvey) that it had integrated the legal artificial intelligence product [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/) into its global practice, where it will by used by more than 3,500 lawyers across 43 offices operating in multiple languages.

Exact returned source excerpt

Allen & Overy (A&O) now describes itself as “the first law firm to use generative AI that’s based on OpenAI’s GPT models.” While that appears to be true, it also true that others are soon to follow.

Corpus source-specific finding

Harvey offered natural-language document creation and legal research using GPT-3, not ChatGPT.

Exact returned source excerpt

Harvey uses the GPT-3 technology (*not* ChatGPT) to enable lawyers to create legal documents or perform legal research by providing simple instructions using natural language.

Exact returned source excerpt

I [wrote about Harvey in November](https://www.lawnext.com/2022/11/stealth-legal-ai-startup-harvey-raises-5m-in-round-led-by-openai.html), when the previously stealth startup emerged with news that it had raised $5 million in funding led by the startup fund of OpenAI, the company that is the developer of the GPT AI technology.

Corpus source-specific finding

Reported trial figures show broad use across A&O’s client work, languages and practice areas.

Exact returned source excerpt

### ‘Game Changer’ A&O said it began a trial of Harvey in November.

Exact returned source excerpt

By the end of the trial, some 3,500 A&O lawyers had asked Harvey around 40,000 queries for their day-to-day client work.

Corpus source-specific finding

Harvey’s founders expected further deployments, with firms choosing practice-specific or firmwide adoption.

Exact returned source excerpt

/in/winston-weinberg/), formerly an associate at law firm O’Melveny & Myers, and [Gabriel Pereyra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabepereyra/), formerly a research scientist at DeepMind and a machine learning engineer at Meta AI, they told me that they are working with other firms that are similarly preparing to deploy Harvey.

Exact returned source excerpt

They are not ready to name names and will leave it to the firms to make their own announcements to clients and the public.

Corpus source-specific finding

A&O’s trial leader characterised Harvey as potentially transformative.

Exact returned source excerpt

[David Wakeling](https://www.allenovery.com/en-gb/global/people/David-Wakeling), A&O partner and head of its Markets Innovation Group, which led the Harvey trial, said the technology is a game-changer.

Exact returned source excerpt

“I have been at the forefront of legal tech for 15 years but I have never seen anything like Harvey,” Wakeling said.
