Corpus synthesis of this source: Kyle Walters argues that AI is eroding the labor-intensive production work supporting the traditional CPA-firm pyramid. He recommends designing firms around enduring client demands—trustworthy judgment, accuracy, responsiveness and coordination—while distinguishing automatable, structured tasks from contextual professional judgment that he expects AI will not soon master.
