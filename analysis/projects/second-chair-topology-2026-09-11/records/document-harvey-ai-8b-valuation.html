# Legal AI Startup Harvey Confirms $8B Valuation *Julie Bort — TechCrunch (2025)* Venture # Legal AI startup Harvey confirms $8B valuation Julie Bort 10:37 AM PST · December 4, 2025 Harvey on Thursday confirmed it closed a round of funding, led by Andreessen Horowitz, that values the legal AI startup at $8 billion after reports of the funding leaked in October.

Exact returned source excerpt

The startup raised $160 million in the round.

Corpus source-specific finding

Harvey reported substantial adoption and revenue, but initially disclosed limited business metrics.

Exact returned source excerpt

In September, just before raising this latest mega round, Harvey released some details about its business.

Exact returned source excerpt

While it declined to share any absolute numbers, just percentages of growth and retention (it later told TechCrunch that it surpassed $100 million in annual recurring revenue back in August), it said that it counts 50 of the top AmLaw 100 firms as its customers.

Bort sees both a strong legal use case for LLMs and a funding-driven adoption mechanism.

Exact returned source excerpt
