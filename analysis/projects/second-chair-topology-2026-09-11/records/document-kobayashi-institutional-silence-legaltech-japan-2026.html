Corpus framing of this source: Institutional silence, LegalTech adoption and generative AI in Japan

Corpus synthesis of this source: The article distinguishes institutionally embedded LegalTech from general-purpose generative AI and uses enterprise survey evidence to explain their different patterns of adoption in Japan. It argues that conventional LegalTech mainly supports internal workflows without disturbing lawyers’ professional authority, while generative AI is diffusing informally beyond established regulatory structures. It links these patterns to an institutional silence that preserves professional legitimacy but may prove fragile, and it highlights the limited attention paid to individuals, small businesses and access to justice.
