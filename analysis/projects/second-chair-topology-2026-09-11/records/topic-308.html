Native topic findings

  1. AI creates demand for implementation talent even as it raises uncertainty about conventional legal hiring.
  2. Firms differ over whether to recruit specialist leaders or develop AI capability within existing teams.
  3. Proprietary knowledge motivates AI development, but build-versus-buy remains a conditional strategic choice.
  4. Client pressure makes demonstrable savings important, but spending, usage and targets do not establish value delivered.
  5. Current hiring strength coexists with warnings about the sustainability and timing of recruitment.
  6. Recruiting decisions increasingly confront uncertainty about future junior work, not demonstrated wholesale displacement.
