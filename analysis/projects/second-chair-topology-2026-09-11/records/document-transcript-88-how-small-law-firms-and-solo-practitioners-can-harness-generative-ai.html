Corpus framing of this source: Feedback learning, authorized action and retrieval beyond training cutoffs

Corpus synthesis of this source: Jen Leonard explains AI through a qualified analogy to human learning and distinguishes generating content from taking authorized action. She describes internet retrieval as an existing, simpler form of agency while stressing that retrieving current information does not update a model’s training. Bridget McCormack agrees with that distinction. The passage offers conceptual explanations and an illustrative scenario, not empirical findings.
