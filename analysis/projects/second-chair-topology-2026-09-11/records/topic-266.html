AI contracting: Deal velocity can undermine negotiation

Corpus synthesis: Shifts from software or hours toward completed work and bounded prices while warning that speed is not quality-adjusted value.

Native topic findings

  1. AI-first contracting shifts the proposition from selling software or lawyer time to delivering completed legal work.
  2. Deal velocity and bounded pricing replace hours worked as the central commercial measures, without eliminating hourly billing everywhere.
  3. Reported speed gains remain heterogeneous and do not establish comparable, quality-adjusted productivity improvements.
  4. Optimising speed creates a recognised risk of sacrificing clients’ negotiating interests.
  5. Business context and human–AI task allocation matter as much as document generation.
  6. The model seeks to expand expert capacity, but its implications for legal careers remain unsettled.
  7. Expansion beyond routine contracting is a strategic hypothesis, contested by incumbent investment and general-purpose AI.
