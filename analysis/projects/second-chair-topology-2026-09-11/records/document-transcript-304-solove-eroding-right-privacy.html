Corpus framing of this source: Daniel Solove on AI’s erosion of privacy protections

Corpus synthesis of this source: Solove argues that AI intensifies longstanding weaknesses in US privacy law by making pervasive data collection, inference and surveillance economically scalable. He rejects both blanket technology bans and reliance on consumer consent, advocating stronger default protections, data minimisation, limits on government access and context-sensitive rules that preserve beneficial uses such as public accountability.
