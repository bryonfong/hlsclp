Corpus framing of this source: Supply structure, scalable duplication and personal-service diseconomies

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source extends its superstar-market model across two contrasting supply structures: services that technology can duplicate for many consumers and one-to-one professional services constrained by the seller’s time. It argues that market size can affect the service quality consumers experience, including through congestion, reduced personal attention and overexposure.
