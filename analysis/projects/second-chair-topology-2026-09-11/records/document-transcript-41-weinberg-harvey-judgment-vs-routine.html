Corpus framing of this source: Winston Weinberg on legal AI, human judgment, demand and adoption

Corpus synthesis of this source: Harvey CEO Winston Weinberg argues that AI will automate and commoditise routine legal production while increasing the relative value of experienced human judgment. He explains why current task-level automation has not clearly reduced legal fees, predicts agent–human workflows and expanding legal demand, identifies regulatory limits on AI-only law firms, and recommends closer provider–buyer collaboration and more adaptive organisational decision-making. The observation that firms using Harvey had not reduced fees came from the interviewer’s private-equity contact, not from Weinberg’s own evidence.
