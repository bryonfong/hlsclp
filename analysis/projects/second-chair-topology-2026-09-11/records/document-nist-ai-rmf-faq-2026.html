Corpus framing of this source: NIST’s AI RMF: voluntary, lifecycle-wide risk management with contextual tradeoffs

Corpus synthesis of this source: NIST’s FAQs explain the AI Risk Management Framework’s purpose, scope, development and implementation resources. They present trustworthiness as a multidimensional, context-dependent objective rather than a guaranteed result of addressing individual characteristics, and recommend adaptable guidance across the AI lifecycle.

Corpus source-specific finding

The framework aims to manage broad AI risks and foster multiple characteristics of trustworthiness.

Exact returned source excerpt

**What is the AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)?**\ \ The Framework is intended to help developers, users and evaluators of AI systems better manage AI risks which could affect individuals, organizations, society, or the environment.

Exact returned source excerpt

It aims to foster the development of innovative approaches to address characteristics of trustworthiness including valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, and fair with harmful bias managed.\   2.

**When do these characteristics apply and can organizations which apply these characteristics ensure that their AI systems will be trustworthy?**\ \ The Framework users and AI actors should consider and encompass trustworthiness characteristics during pre-design, design and development, deployment, use, and test and evaluation of AI technologies and systems.

Corpus synthesis of this source: The AI Risk Management Framework FAQs present NIST’s rationale, intended scope, development process and supporting resources for the AI RMF. NIST characterises trustworthiness as contextual and multidimensional, argues that AI-specific risks warrant dedicated guidance, and offers the framework and companion Playbook as voluntary, adaptable resources shaped through public-private consultation.
