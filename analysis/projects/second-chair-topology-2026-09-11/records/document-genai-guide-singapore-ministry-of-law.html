Corpus synthesis of this source: The Guide for Using Generative Artificial Intelligence in the Legal Sector offers non-binding guidance for responsible adoption in Singapore. It presents GenAI primarily as an aid to legal work, grounds safeguards in existing professional duties, and translates those duties into verification, confidentiality, disclosure and governance practices. Its contribution is practical and normative rather than a measured assessment of AI performance.
