Corpus synthesis of this source: Andrew R. Lee, Jason M. Loring and Graham H. Ryan synthesize forecasts from Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey, Thomson Reuters and other legal-technology analysts. They argue that legal AI will move beyond experimentation in 2026, driven by agentic workflows, in-house adoption, contract automation and regulatory mandates. They temper that outlook with unresolved hallucination risk, uncertain workforce effects, fragmented US regulation and evidence of weak or unclear returns on some AI investments.
