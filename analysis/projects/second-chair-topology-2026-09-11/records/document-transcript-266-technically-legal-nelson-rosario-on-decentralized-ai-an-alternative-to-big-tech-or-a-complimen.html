Corpus framing of this source: Nelson Rosario on decentralized AI’s origins, control points and institutional role

Corpus synthesis of this source: Rosario argues that decentralized AI emerged under conditions unlike earlier open internet systems: concentrated compute and data, transformer technology and consumer-facing generative AI gave large technology companies an early advantage. He treats decentralization as a perspective-dependent effort to reduce infrastructure, model or data choke points rather than a single architecture. He also presents nonprofit coordination as a way to convene regulators, policymakers, decentralization advocates and established technology companies. The discussion distinguishes Rosario’s claims from an interlocutor’s concerns about market concentration and the relative difficulty of decentralizing models.
