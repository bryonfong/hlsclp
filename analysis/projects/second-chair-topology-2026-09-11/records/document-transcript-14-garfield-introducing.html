Corpus framing of this source: Introducing Garfield AI: Faster, Lower-Cost Small-Claims Debt Recovery

Corpus synthesis of this source: The speaker presents Garfield as a pay-as-you-go legal-AI service designed to make small-debt recovery faster and more economical, particularly for small businesses. The argument rests on the structured nature of small claims, technology’s document-processing speed, and fees designed around recoverable court costs. The speaker qualifies early performance claims, limits current suitability to lower-value claims, and predicts that modular design and advances in reasoning-focused models could support broader legal uses.
