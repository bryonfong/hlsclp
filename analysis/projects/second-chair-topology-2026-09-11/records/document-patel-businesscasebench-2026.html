Corpus framing of this source: Frontier AI performance across the business disciplines: a case-grounded benchmark of knowledge work and analytical reasoning

Corpus synthesis of this source: Patel and co-authors argue that conventional AI benchmarks miss important forms of white-collar analytical work. Their case-grounded benchmark distinguishes plausible drafts from complete answers, draws implications for business education and labour-market exposure, and identifies context-dependent areas of possible human advantage. They also report reproducibility and contamination checks.
