Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Privileged information for expert-level automated LM evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Zhou and co-authors propose a training-free evaluation method that supplies LM graders with ground-truth solutions, prompt-specific guidelines, references, captions or prior ratings. Across language, multimodal and frontier-reasoning benchmarks, they report stronger agreement with humans, improved model separability and reduced selected grading biases, while identifying limits around self-enhancement bias and overly easy or difficult evaluations.

Corpus source-specific finding

The paper frames frontier evaluation as a competence paradox and proposes privileged information as the remedy.

Exact returned source excerpt

But this presents a paradox: how can we trust the grader LM, which is presumably weaker than the candidate LM, to assess problems that are beyond the frontier of the capabilities of either model or both?

Exact returned source excerpt

For instance, today’s arXiv:2502.10961v1 [cs.LG] 16 Feb 2025 LMs struggle on graduate-level physics and Olympiad-level math, making them unreliable graders in these domains.
