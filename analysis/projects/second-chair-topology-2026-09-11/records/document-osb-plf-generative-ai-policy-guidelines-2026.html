Corpus framing of this source: A discretionary framework for accountable generative-AI use in law firms

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Oregon State Bar Professional Liability Fund’s April 2026 practice aid recommends firm-specific AI policies covering tool approval, permitted tasks, confidentiality, verification, professional duties and ongoing governance. It offers practical risk-management guidance rather than empirical findings or forecasts, and keeps responsibility for legal work with humans.

Corpus framing of this source: Oregon PLF framework for law-firm generative-AI policies

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Oregon State Bar Professional Liability Fund presents a risk-based practice framework for law firms to govern generative AI. It recommends tailoring policies to firm circumstances, defining approved tools and accountability, restricting risky uses, protecting confidential data, verifying outputs through qualified human review, and integrating existing professional duties. It offers guidance rather than empirical findings or mandatory implementation rules.
