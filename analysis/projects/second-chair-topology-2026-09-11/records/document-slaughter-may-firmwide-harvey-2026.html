# Slaughter and May Adopts Harvey Firmwide *Harvey Team — Harvey (2026)* Slaughter and May, the leading international law firm, is rolling out Harvey’s AI platform firmwide.

Exact returned source excerpt

The firm is adopting Harvey’s full platform across all practice areas to support its lawyers on multi-jurisdictional matters spanning M&A, due diligence, regulatory research, and document analysis.
