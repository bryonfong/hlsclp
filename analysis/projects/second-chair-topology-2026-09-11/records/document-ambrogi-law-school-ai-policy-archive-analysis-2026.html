Open topic: AI curriculum: Assistance must preserve intellectual development

Corpus framing of this source: Law schools pair AI restrictions with expanding instruction and redesigned assessment

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bob Ambrogi analyses Andrew Perlman’s law-school AI policy archive and argues that schools increasingly restrict AI in assessed work while teaching its use elsewhere. He contextualises Berkeley’s controversial policy, documents curricular and assessment changes, and recommends the archive as a policy resource while emphasising its incomplete coverage and AI-assisted, partly unverified methodology.

Corpus source-specific finding

The archive offers a broad, traceable snapshot rather than a comprehensive or fully verified census.

Default prohibition with instructor opt-in dominates the catalogued assessment policies.
