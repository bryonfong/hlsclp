Corpus framing of this source: Private equity hospital acquisitions: higher income and charges, with mixed quality changes

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bruch, Gondi and Song's 2020 cohort study associates private equity acquisition with higher hospital income, charges and reported illness burden, a lower Medicare discharge share, and improvements in some aggregate quality measures. The authors qualify the overall findings with differences between HCA and non-HCA hospitals and frame their results as associations.

Corpus source-specific finding

The study compares acquired hospitals with matched controls through three years after acquisition.

**DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS:** This cohort study identified 204 hospitals acquired by private equity firms from 2005 to 2017 and 532 matched hospitals not acquired by private equity.
