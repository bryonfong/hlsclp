Corpus framing of this source: Withers v. City of Aberdeen sanctions order on unverified AI citations

Corpus synthesis of this source: The court treats fabricated AI-generated legal authority as a Rule 11 and judicial-process violation, applies responsibility separately to drafting and local counsel, and imposes monetary, practice-related, educational and disciplinary sanctions. It stresses independent verification, meaningful review by signing counsel, deterrence, candor and accountability, while distinguishing bad-faith AI misuse from negligent supervisory failures.
