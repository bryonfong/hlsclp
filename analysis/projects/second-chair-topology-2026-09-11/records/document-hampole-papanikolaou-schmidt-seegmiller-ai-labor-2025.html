Corpus framing of this source: AI task substitution, offsetting reallocation and firm growth

Corpus synthesis of this source: Artificial Intelligence and the Labor Market studies first-wave AI adoption during 2014–2023. It argues that substantial substitution within tasks produces modest net changes in employment composition because workers reallocate effort and adopting firms grow. Its contribution combines granular exposure measurement, a task-based model and empirical estimates, while qualifying generalisation beyond publicly traded firms and to generative AI.
