Corpus framing of this source: CELA 2025: reorganising Microsoft’s legal and external-affairs function for regulation, customers and societal challenges

Corpus synthesis of this source: Brad Smith’s June 2021 internal memorandum presents Microsoft’s largest CELA expansion since 2010 and its biggest reorganisation in 20 years. He frames the four-year plan around public trust and regulatory adaptation, technology-enabled customer transformation, and societal impact. He supports the plan with Microsoft’s regulatory experience and recent initiatives, forecasts wider global regulation, prescribes early investment and cross-company governance, details corresponding team and leadership changes, and introduces a post-COVID employee-renewal programme.

Corpus source-specific finding

Smith frames CELA 2025 as a major, mission-led expansion organised around three strategic pillars.

Exact returned source excerpt

We’re embarking on the largest expansion of our team since 2010 and our biggest re-organization in 20 years.

Exact returned source excerpt

It’s all part of what we call “CELA 2025,” as we prepare for what Microsoft, our customers, and the world will need from us in the middle of the decade.

Corpus source-specific finding

He predicts rapid, geographically varied regulation across numerous technology-related legal fields and treats it as a durable consequence of technology’s social importance.

Exact returned source excerpt

We’re clearly entering an era of rapidly expanding global technology regulation.

Exact returned source excerpt
