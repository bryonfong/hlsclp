Corpus framing of this source: Anthropic’s Matt Samuels and Den Delimarsky on MCP-enabled legal workflows

Corpus synthesis of this source: Samuels and Delimarsky present MCP as an open, composable connection layer that lets AI clients use enterprise systems and data within unified legal workflows. They argue that established security controls can govern access, while plugins can combine tools, firm-specific skills and automated checks. They anticipate rapid capability and adoption gains but qualify forecasts because change is unusually fast, and Samuels warns that excessive delegation could erode lawyers’ strategic judgment and professional craft.
