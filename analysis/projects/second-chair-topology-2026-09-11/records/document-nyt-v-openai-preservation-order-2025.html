Corpus framing of this source: In re OpenAI (NYT v. OpenAI) — preservation and segregation of output logs

Corpus synthesis of this source: Magistrate Judge Ona T. Wang orders OpenAI to preserve and segregate output-log data that would otherwise be deleted while the court separately considers the deletion dispute. The order reverses the practical effect of an earlier refusal to require wholesale preservation after renewed submissions indicated potentially significant deletions and did not establish that OpenAI would preserve the relevant data voluntarily.

The court initially rejected wholesale preservation but explored narrower safeguards.
