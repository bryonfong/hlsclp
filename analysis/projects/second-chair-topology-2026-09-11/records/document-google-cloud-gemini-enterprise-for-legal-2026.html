Corpus framing of this source: Gemini Enterprise for Legal: Google Cloud’s governed agentic platform for legal workflows

Corpus synthesis of this source: Google Cloud presents Gemini Enterprise for Legal as a preview-stage, purpose-built platform that combines legal skills, enterprise connectors, task-executing agents, partner integrations and centralized governance. It argues that legal AI requires this system-level architecture—not a general-purpose model or fragmented point solutions—to preserve confidentiality, permissions and grounding while automating end-to-end work. The source illustrates intended workflows and integrations and reports supportive expectations from four launch firms, but supplies no quantitative outcome data.
