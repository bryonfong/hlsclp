Corpus synthesis of this source: Barke, James and Polikarpova develop a grounded theory of Copilot-assisted programming from observations of 20 participants completing varied tasks in four languages. They identify acceleration and exploration as distinct interaction modes, examine how expertise, task, prompting and validation shape those modes, and derive interface recommendations.
