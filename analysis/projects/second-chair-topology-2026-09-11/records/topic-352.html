Native topic findings

  1. Outside capital could finance AI and organisational change, but permission to invest does not create an investable business.
  2. ABS ownership and MSO investment offer different trade-offs between financial participation and geographic reach.
  3. The regulatory landscape permits selective experimentation, not a uniform national opening.
  4. Licensing and reported transactions establish market entry, but not widespread adoption or improved outcomes.
  5. The central disagreement concerns whether operational ownership can remain separate from professional control.
  6. Practical safeguards must govern compensation, decision rights and financial distress—not merely formal ownership.
  7. Access-to-justice benefits remain contested and distinct from commercial efficiency.
  8. Firms need a credible use of capital and durable enterprise value before accepting investor obligations.
