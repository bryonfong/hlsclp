Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Finite-sample limits on generative-model evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: A Theoretical Framework for Statistical Evaluability of Generative Models develops mathematical guarantees and impossibility results for ranking models from held-out IID data. It distinguishes approximate ranking from metric estimation, identifies rare-event sensitivity and discontinuity as obstacles, and qualifies when perplexity can support distributional comparisons.

Corpus source-specific finding

The paper treats statistical evaluation as a distinct, complementary approach to benchmarks and human feedback.

Exact returned source excerpt

By statistical evaluation, we mean assessing models using held-out IID data drawn from a ground truth distribution.
