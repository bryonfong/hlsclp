Corpus framing of this source: Vos on expanding judicial AI while preserving human adjudication and informed consent

Corpus synthesis of this source: In his 30 April 2026 lecture to the Judicial Institute for Scotland, Sir Geoffrey Vos anticipates wider AI use in routine judicial decisions but distinguishes assistance from replacing human adjudication. He argues for informed consent, careful legislative debate and continuing human responsibility for final appeals and legal development, while describing legal constraints and changing public expectations.

Corpus framing of this source: Sir Geoffrey Vos on AI, generational change and the future judiciary

Corpus synthesis of this source: Vos argues that justice systems must urgently combine technological redesign with clearer public communication. He predicts extensive AI involvement in routine adjudication but preserves a distinctive role for humans in final rights decisions, legal development, explanation and institutional legitimacy. He advocates transparent online justice systems designed with younger generations, subject to informed consent, legislation, human oversight and careful debate.
