Corpus framing of this source: Closing the AI Accountability Gap: an end-to-end framework for internal algorithmic auditing

Corpus synthesis of this source: Raji and co-authors propose SMACTR, a pre-deployment internal audit framework that operationalizes organizational AI principles across the development lifecycle. They distinguish ethical and social accountability from technical reliability, adapt practices from safety engineering, medicine and finance, and prescribe documentation, interdisciplinary review, testing, reflection and remediation while acknowledging contextual, procedural and institutional limits.
