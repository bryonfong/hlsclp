Corpus framing of this source: Descrybe’s low-cost, precomputed approach to AI-assisted legal research

Corpus synthesis of this source: Kara Peterson and Rich DiBona present Descrybe as an affordable legal-research platform built on curated legal data, precomputed AI analysis and database retrieval rather than live LLM generation. They describe its research and citation features, planned source expansion, hallucination safeguards and privacy choices; explain how AI made the product economically feasible; and predict accelerating technological change, data competition and talent constraints in legal services. Most evidence consists of product descriptions, anecdotal user feedback and conference reactions rather than measured adoption or error data.
