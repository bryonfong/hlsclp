Corpus synthesis of this source: The court excluded Professor Hancock’s declaration from the preliminary-injunction record because AI-generated fake citations destroyed the reliability and credibility required of sworn expert testimony. It distinguished permissible AI-assisted research from unverified reliance, suggested that attorneys’ reasonable inquiry may now include asking witnesses about AI use and verification, and identified institutional and reputational harms from fabricated sources. The court separately admitted Professor West’s general background testimony under the more flexible evidentiary standards applicable at the preliminary-injunction stage.
