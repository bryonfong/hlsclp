Corpus framing of this source: Judges and the Deregulation of the Lawyer’s Monopoly

Corpus synthesis of this source: Steinberg, Carpenter, Shanahan and Mark use a two-year, cross-jurisdictional study of domestic-violence protective-order courts to identify trial judges’ largely hidden reliance on nonlawyer advocates as de facto deregulation. They argue that this collaboration improves access and court administration but lacks transparent authorization and creates serious due-process disparities for defendants. They recommend formal recognition, balanced expansion and bottom-up regulatory design informed by judges and existing advocates.
