Corpus framing of this source: EPIC reports rising malpractice claims, active AI exposure and insurance cost pressure

Corpus synthesis of this source: EPIC’s 2026 Lawyers’ Professional Liability Claims Survey reports insurer experience of increasing claim frequency, severity and defense costs. Survey author Eileen Garczynski links these pressures to economic conditions, complex work and emerging risks, while stressing firms’ continuing responsibility for competence and risk controls.

Corpus source-specific finding

Insurer reports indicate renewed growth in malpractice claim frequency and continued growth in severity.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey reflects the experience of senior claims executives at thirteen leading Lawyers' Professional Liability insurers that collectively provide insurance to more than 80% of the Am Law 200 firms.

Exact returned source excerpt

"After five years of relative stability, we are seeing a clear inflection point," said **Eileen Garczynski, Esq., a principal of EPIC Law Firm Group and author of the survey**.
