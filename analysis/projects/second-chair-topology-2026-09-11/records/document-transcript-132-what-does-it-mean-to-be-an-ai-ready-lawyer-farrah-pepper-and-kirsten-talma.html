Corpus framing of this source: An adaptable AI-fluency framework and experimental tools for legal education

Corpus synthesis of this source: Farrah Pepper and Kirsten Talmage present a cross-setting competency framework intended to give the legal profession shared language for AI fluency. They describe its non-linear structure, compare its standardising role with the EDRM model, outline experimental assessment and certification possibilities, and report early use of agentic systems to accelerate PLI program development. The speakers and hosts emphasise that rapid technological change requires adaptable training processes and continued stakeholder feedback.

Corpus source-specific finding

Exact returned source excerpt
