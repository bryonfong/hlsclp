SupportsWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: 2023 Year-End Report on the Federal Judiciary: AI will transform but not replace human judging

Corpus synthesis of this source: The report places AI within the federal judiciary’s long history of technological adaptation. It argues that AI can improve efficiency, information access and access to justice, but cannot replace judges where adjudication requires discretion, contextual perception or development of the law. It predicts substantial changes to judicial work, especially at trial level, and calls for cautious, institutionally coordinated consideration of AI’s proper uses in light of hallucinations, confidentiality, due-process, reliability, bias, privacy and dehumanisation risks.
