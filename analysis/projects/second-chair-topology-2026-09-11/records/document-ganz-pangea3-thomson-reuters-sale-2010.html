# Exclusive: Payday for Pangea3 founders, staff in Thomson Reuters buy; LPO to build onshore centres in US *Kian Ganz — Legally India (2010)* Only one week after US-Indian LPO [UnitedLex bought California-headquartered LawScribe](https://www.legallyindia.com/201011161510/Legal-Process-Outsourcing-LPO/lpo-unitedlex-buys-smaller-rival-lawscribe-after-growing-20-in-8-months), Pangea3 at 3:30 am Indian time this morning closed its [sale to Thomson Reuters](https://www.legallyindia.com/201011181531/Legal-Process-Outsourcing-LPO/breaking-thomson-reuters-to-acquire-lpo-pangea3-for-up-to-40m-strategic-buy), a company which provides data, information and news services but is a new entrant to the LPO game.

Exact returned source excerpt

Former OfficeTiger CFO and GC Kamlani, who founded Pangea3 together with former Monster.com GC David Perla, told *Legally India* that Thomson Reuters had acquired 100 per cent of Pangea3’s shares.

Corpus source-specific finding

The reported $35–40 million purchase price remained unconfirmed.

Exact returned source excerpt

Kamlani, Perla, managment and employees jointly held around the other half of the LPO’s equity before yesterday’s sale but Kamlani declined to comment on *Business Standard*’s cited [transaction value figure of $35 to $40m](https://www.legallyindia.com/201011181531/Legal-Process-Outsourcing-LPO/breaking-thomson-reuters-to-acquire-lpo-pangea3-for-up-to-40m-strategic-buy).

Exact returned source excerpt

One rival in the LPO sector commented that he thought that Thomson Reuters probably picked up the LPO for less.

Corpus source-specific finding

Kamlani presented the sale as a strategic partnership, not simply a capital raise.

Exact returned source excerpt

Recounting the path to the acquisition, Kamlani said: “We set out to raise capital but concluded that we needed to raise capital and have a strategic partner, and this includes things like building an onshore presence in the US.” Pangea3 currently has a US presence for BD and marketing purposes, but the company would now start building US-based onshore delivery centres in the US to mirror the Indian LPO centres, although they had not yet decided on location, headcounts and financials of such an operation, which Kamlani conceded would have lower margins that LPO in India.

Exact returned source excerpt

“There’s been a lot of interest that has come from both private equity and others in the market but it was very clear to us that Thomson Reuters was far and away a great fit for our organisation culturally and in terms of business – the Thomson Legal business, everything they have in their business, is synergistic with our portfolio,” said Kamlani.
