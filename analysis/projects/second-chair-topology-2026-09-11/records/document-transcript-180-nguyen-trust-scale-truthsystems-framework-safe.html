Corpus framing of this source: Nam Nguyen on embedding adaptive AI governance across legal workflows

Corpus synthesis of this source: Nguyen argues that law firms should govern AI through a platform-agnostic, workflow-level layer rather than static policies or controls confined to individual products. He presents TruthSystems as a governance, risk and compliance platform that uses AI to monitor conduct, enforce tool-specific guardrails and surface risks in real time. He also explains why retrieval-augmented generation cannot eliminate hallucinations, advocates verification and predictable models over confident output, and predicts that governance data will become a source of client trust and competitive differentiation. He qualifies his technological optimism by noting uncertainty over scaling laws and whether large language models can ultimately solve current reliability problems.
