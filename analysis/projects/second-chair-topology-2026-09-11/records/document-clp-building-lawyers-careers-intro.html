Corpus framing of this source: Lawyers’ careers: enduring inequality, individual agency and professional fulfilment

Corpus synthesis of this source: Introducing the July 2024 Issue presents The Making of Lawyers’ Careers and the After the JD research as a challenge to assumptions that legal careers reward merit alone or generally produce dissatisfaction. It organises the discussion around career structures, strategies and inequality, distinguishing the book’s findings from interviewees’ interpretations and advice.

The book reexamines and questions accepted truths in the mythos around lawyers, including the idea that the law is a meritocracy where skill and acumen are the only necessities on the path toward partnership and the impression that lawyers largely lead dissatisfactory lives.
