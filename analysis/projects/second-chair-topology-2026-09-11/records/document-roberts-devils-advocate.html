Corpus framing of this source: Digital dissent as a route to more authentic strategic debate

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anthea Roberts argues that hierarchy and emerging consensus suppress privately held doubts, while assigned human devil’s advocates often produce tokenistic criticism and impose reputational costs. Drawing on recurring observations across organisational settings and a failed digital-transformation example, she proposes that an AI devil’s advocate could surface concerns without exposing a human dissenter—a possibility she calls “laundered dissent” or “authenticity inversion.” She presents this as a qualified possibility rather than an established outcome.
