Corpus framing of this source: A Safe Harbor for AI Evaluation and Red Teaming

Corpus synthesis of this source: The ICML 2024 position paper argues that independent evaluation of deployed generative-AI systems faces legal, technical and institutional barriers. Drawing on the authors’ experience, policy reviews, reported account suspensions and prior research, it proposes paired voluntary legal and technical safe harbors, independent access review and reciprocal researcher duties, while identifying legal limits and unresolved design trade-offs.
