Corpus framing of this source: Belief calibration predicts who benefits from AI assistance

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper develops and experimentally tests a causal account of heterogeneous gains from AI assistance. It argues that baseline ability alone does not explain those gains: people with better-calibrated beliefs gain more, while people with lower accuracy or IQ also tend to gain more. It distinguishes this calibration mechanism from prior evidence that AI raises average productivity and narrows performance disparities, examines confidence-related reliance errors, and reports several robustness checks. The supplied material contains no explicit recommendation.

Corpus source-specific finding

The paper predicts that calibration independently increases the benefit from AI assistance.

Our main prediction is that conditional on their individual ability Ai , individuals who are more calibrated will benefit more from working with AI in terms of their AI-augmented ability Āi .
