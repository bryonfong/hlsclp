Corpus framing of this source: FTC scrutiny and constitutional challenge to the KBA’s LawTalk ban

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source reports parallel competition-law and constitutional challenges to the Korean Bar Association’s restrictions on lawyers using LawTalk. It explains the relevant Fair Trade Act prohibitions, outlines the potential FTC process, predicts a slow ruling based on precedent, and separately records the KBA’s stated rationale and proposed free alternative.
