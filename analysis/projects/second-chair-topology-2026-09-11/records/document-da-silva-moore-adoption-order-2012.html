Corpus framing of this source: Da Silva Moore v. Publicis Groupe: Adoption of Predictive-Coding Discovery Rulings

Corpus synthesis of this source: The district court upheld Judge Peck’s predictive-coding ESI protocol under the deferential standard governing nondispositive discovery rulings. It treated the plaintiffs’ reliability objections as premature, relied on the protocol’s testing, participation and reconsideration safeguards, compared predictive coding with the costs and fallibility of manual keyword review, and preserved routes for later challenge if results proved deficient.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court adopted Judge Peck’s predictive-coding rulings and denied the plaintiffs’ objections.
