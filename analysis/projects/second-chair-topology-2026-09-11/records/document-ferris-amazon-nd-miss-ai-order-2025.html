Corpus synthesis of this source: In Ferris v. Amazon.com Services, LLC, the court distinguishes prudent AI use from careless fabrication, explains the disproportionate work required to check false legal submissions, and invokes truth-seeking and Rule 11 obligations, including for self-represented litigants. The supplied excerpt presents judicial reasoning, not quantified evidence of AI errors or a stated sanctions outcome.

Exact returned source excerpt
