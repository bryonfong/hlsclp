Corpus synthesis of this source: Amanda Robert’s ABA Journal report recounts Neal Katyal’s claims about Harvey AI’s role in preparing his tariffs argument and the criticism they prompted. Drawing on Bloomberg Law, it distinguishes potential value in predicting judicial questions from disputed claims that such preparation determined the outcome; it does not independently establish AI’s causal contribution.
