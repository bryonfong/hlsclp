Corpus synthesis of this source: How the Billable Hour Can Survive Generative AI offers a qualified, preliminary challenge to predictions that AI efficiency necessarily undermines hourly billing. It combines billing history, institutional explanations, published market evidence and a firm-level financial framework to identify possible routes to survival, while acknowledging professional harms and uncertain demand effects.
