Corpus framing of this source: Jurimetrics: replacing speculative jurisprudence with investigation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Jurimetrics — The Next Step Forward traces challenges to natural-law and rule-centred accounts of adjudication, then argues that even modern jurisprudence remains speculative. The author's central recommendation is to study law through observation and scientific investigation rather than philosophical introspection. The supplied excerpts develop a historical and methodological argument, not an empirical demonstration.

Corpus framing of this source: Lee Loevinger’s empirical programme for jurimetrics

Corpus synthesis of this source: In “Jurimetrics—The Next Step Forward” (1949), Loevinger argues that law should adopt scientific investigation of how legal rules affect human behaviour. He presents jurimetrics as a difficult but necessary alternative to abstract jurisprudence and institutional cure-alls, illustrates its research agenda with negligence law, invokes historical resistance to empirical inquiry, and separately reports Legal Realist claims about legal uncertainty.

Corpus source-specific finding
