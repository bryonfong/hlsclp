Corpus synthesis of this source: This 2024 working paper compares LLMs with junior lawyers and legal process outsourcers in contract review. It reports near-leading issue-determination accuracy and substantial time and cost savings, while distinguishing those findings from weaker issue-location performance and broader predictions of industry disruption.
