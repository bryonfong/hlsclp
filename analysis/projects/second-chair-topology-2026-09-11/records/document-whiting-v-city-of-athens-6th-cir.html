Corpus synthesis of this source: The Sixth Circuit treats fabricated authorities, unsupported quotations and material record misrepresentations as sanctionable appellate misconduct. It grounds sanctions in Rule 38 and inherent authority, stresses the resulting verification burden and damage to institutional trust, rejects procedural objections, and distinguishes misconduct sanctions from punishment merely for losing a case.
