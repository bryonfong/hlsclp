Corpus framing of this source: Responsible by Design: court leadership framework for evaluating generative-AI tools

Corpus synthesis of this source: Angela Tripp, Margaret Hagan and Keith Porcaro present a management framework for courts evaluating internal and public-facing generative-AI tools, especially tools serving self-represented litigants. They focus primarily on reliability and accuracy while treating usability as a separate, necessary inquiry. They argue that leaders—not vendors or builders—must govern evaluation across initial approval, pre-launch refinement and post-launch maintenance. The webinar states that its views belong to the presenters rather than their institutions and that product references are illustrative, not endorsements.

Corpus source-specific finding

The framework organises evaluation into initial green-lighting, a pre-launch “hill climb” and continuous post-launch testing.

Exact returned source excerpt

There'll be an idea phase where you have to make as leaders the decision about whether a project should actually go forward, whether that's making a contract with a vendor and buying, whether that's green-lighting an internal team to build in-house.

Exact returned source excerpt

And so the next phase is what we call a hill climb, which is trying to scale that difference between the tool, the demo, the stress test thing that's pretty good, and something that is really ready for a wider array of your use cases.
