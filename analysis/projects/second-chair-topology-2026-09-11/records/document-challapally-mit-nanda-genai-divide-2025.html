Corpus framing of this source: The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025

Corpus synthesis of this source: A preliminary MIT Project NANDA report argues that widespread GenAI experimentation has produced little measurable enterprise-level return because most systems fail to learn from feedback, retain context or fit operational workflows. Drawing on public initiatives, interviews and surveys, it identifies implementation, procurement and organizational practices associated with deployment; documents uneven ROI and workforce effects; and predicts a shift toward adaptive, interoperable agents. It explicitly treats many results as directional, correlational and sample-dependent.
