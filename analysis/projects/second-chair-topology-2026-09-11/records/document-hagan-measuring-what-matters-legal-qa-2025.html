Corpus framing of this source: Measuring What Matters: Human-Centred Standards for Legal Q-and-A

Corpus synthesis of this source: Margaret Hagan develops a participatory framework for evaluating consumer-facing AI legal Q-and-A. Drawing on three mixed-methods studies with community members and frontline experts, the paper argues that legal accuracy and hallucination detection are necessary but insufficient. It identifies accurate, local, current, accessible and actionable guidance as central; treats AI chiefly as an initial navigator; documents qualifications concerning citations, referrals and disclaimers; and recommends embedding stakeholder knowledge in AI design, evaluation and regulation.
