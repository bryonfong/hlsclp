Clinical AI assistance: Standalone accuracy does not ensure better care

Corpus synthesis: AI support can fail to improve—and may worsen—joint clinical decisions when practitioners misjudge their own limits, model errors or case difficulty. The exemplars distinguish model accuracy and clinician performance from patient outcomes, effective delegation and workforce replacement. They favour selective task allocation, workflow experiments and local testing, including on difficult and demographic cases, over routine deployment based on headline performance.
