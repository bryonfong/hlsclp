Corpus framing of this source: NIST’s guidance for valid, purpose-specific automated language-model benchmarking

Corpus synthesis of this source: NIST AI 800-2 ipd offers methodological guidance rather than model-performance findings. Drawing on CAISI evaluation experience and measurement research, it explains how evaluators should select and run existing benchmarks, limit their interpretations, and support reproducibility. Its central qualification is that automated benchmark scores cannot meet every evaluation objective or directly establish performance in human-interactive, real-world settings.

Corpus framing of this source: NIST practices for valid, reproducible automated language-model benchmark evaluations

Corpus synthesis of this source: NIST offers a preliminary, evolving set of voluntary practices for designing, running, analysing and communicating automated benchmark evaluations of language models and agents. It centres measurement validity, protocol realism, uncertainty, reproducibility and qualified interpretation, while identifying emerging practices and limits on when automated benchmarks are appropriate.

Corpus source-specific finding
