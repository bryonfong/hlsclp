Corpus framing of this source: Legal AI needs a Lawyer Analysis Metric (LAM)

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source proposes a task-specific metric for determining sufficient human verification of AI-generated legal work. It frames LAM as a practical middle ground between reviewing everything and reviewing nothing, calibrates review intensity to risk and judgment, predicts that required review should decline as AI improves, and calls for continued measurement and recalibration.
