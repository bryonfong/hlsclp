Corpus framing of this source: LIST as shared infrastructure for legal issue recognition and access to justice

Corpus synthesis of this source: Margaret Hagan presents the Legal Issues Taxonomy (LIST) as a comprehensive, machine-readable common language for civil legal problems. The paper links the taxonomy to the justice gap, explains its expert- and data-informed design, situates it alongside SALI and local schemes, and calls for crosswalks, classifier benchmarks and sustained taxonomy maintenance.
