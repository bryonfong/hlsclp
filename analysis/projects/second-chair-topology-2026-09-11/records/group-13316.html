Employer workforce planning: Skills and organisational capacity mediate AI’s employment effects

Corpus synthesis: The evidence frames AI-related labour change as an organisational transition combining task automation with augmentation, training, recruitment, retention and redeployment, rather than as a uniform reduction in lawyers. Outcomes vary by sector, occupation and geography because talent supply, implementation capacity and unequal access to reskilling shape what employers can achieve. Many exemplars report plans or forecasts rather than realised employment effects, and some caution that training and inclusion initiatives may not reach every worker.
