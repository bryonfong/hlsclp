Legal-work exposure measures: Rankings and task maps do not forecast displacement

Corpus synthesis: Measures of computerisation probability, occupational susceptibility, AI usage and task composition capture different technologies and concepts, so they can rank legal work differently and resist direct comparison. Task inventories describe what legal workers do but do not by themselves establish technical capability, adoption, autonomous performance or staffing effects.
