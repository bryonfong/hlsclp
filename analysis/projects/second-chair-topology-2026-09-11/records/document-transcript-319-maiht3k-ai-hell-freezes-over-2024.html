Corpus synthesis of this source: Emily M. Bender and Alex Hanna survey reported AI incidents across research, journalism, healthcare, law, government, labour, privacy, transport and warfare. They argue that fluent generation does not establish understanding or intelligence; criticise weak construct validity and hype-driven reporting; emphasise verification, disclosure, consent and human responsibility in consequential uses; and distinguish useful aggregate statistical analysis from claims to predict individual lives.
