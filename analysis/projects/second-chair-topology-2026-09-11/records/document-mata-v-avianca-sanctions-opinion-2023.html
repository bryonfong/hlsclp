Corpus framing of this source: Mata v. Avianca: Attorney Gatekeeping and Sanctions for AI-Fabricated Authorities

Corpus synthesis of this source: Judge P. Kevin Castel’s sanctions opinion holds that lawyers may use reliable AI assistance but remain responsible for verifying court filings. The court found that the respondents submitted fabricated ChatGPT-generated opinions, failed to correct them after receiving warnings, made misleading or contradictory representations, and acted with subjective bad faith. It imposed Rule 11 sanctions on the individual lawyers and their firm while tailoring the remedy to deterrence and the case-specific mitigating circumstances.
