Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: GDPval: real-world evaluation of frontier AI on economically valuable professional tasks

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source introduces GDPval, a benchmark of multi-format, long-horizon professional tasks selected for economic significance and evaluated against expert human deliverables. It reports that frontier models are approaching—but generally have not reached—expert parity, with performance varying materially by model, format, occupation, sector, task duration and prompt context. It also analyses model failure modes, potential workflow savings, reasoning and scaffolding effects, and the limits of automated grading.

Corpus source-specific finding

GDPval targets economically significant, realistic professional work rather than conventional text-only benchmark questions.
