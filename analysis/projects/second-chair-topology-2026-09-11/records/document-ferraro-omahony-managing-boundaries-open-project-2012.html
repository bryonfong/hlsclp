Corpus framing of this source: Managing the Boundaries of an 'Open' Project

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source uses qualitative field evidence and social-network analysis of Debian to explain how an open, distributed knowledge community built membership controls in response to security, trust and mission threats. It argues that incumbent network position shapes who writes boundary rules, while those rules subsequently reshape the network through gatekeeping and preferential attachment. It also identifies tensions between protection and openness, qualifies how outcomes should be measured, and recommends comparative research on trust mechanisms, crises and community scaling.
