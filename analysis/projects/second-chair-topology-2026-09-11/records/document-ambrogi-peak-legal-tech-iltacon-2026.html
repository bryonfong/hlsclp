Corpus framing of this source: ILTACON as evidence of legal tech’s expansion, volatility and unequal gains

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bob Ambrogi uses ILTACON 2026 as a snapshot of a legal-tech market at unprecedented scale and intensity. He adopts a CEO’s “peak legal tech” framing for the industry’s current energy, but predicts continued overall growth accompanied by correction, consolidation and vendor churn rather than an imminent bubble burst. He also highlights rapid integration, unequal purchasing capacity among law firms and a funding imbalance between enterprise legal technology and access-to-justice technology.

Corpus source-specific finding

Ambrogi treats ILTACON’s record scale and international reach as evidence that legal tech has reached a new high-water mark.
