AI performance evidence: gains depend on verification behaviour and statistical assumptions

Corpus synthesis: Formal and statistical models show that stronger AI assistance or additional observations do not necessarily improve joint output or stabilize model rankings. Human effort may adjust as assistance improves, while metric design, weighting, support probabilities and uncertainty bounds can change comparative results. These exemplars therefore qualify performance claims theoretically and methodologically rather than establishing empirical productivity effects.
