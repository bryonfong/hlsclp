Corpus framing of this source: Ken Crutchfield on legal AI’s incremental, fragmented and adoption-intensive transition

Corpus synthesis of this source: Crutchfield frames legal AI as a fast-moving industrial transition rather than an established winner-take-all market. He sees useful but mostly incremental automation, difficult economics under the billable hour, substantial integration and adoption demands, and unresolved competition between proprietary legal information and emerging “good enough” alternatives. He recommends durable data work, selective pilots, sustained implementation support and simple scenario planning while anticipating continued vendor expansion, strong incumbent positions and more data-related litigation.
