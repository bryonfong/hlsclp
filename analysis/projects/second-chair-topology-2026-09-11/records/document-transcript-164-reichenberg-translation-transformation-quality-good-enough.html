Corpus framing of this source: Paula Reichenberg on AI’s legal “last mile”: data, process, verification and differentiated quality

Corpus synthesis of this source: Reichenberg draws on legal translation and product-building experience to argue that impressive AI output remains imperfect, particularly across Europe’s fragmented legal and linguistic systems. She presents the central opportunity as helping professionals detect and correct errors efficiently through specialised data, workflows and multidisciplinary teams—not promising autonomous perfection. She also identifies uneven user behaviour and misplaced trust as adoption risks, while expressing qualified optimism that imperfect language technology can improve access to justice where human services are severely underfunded.
