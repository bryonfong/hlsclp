Corpus synthesis of this source: Sir Geoffrey Vos predicts that AI will become a decision-maker in some disputes, while distinguishing consensual arbitration from constitutionally protected court adjudication. His argument combines anticipated cost and speed advantages, reported examples and professional experience with qualifications about model readiness, legal safeguards, global inequality and human acceptance.
