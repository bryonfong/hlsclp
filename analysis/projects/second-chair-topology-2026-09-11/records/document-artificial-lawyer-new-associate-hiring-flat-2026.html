Open topic: Associate training: Automation threatens expertise reproduction

Corpus framing of this source: New Associate Hiring is Flat — Is AI The Cause?

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents flat entry-level associate hiring as a potentially AI-influenced but multi-causal trend. It reasons that law-firm and client adoption may remove junior work from both sides of the market, while emphasising that the pattern predates widespread legal AI. It proposes additional commercial and in-house factors, predicts stronger demand for laterals, recommends faster junior development, and cautions that the trend may reverse.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI may now be contributing to flat junior hiring through a two-sided reduction in outsourced entry-level work.
