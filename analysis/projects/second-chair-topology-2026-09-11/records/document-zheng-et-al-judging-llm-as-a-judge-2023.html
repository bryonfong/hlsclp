Corpus framing of this source: LLM-as-a-Judge for Scalable Chatbot Evaluation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper proposes and tests LLM-based judging through MT-bench and Chatbot Arena. It finds that strong models can approximate human preferences at scale, while documenting position bias, prompt sensitivity, judge failures, benchmark trade-offs and important scope limitations. It recommends combining human-preference and standardized benchmarks and pursuing broader, safer and more robust evaluation methods.
