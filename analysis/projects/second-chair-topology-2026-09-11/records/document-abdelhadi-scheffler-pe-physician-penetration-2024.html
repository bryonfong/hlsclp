Corpus framing of this source: Private equity’s expanding footprint and local concentration in physician practices, 2012–21

Corpus synthesis of this source: Abdelhadi and colleagues estimate private equity firms’ local physician-market shares across ten specialties. Their 2024 Health Affairs study documents substantial growth in acquired practice sites and high single-firm shares in some local specialty markets, prompting a call for regulatory scrutiny.

Corpus source-specific finding

The study addresses a gap in measuring private equity’s local market control.

Exact returned source excerpt

3 (2024): 354–362 (2024)* ## Abstract (published, verbatim and complete) Private equity (PE) firms have been acquiring physician practices at an increasing rate, raising concerns about such firms' penetration at the physician level into local markets and the impact on health care quality and prices.

Exact returned source excerpt

However, limited knowledge exists about the extent of PE firms' control in local markets.

Corpus source-specific finding

PE-acquired practice sites and their geographic reach expanded substantially.

Exact returned source excerpt

PE-acquired physician practice sites increased from 816 across 119 MSAs in 2012 to 5,779 across 307 MSAs in 2021.

Corpus source-specific finding

Individual PE firms held large shares of some local specialty markets.

Exact returned source excerpt

Single PE firms had significant market share, exceeding 30 percent in 108 MSA specialty markets and exceeding 50 percent in 50 of those markets.

Corpus source-specific finding

The authors recommend closer regulatory scrutiny on competition grounds.

Exact returned source excerpt

The findings raise concerns about competition and call for closer scrutiny by the Federal Trade Commission, state regulators, and policy makers.
