Corpus framing of this source: Applicant experience of the ABS authorisation process

Corpus synthesis of this source: Official research reports respondent firms’ views of regulatory staff, application handling, and supporting information and guidance. It finds strong ratings for staff professionalism alongside concerns about bureaucracy, delays, explanations and online resources. It records demand for more face-to-face engagement but makes no explicit prediction.

Corpus source-specific finding

Respondents generally rated staff as professional and effective communicators, while many successful applicants also found them bureaucratic.

Exact returned source excerpt

 93 percent of respondents 'strongly agreed' or 'agreed' that our staff were 'professional to deal with'  Over half of respondents 'strongly agreed' or 'agreed' that our staff communicated effectively with them  51 percent of successful applicants indicated that our staff were bureaucratic to deal with.

Corpus source-specific finding

Successful and withdrawn applicants reported substantial dissatisfaction with timeliness and the handling of delays.

Exact returned source excerpt

With regard to application handling, just over half (54 percent) of successful applicants 'disagreed' or 'strongly disagreed' with the view that their application was handled in a timely manner.

Exact returned source excerpt

Where delays were encountered, 42 percent of successful applicants, and two thirds of withdrawn firms, felt that reasons for the delays were not adequately explained or remedied.

Corpus source-specific finding

Respondents raised concerns about the clarity of online resources and the justification for information requests.

Exact returned source excerpt

39 percent of respondents 'disagreed' or 'strongly disagreed' that our website and online resources were clear and useful.

Exact returned source excerpt

43 percent 'disagreed' or 'strongly disagreed' that adequate reasons were provided for the request of particular information and evidence.

Corpus source-specific finding

Some respondents wanted more face-to-face engagement.

Exact returned source excerpt

This viewpoint was supported by a number of respondents requesting more access to face-to-face engagement.
