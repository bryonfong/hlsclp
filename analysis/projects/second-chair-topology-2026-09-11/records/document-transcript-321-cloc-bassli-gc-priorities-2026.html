Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Lucy Bassli on AI adoption, governance and leadership in corporate legal departments

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bassli argues that AI has become a practical, enterprise-wide priority for general counsel, layered over longstanding concerns about legal spend and contracting. Drawing principally on her work with corporate law departments and reported implementation experience, she identifies a strategy gap, pressure from both executives and employees, and a need to balance access with governance. She recommends controlled experimentation, visible GC participation and legal-operations-led change management, while predicting leadership and career consequences from how departments manage the transition.
