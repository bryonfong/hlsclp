Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Future of Jobs Report 2025: skills constraints and workforce adaptation to 2030

Corpus synthesis of this source: The World Economic Forum reports employers’ 2024 expectations for workforce transformation through 2030. Its contribution combines global survey rankings with industry, economy and regional profiles: employers anticipate more technology-delivered work while prioritising training, recruitment and better employment practices. These figures describe perceptions and plans, not demonstrated intervention effects or realised job losses. Many supplied profile excerpts omit geographic names or interleave chart columns, limiting precise attribution.
