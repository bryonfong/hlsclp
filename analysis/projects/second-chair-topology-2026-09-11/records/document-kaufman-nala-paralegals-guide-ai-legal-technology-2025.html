Corpus synthesis of this source: The guide argues that AI augments rather than replaces paralegals. It describes applications in research, contracts, litigation analytics, client intake, workflow and administration; recommends technical adaptation and a shift toward higher-value, human-centred work; and qualifies these benefits with concerns about bias, confidentiality, cybersecurity and the need for human oversight. It offers product examples and asserted capabilities, but supplies no empirical studies or measured outcomes.
