Corpus framing of this source: LSC appropriations: funding, grant restrictions and governance flexibility

Corpus synthesis of this source: LSC’s compilation documents enacted appropriations, competing congressional proposals and the continuing conditions attached to federally funded legal aid. It presents legislative evidence rather than an independent policy argument, and directs readers to Congress.gov for official laws. Its principal contribution is to distinguish funding decisions from proposals while showing how longstanding restrictions, competitive grants, oversight and board reforms shape legal-aid provision.
