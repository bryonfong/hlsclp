Corpus framing of this source: Matter of Weber: fiduciary liability and the factual reliability of expert damages evidence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied excerpts address a trust-accounting dispute over retention and personal use of Bahamian property. The court finds that the beneficiary failed to prove fiduciary misconduct or damages and identifies extensive weaknesses in his expert’s analysis. These excerpts do not set out an AI-evidence disclosure duty or Frye ruling.

The court applies a lay-trustee standard and places the burden of proving misconduct and resulting damages on the beneficiary.
