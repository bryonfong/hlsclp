Corpus framing of this source: Why specialist lawyers only partly offset repeat-player advantages

Corpus synthesis of this source: Galanter examines whether specialist lawyers can give one-shot clients (OSs) the advantages enjoyed by repeat players (RPs). Drawing on earlier studies and a speculative model of professional organisation, he argues that expertise helps but does not eliminate strategic inequality, weak client relationships or conflicting lawyer incentives.

Corpus framing of this source: Why the ‘Haves’ Come Out Ahead: Repeat-Player Advantage and the Limits of Legal Reform

Corpus synthesis of this source: Galanter develops a structural account of why repeat players tend to outperform one-shotters. He links party organization, legal services, institutional passivity and overload, rule formation, implementation, and alternative remedy systems, then argues that effective equalizing reform must change parties’ strategic capacity rather than rely on substantive rule change alone.
