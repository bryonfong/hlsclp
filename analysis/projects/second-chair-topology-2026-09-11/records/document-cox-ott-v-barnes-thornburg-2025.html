Corpus framing of this source: Cox-Ott: Professional judgment remains subject to reasonable care

Corpus synthesis of this source: In Cox-Ott v. Barnes & Thornburg, LLP, 321 Ga. 688, 915 S.E.2d 894 (2025), the Georgia Supreme Court reaffirms reasonable care as the governing malpractice standard. It preserves protection for reasonably exercised judgment while overruling precedent that treated honest professional judgment as sufficient without examining the care used to reach it.

Corpus framing of this source: Cox-Ott on reasonable care and errors of professional judgment

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Georgia Supreme Court traces the traditional legal-malpractice standard: attorneys retain discretion in uncertain matters and are not liable merely because a reasonably made professional judgment proves mistaken. The excerpt says Georgia appellate decisions reaffirmed this approach, while noting—but not explaining—that the doctrine later began to shift after Hudson.
