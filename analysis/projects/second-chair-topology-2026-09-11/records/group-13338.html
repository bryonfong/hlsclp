Job quality and progression: Employment growth can coexist with occupational hollowing

Corpus synthesis: Rising employment does not necessarily produce secure, productive work or viable career progression; it may conceal skills mismatch, weak mobility and unequal access to advancement. The exemplars vary between diagnosing this hollowing and identifying procurement, funding, wage and skills policies that might improve work quality, while noting that many proposed benefits remain prospective.
