Corpus framing of this source: Legalbenchmarks.ai on comparing legal AI, general models and lawyers

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anna Guo presents imperfect but practical benchmarking as a first step toward tracking the changing performance gap between lawyers and AI. Her studies assess reliability, usability and platform support rather than accuracy alone. Within the tested sample, selected general-purpose assistants led overall performance, legal-specific tools offered distinctive qualitative and risk-spotting strengths, and AI-assisted lawyers improved on unaided lawyers but did not beat the top standalone model. She repeatedly limits these findings to the tested tools, lawyers and tasks.

Corpus source-specific finding

Guo argues that legal-AI benchmarking should measure the human–AI performance gap now and track it as technology develops.
