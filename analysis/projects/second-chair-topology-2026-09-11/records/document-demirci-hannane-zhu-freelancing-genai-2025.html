The Impact of Generative AI on Online Freelancing Platforms *Ozge Demirci, Jonas Hannane, Xinrong Zhu — Management Science 71(10), 8097–8108 (2025)* ## Published abstract (complete, via Crossref) > This paper studies the impact of generative artificial intelligence (AI) technologies on the demand for online freelancers using a large data set from a leading global freelancing platform.

Exact returned source excerpt

Our findings indicate a 21% decrease in the number of job posts for automation-prone jobs related to writing and coding compared with jobs requiring manual-intensive skills within eight months after the introduction of ChatGPT.

Corpus framing of this source: Generative AI reduced demand and reshaped work on an online freelancing platform

Corpus synthesis of this source: Demirci, Hannane and Zhu provide empirical evidence on heterogeneous changes in freelance demand after the introduction of generative AI. Using platform data and Google Trends, they report declines in writing, coding and image-creation postings, alongside greater freelancer competition and a shift in remaining automation-prone work toward higher complexity and pay. The abstract offers no explicit predictions or recommendations.
