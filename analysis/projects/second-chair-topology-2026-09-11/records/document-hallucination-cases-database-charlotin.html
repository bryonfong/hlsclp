Corpus framing of this source: Court-documented AI hallucinations: scope, consequences and verification failures

Corpus synthesis of this source: Damien Charlotin’s AI Hallucination Cases Database compiles judicial treatment of AI-generated false material. The supplied 2026 snapshot reports 1,174 cases and illustrates procedural, financial and professional consequences across jurisdictions. Its contribution is a qualified case inventory, not a measurement of model error rates or the prevalence of AI misuse.
