Corpus framing of this source: State v. Puloka — Exclusion of AI-Enhanced Video Evidence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The court treated AI video enhancement as a novel forensic technique and excluded Topaz Video AI-enhanced exhibits under Frye and evidentiary rules. It relied principally on credited forensic testimony that the process generated and removed image information, lacked reproducibility and acceptance in the forensic video analysis community, and risked misleading the jury. The ruling remains case- and record-specific, repeatedly qualifying the technology’s status as current rather than permanent.
