Corpus framing of this source: Utah tightens sandbox access-to-justice requirements; at least 27 entities may leave

Corpus synthesis of this source: Debra Cassens Weiss’s March 4, 2025 ABA Journal report describes possible departures from Utah’s regulatory sandbox following tighter participation rules. Drawing on Reuters, she reports prospective exits rather than confirmed departures and explains the new requirement to demonstrate substantial reach among underserved Utah consumers.

Corpus source-specific finding

At least 27 law firms and businesses may leave Utah’s regulatory sandbox after regulations tightened.

Exact returned source excerpt

(Image from Shutterstock)* At least 27 law firms and businesses may be leaving a Utah program that permits nontraditional legal services providers to operate in the state, including firms operated by nonlawyers, according to a published report.

Exact returned source excerpt

The exodus follows a tightening of regulations governing the program, [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/legalindustry/utah-rethinks-legal-industry-reforms-arizona-speeds-ahead-2025-03-03) reports.

Corpus source-specific finding

Participants must demonstrate substantial impact on underserved Utah consumers relative to their overall reach.

Exact returned source excerpt

Under the [new rules](https://utahinnovationoffice.org/info-for-interested-applicants), program participants [must show](https://www.abajournal.com/web/article/new-rules-issued-for-utahs-regulatory-sandbox) that they will reach Utah consumers currently underserved by the legal market, and that the impact is substantial compared to an entity’s overall reach.
