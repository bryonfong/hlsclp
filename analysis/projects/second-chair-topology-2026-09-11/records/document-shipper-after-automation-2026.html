Corpus framing of this source: Dan Shipper: AI automates framed tasks while expanding expert human work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Drawing on Every’s intensive AI use, benchmark results, a security case study and a conceptual distinction between a task’s frame and the human framer, Shipper argues that AI transforms and expands expert knowledge work rather than simply eliminating it. He expects human judgment to remain structurally ahead by defining goals and deciding what matters, including under AGI, but limits his direct empirical claim to expert-level knowledge work and acknowledges that sufficiently capable AGI could theoretically threaten all jobs.

Corpus source-specific finding
