Lawyer liability: Error or ethical breach does not itself establish malpractice

Corpus synthesis: Civil malpractice requires deficient care, causation and substantiated loss; an error, adverse outcome or corrective expense alone may not suffice. Ethical rules can inform the standard of care, but professional discipline and civil liability remain distinct accountability mechanisms.
