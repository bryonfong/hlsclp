Corpus synthesis of this source: The Future of Employment: How Susceptible Are Jobs to Computerisation? extends the task model to explain potential automation of non-routine work. The authors ground their argument in advances in machine learning and mobile robotics, distinguish technological possibilities from remaining engineering constraints, and predict labour reallocation as computing improves and becomes cheaper.
