Corpus synthesis of this source: The Hourglass Economy: An analysis of London's labour market argues that headline employment growth masks insecurity, unequal pay and weak career progression. It combines labour-market findings with first-hand accounts and recommends that the Mayor and London Enterprise Panel prioritise job quality, flexible hiring, skills, the London Living Wage and greater local control.
