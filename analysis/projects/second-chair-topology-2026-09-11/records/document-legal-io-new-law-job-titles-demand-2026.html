Corpus framing of this source: New-Law Job Titles Are Outpacing the Market

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that legal hiring terminology is diversifying, but the roles represented by newer titles remain unevenly developed and collectively modest in hiring volume. It treats legal operations as established, legal engineering as an early but notable signal, and cautions that small samples and database expansion make matched first-half comparisons more reliable than raw multi-year growth.
