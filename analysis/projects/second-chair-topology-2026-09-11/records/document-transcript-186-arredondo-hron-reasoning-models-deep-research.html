Corpus framing of this source: Arredondo and Hron on reasoning models, controlled agency and legal-AI verification

Corpus synthesis of this source: Pablo Arredondo and Joel Hron argue in their personal capacities that reasoning models and agents can substantially expand litigation analysis, research and error prevention, but require domain-specific tools, transparent verification workflows and use-case-specific control. Hron predicts that AI will automate routine work while increasing the importance of expert judgment and changing team design. Arredondo is highly optimistic about litigation technology but qualifies that optimism with concerns about model opacity, human overreliance, the loss of law’s human dimensions and AI-drafted binding precedent.
