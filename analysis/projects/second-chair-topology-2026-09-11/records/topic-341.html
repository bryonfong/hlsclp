Native topic findings

  1. The cluster frames AI as part of buyer-side operating-model redesign, not simply a technology purchase or a headcount-cutting tool.
  2. Resource pressure motivates transformation, but the evidence does not show uniform budget or staffing contraction.
  3. AI engagement is rising, but adoption figures do not establish deployment depth, integration or scalable performance.
  4. More selective purchasing can coexist with continued reliance on outside counsel; the sources do not establish wholesale substitution or the end of hourly billing.
  5. Strategic status and time savings remain distinct from demonstrable business value.
  6. The practical prescription is to build operational capability and use benchmarks selectively, with clear limits on what the evidence proves.
