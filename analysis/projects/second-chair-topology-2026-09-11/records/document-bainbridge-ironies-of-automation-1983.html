Builds onWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Ironies of Automation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Lisanne Bainbridge argues that automating industrial control does not remove the human role; it can leave operators with infrequent but unusually demanding monitoring, diagnosis and recovery tasks while eroding the skills and process knowledge those tasks require. She draws on human-factors studies, distinguishes her conclusions from proposals reported from other researchers, and recommends designing automation, training, alarms, feedback and displays as an integrated human–computer system.
