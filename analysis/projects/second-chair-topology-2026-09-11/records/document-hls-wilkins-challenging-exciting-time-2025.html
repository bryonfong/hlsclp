Open topic: Law-firm pyramid: Efficiency threatens revenue and training

Corpus framing of this source: Wilkins: AI may reduce junior hiring while changing skills demand and generating legal work

Corpus synthesis of this source: In an April 2025 Harvard Law Today interview, David Wilkins anticipates faster automation but rejects a simple narrative of machines replacing lawyers. He distinguishes potential reductions in entry-level hiring from overall employment, stresses technology-enabled problem-solving and educational preparation, and identifies government cuts and new litigation as additional forces shaping demand. His contribution offers qualitative reasoning and predictions rather than quantified employment estimates.
