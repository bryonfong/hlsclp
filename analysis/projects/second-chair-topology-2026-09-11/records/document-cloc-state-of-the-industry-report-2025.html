Open topic: Legal departments: AI supports selective sourcing redesign

Corpus framing of this source: CLOC benchmarks rising legal demand, AI adoption and legal operations priorities

Corpus synthesis of this source: The 2025 CLOC State of the Industry Report uses the 2024 Harbor survey to describe corporate legal departments’ demand, spending, staffing, technology and provider management. It presents respondent-reported practices and intentions, rather than evidence that AI has delivered productivity gains, and positions its benchmarks as a resource for planning legal functions.

Corpus source-specific finding

The report benchmarks 186 organizations and encourages departments to use the findings for planning.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey contained responses from 186 organizations across more than 15 industries and 14 countries.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey, now in its 21st year, continues to expand its focus on global legal function management, including data from 200+ participating companies representing over 15 industries.

Corpus framing of this source: 2025 CLOC State of the Industry Report: Legal Operations Demand, Capacity, Technology and Maturity

Corpus synthesis of this source: CLOC and Harbor use a multinational law-department survey to describe rising demand, constrained internal capacity, accelerating AI adoption, spending and staffing patterns, legal-operations structures, and departmental maturity. The report chiefly presents descriptive survey findings and near-term expectations rather than causal analysis, while qualifying some comparisons by changes in the respondent mix.

The report draws on a broad but selectively grouped survey sample.
