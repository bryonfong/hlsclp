Corpus synthesis of this source: The stipulation documents a lawyer’s unverified use of ChatGPT-generated case citations, his failure to disclose and remedy the problem promptly after discovering it, and his misleading explanation to the court. It applies the ABA sanction framework to distinguish negligent reliance on AI from knowing post-discovery misconduct, identifies resulting harms and aggravating circumstances, and explains why sanctions require an individualized assessment.
