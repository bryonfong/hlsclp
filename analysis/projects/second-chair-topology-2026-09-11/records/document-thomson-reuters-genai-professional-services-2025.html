Open topic: Legal workflows: Immediate gains create competence risks

Corpus framing of this source: 2025 Generative AI in Professional Services Report

Corpus synthesis of this source: Thomson Reuters Institute uses a January–February 2025 survey of 1,702 GenAI-familiar professionals across legal, tax, risk and government markets to document accelerating adoption, increasingly positive sentiment and expectations of workflow centrality. It argues that implementation remains strategically immature: organizations often lack ROI measurement, client transparency, policies, continuous training and hiring alignment. The report presents GenAI primarily as an augmentative productivity tool while qualifying that accuracy, verification, privacy, bias and human-oversight concerns remain unresolved and that long-term effects on jobs, revenue and professional structures are uncertain.
