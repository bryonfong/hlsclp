Corpus framing of this source: Joe Cohen on Dentons’ fleetAI: confidential GPT4 deployment and early uptake

Corpus synthesis of this source: Cohen describes fleetAI’s firm-wide rollout, attributes its development to executive support and technology partnerships, and reports strong staff and client interest. His account presents safety, performance and industry-first status as his own claims rather than independently demonstrated findings.

Corpus source-specific finding

Cohen presents fleetAI as a confidential way to use GPT4 for client work, with accuracy supported by guardrails and guidance.

Exact returned source excerpt

The platform essentially allows us to use GPT4 on our client work in a safe, confidential manner – both for chat and for document upload.

Exact returned source excerpt

The platform can analyse and generate over 7m words per minute and we’re really happy about levels of accuracy, pursuant to the various guardrails and (particularly) guidance we’ve put in place.

Corpus source-specific finding

He attributes the rapid rollout to executive backing and partnerships, while identifying privacy, confidentiality and retention as conditions for adoption.

Exact returned source excerpt

**What inspired you to do this?** I presented to our Executive Committee in February about ChatGPT and everyone was really on board with the potential of the underlying technology and what it could mean for law firms, if the various concerns (data privacy, confidentiality, and retention) could be solved.

Exact returned source excerpt

The project was really condensed in terms of the timeframes, and it was amazing to be able to have launched it firm-wide in September.

Corpus source-specific finding

He reports broad internal uptake and heightened enthusiasm, extending beyond lawyers.

Exact returned source excerpt

**How have people at the firm responded so far?** The tool was launched in September and is already used across the firm.

Exact returned source excerpt

I think most of our staff (the tool is available to everyone, not just lawyers) hadn’t really used ChatGPT on work-related queries before and when the firm started to actively encourage them to do so, the high quality of the responses definitely caused some shockwaves!

Corpus source-specific finding

He cites frequent client meetings as evidence of strong interest in AI.

Exact returned source excerpt

The client response has also been overwhelming – I’m having about 7-10 client meetings per week to discuss AI at the moment!
