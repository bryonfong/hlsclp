Corpus framing of this source: Connector capability checks and security review for legal AI integrations

Corpus synthesis of this source: Liam Barnes advises firms to confirm that a connector supports the required functions before adoption and to consider an MCP when native functionality is unavailable. For non-native connectors touching firm data, he outlines a six-part review framework, although the supplied material describes only the first four areas: data residency, credential storage, audit trails, and supply-chain and code review. He presents the discussion as practical guidance and qualifies his recollection of relevant ABA guidance.
