Corpus framing of this source: Wilkins on racial inequality, legal careers and institutional responses

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source reports David Wilkins’s account of persistent racial inequalities in legal careers and access to justice. He contrasts Black women’s representation among law graduates with their scarcity in senior roles, describes how events since 2020 sharpened racial divides and scrutiny, and links racial-justice initiatives to leadership opportunities created by Brown v. Board of Education.
