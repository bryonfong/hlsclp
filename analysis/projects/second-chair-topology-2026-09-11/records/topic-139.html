Native topic findings

  1. Employer expectations frame AI as a driver of workforce restructuring, not uniform job displacement.
  2. Lawyer forecasts vary by location, qualifying any claim of profession-wide expansion or contraction.
  3. The anticipated transition combines technical skills with human capabilities and internal workforce adaptation.
  4. AI adoption, skill disruption and labour-market churn measure different aspects of change.
  5. Sector differences and non-AI forces limit an AI-only account of the employment outlook.
