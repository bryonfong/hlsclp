Corpus framing of this source: NALA 2024 report: expanding paralegal roles, workplace conditions and compensation

Corpus synthesis of this source: NALA’s executive summary presents the paralegal profession as broadening its scope while confronting workplace pressures and demographic pay disparities. It reports national employment and compensation trends, predicts advantages for technologically adaptable paralegals, and recommends employer responses intended to improve retention, productivity and competitiveness.

Corpus source-specific finding

States are expanding paralegals’ permitted responsibilities.

Exact returned source excerpt

**Paraprofessional Model:** An increasing number of states are permitting paralegals to undertake responsibilities once reserved for attorneys, effectively addressing the expanding workload and complexity within the legal system.

Exact returned source excerpt

*Potential Impact:* Paralegals who adapt by embracing technological advancements and expanding their skill sets will be well-positioned to thrive in this dynamic environment.
