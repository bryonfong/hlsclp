Corpus framing of this source: Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction

Corpus synthesis of this source: Sunny Yu and colleagues report a preregistered behavioural study of how US adults estimate and experience completion time and effort on short cognitive tasks performed independently or with GPT-4o. They identify an asymmetric “speedup illusion”: participants expected AI to save substantial time and accurately estimated unaided completion times, but underestimated AI-assisted times even though AI did not reduce actual completion time. The authors argue that reduced subjective effort may make AI-assisted work feel faster, discuss possible overreliance and deskilling feedback loops, and call for broader productivity measures and further study across task complexity, incentives and within-subject behaviour.
