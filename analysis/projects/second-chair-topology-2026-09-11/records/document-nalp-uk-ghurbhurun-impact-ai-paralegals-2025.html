Corpus framing of this source: AI will reshape rather than eliminate paralegal work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Robin Ghurbhurun argues that AI will move paralegals away from routine data administration and towards business-led advice, contractual safeguards, prompt engineering, quality control and verification. He presents AI chiefly as a productivity-enhancing support system, while qualifying that its outputs depend on input quality and remain vulnerable to hallucinations. The introductory framing reports that some legal-tech experts foresee uncertainty for paralegals, but Ghurbhurun takes a more optimistic, augmentation-oriented position.
