Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied ABA Model Rule 1.1 comments offer professional guidance on continuing competence, relevant technology and shared representations. They identify considerations involving nonfirm lawyers and qualify responsibility allocations by noting that tribunal matters may carry additional legal obligations.

### Maintaining Competence [8] To maintain the requisite knowledge and skill, a lawyer should keep abreast of changes in the law and its practice, including the benefits and risks associated with relevant technology, engage in continuing study and education and comply with all continuing legal education requirements to which the lawyer is subject.

Corpus synthesis of this source: The ABA Model Rule and its official comments define competent representation and explain how lawyers can attain, maintain and allocate it.
