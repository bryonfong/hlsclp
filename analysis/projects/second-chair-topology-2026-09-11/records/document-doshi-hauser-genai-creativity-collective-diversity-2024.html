Corpus framing of this source: GenAI improves short-story creativity, especially for less creative writers

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source reports an online experiment in which access to GenAI ideas improved short stories’ novelty and usefulness. It attributes particular gains to access to multiple starting ideas and reports larger benefits for less creative writers. The supplied excerpts do not establish a finding about collective diversity, despite the source’s title.

Corpus source-specific finding

The study tests GenAI’s effect on open-ended literary creativity rather than workplace productivity.

Exact returned source excerpt

We offer an answer to this question by experimentally studying the causal effect of having access to GenAI on writing short stories in an online experiment.

Exact returned source excerpt

The domain we focus on is literature, an “open-creativity” task.

Corpus source-specific finding

Access to GenAI increased average novelty and usefulness, particularly when writers could request multiple ideas.

Exact returned source excerpt

We find that having access to GenAI causally increases the average novelty and usefulness—two frequently studied dimensions of creativity—relative to human writers on their own.

Exact returned source excerpt

This is driven, in particular, by our experimental ^Lcondition that enables writers to request multiple GenAI ideas—up to five in our study—each presenting a different starting point, leading to a “tree” branching off to potential storylines (3).

The authors propose that AI ideas support creativity by providing starting points and easing writer’s block.

Exact returned source excerpt

On the one hand, GenAI may enhance: generated ideas from AI may be used as a “springboard” for the human mind, providing potential starting points that can result in a “tree structure” of different storylines (3,10).

Exact returned source excerpt

It can also offer multiple starting venues that help a human writer overcome “writer’s block” and the fear of a blank page (11).
