Corpus synthesis of this source: Amir Ali OBE identifies business debt recovery as a poorly served area and attributes obstacles to a risk-averse, change-resistant legal profession. He grounds his critique in his legal, enforcement and court-user lobbying experience, rather than presenting empirical findings or a specific technological solution.
