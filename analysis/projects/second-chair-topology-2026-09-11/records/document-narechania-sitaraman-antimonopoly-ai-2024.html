Open topic: AI infrastructure: Scale advantages need not create monopoly

Corpus framing of this source: Concentration across the AI stack and the limits of open models

Corpus synthesis of this source: An Antimonopoly Approach to Governing Artificial Intelligence argues that costly data, computing infrastructure and first-mover advantages tend to concentrate foundation-model markets and reinforce incumbent power across the AI stack. The authors use technical explanations, company examples and reported cost estimates to explain these mechanisms, distinguish possible efficiency benefits from competition risks, and argue that open models alone cannot resolve underlying infrastructure concentration.
