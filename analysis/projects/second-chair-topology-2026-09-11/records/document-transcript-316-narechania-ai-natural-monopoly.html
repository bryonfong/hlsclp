Corpus framing of this source: AI Market Domination and the Natural Monopoly Problem

Corpus synthesis of this source: Tejas Narechania argues that AI model development will probably concentrate among one or a few providers because compute costs, economies of scale and network effects reward early scale. He treats a true natural monopoly as uncertain, weighs concentration’s efficiency and environmental benefits against its competitive and systemic risks, and advocates early, accountable regulation, fair-access rules and shared computational infrastructure. The discussion relies chiefly on economic reasoning, comparisons with other network industries and hypothetical risk scenarios.
