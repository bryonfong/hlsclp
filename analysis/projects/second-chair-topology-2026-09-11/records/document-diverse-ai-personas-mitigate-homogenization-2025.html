Corpus framing of this source: Diverse AI personas as a design response to creative homogenization

Corpus synthesis of this source: Yun Wan and Yoram M. Kalman argue that uniform AI inputs, rather than an unavoidable property of generative AI, can narrow collective creative output. Their two-phase story-writing experiment offers initial evidence that diversified persona-generated plots can mitigate homogenization, but limited statistical power leaves both diversity enhancement and preservation of individual creativity gains unresolved.

Corpus framing of this source: Diverse AI Personas Can Mitigate the Homogenization Effect in Human-AI Collaborative Ideation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents a two-phase experiment testing whether persona-diversified AI inputs can avoid the collective homogenization previously associated with generative-AI-assisted creativity. It finds strong differentiation among persona-generated plots and initial evidence that human stories inspired by those plots preserve—and in one condition may enhance—semantic and linguistic diversity. The authors attribute outcomes primarily to deployment and interaction design rather than to GenAI alone, but describe the human-participant phase as underpowered and call for larger, broader replications.
