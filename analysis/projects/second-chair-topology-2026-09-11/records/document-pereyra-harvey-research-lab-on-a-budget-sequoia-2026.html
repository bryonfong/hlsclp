Corpus synthesis of this source: The speaker explains Harvey’s approach to creating training and evaluation data for legal AI when authentic client materials are privileged, confidential and unavailable. The approach combines lawyer-guided synthetic data generation, rubric-first construction of coherent legal scenarios, external scaling support, efficient reinforcement-learning infrastructure and selective open-sourcing, while acknowledging synthetic data’s limits and substantial evaluation costs.
