Corpus framing of this source: Vos on the appeal and risks of AI judicial decision-making

Corpus synthesis of this source: In his 15 October 2025 Legal Geek speech, Sir Geoffrey Vos acknowledges the potential speed and public appeal of machine-made judicial decisions but offers three reasons for caution: finality, limits to replicating human qualities, and risks to the continuing human development of law. The excerpt presents illustrative estimates and prospective arguments rather than empirical findings; it stops before setting out recommendations.

He conditions those uses on informed handling, data confidentiality and human verification, and distinguishes technical capability from the harder ethical question of AI’s proper role in judicial decision-making.
