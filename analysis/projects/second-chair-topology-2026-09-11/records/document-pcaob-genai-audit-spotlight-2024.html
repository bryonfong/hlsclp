Corpus synthesis of this source: The July 2024 staff update reports outreach findings rather than independent performance testing. It characterises audit firms’ current GenAI use as limited and uneven, records expectations of broader use, and describes firms’ emphasis on human review, continuing accountability, data controls, auditability and training.
