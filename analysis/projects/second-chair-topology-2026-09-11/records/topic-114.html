Future of Jobs Report 2025

Market analysts, consultancies & press · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Future of Jobs Report 2025: employer expectations for jobs, skills and workforce adaptation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The report uses employer survey evidence to project workforce change in 2025–2030. It anticipates growing demand for AI-related roles and skills alongside substantial clerical contraction, with variation across industries and economies. It reports employers’ plans for training, hiring, automation and redeployment rather than demonstrating realised AI-driven displacement. Many supplied profile excerpts omit their industry or geographical names, limiting precise attribution.

The findings describe employer expectations, not observed employment outcomes.
