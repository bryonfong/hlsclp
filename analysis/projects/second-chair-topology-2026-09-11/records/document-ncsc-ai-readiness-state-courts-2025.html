Corpus framing of this source: A staged, human-centred framework for state-court AI readiness and implementation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The NCSC guide presents AI readiness as an iterative institutional programme rather than a one-off technology purchase. It combines governance, policy, data, project-selection, procurement, change-management and post-project guidance with illustrative court case studies. The authors recommend beginning with low-risk workflow problems, retaining human responsibility and court control, measuring performance and operational effects, and increasing ambition only as institutional capability grows. State-policy excerpts and interview-based case studies are reported examples, not necessarily the authors’ own practices or independently validated findings.
