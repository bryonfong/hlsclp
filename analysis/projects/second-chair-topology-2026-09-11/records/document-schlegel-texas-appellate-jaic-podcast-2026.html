Corpus framing of this source: Judge Scott Schlegel on technology-enabled courts and the limits of judicial AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Schlegel advocates inexpensive, practical technology that improves court administration, accessibility and procedural experience while preserving human judgment. He rejects delegating adjudication to generative AI in non-consensual court proceedings, stresses that legal experience must accompany technical competence, and recommends cautious adoption, verification and appropriate safeguards. The hosts separately frame AI as a thought partner and report a consent-based arbitration example.
