Corpus framing of this source: Who Should Regulate Lawyers? — Contextual, Multi-Door Professional Regulation

Corpus synthesis of this source: David B. Wilkins rejects a single-regulator model for lawyer misconduct. He develops a contextual framework that compares disciplinary, liability, institutional and legislative controls by their compliance costs, effects on professional independence, type of misconduct and lawyering setting. He concludes that coordinated, context-sensitive use of all four systems can improve compliance while preserving legitimate independent judgment.
