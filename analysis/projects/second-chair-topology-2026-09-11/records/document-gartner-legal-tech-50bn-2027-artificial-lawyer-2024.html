SupportsWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Gartner’s $50bn legal-technology forecast and its practical uncertainties

Corpus synthesis of this source: Gartner predicts that GenAI will expand legal-technology use cases, purchasing and adoption, taking the global market to $50 billion by 2027. It bases the forecast on consumer GenAI availability and existing growth across legal-department software categories, while anticipating changes to billing, sourcing and talent. The accompanying commentary welcomes greater adoption but questions whether the forecast reflects genuinely larger budgets, new buyers or direct use of general-purpose AI. It also stresses human verification and the difficulty of monetising consumer legal products.
