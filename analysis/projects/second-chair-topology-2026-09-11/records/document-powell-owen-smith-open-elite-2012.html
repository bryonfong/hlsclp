Corpus framing of this source: An open but selective elite reshaped biopharmaceutical innovation

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source traces how biopharmaceutical production moved from commercially dominated, specialised partnerships toward a cohesive but changing network spanning science, finance and commerce. Its historical network analysis argues that selective recruitment, diverse affiliations and circulating people sustained organisational innovation, while funding scarcity and consolidation threaten that openness.
