Legal reasoning: Doctrinal analysis is foundational but insufficient

Corpus synthesis: Case-based analysis and formal legal rules provide useful foundations, but neither captures the full range of professional competence or determines legal outcomes by itself. The exemplars vary between curricular reform—adding practical, ethical, multidisciplinary and role-specific development—and jurisprudential accounts that supplement doctrine with prediction and empirical inquiry. They favour practical complements over either abandoning legal analysis or restoring an idealised apprenticeship model.
