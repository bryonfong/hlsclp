Native topic findings

  1. Legal-AI performance depends on the task and retrieval design, rather than a single measure of legal competence.
  2. Provision-specific query optimisation substantially improved retrieval in the RAG Report's experiments.
  3. Retrieval gains vary by provision and retrieval budget, supporting selective rather than uniform optimisation.
  4. Strong contract-clause benchmark scores do not generalise across legal tasks or produce a universal model ranking.
  5. The evidence supports targeted testing, but leaves deployment reliability and causes of performance differences unresolved.
