Corpus framing of this source: Otto von Zastrow on legal research moving into general-purpose AI agents

Corpus synthesis of this source: In “Midpage Goes Native: Legal Research Inside Claude and ChatGPT, with Otto von Zastrow,” von Zastrow argues that integrated general-purpose chat platforms will become the main interface for legal work, while specialist providers supply proprietary legal data, citators, controls and other capabilities behind that interface. He presents Midpage’s software-based collection and organisation of US legal materials as an example, predicts extensive automation of research and publishing workflows, and expects established publishers to survive through content, trust, customer relationships, global reach and acquisitions. He qualifies these forecasts by stressing platform dependence, immature integrations, enterprise adoption delays, the difficulty of trustworthy citation analysis, and law’s greater need for review and reliability compared with software development.
