Corpus framing of this source: Evaluating agents beyond task success: realism, diagnosis, cost and safety

Corpus synthesis of this source: A Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents synthesises benchmark and framework literature rather than presenting a new performance experiment. It argues that interactive agents need dynamic, continually updated evaluation of their execution processes, operational costs and policy compliance. Its domain-specific examples document progress and persistent limitations; its recommendations remain explicitly provisional.
