Corpus framing of this source: Silicon Formalism: Rules, Standards, and Judge AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Posner and Saran replicate a controlled choice-of-law experiment with GPT-5 and compare its decisions and explanations with those of 61 federal judges. They find markedly greater formal legal compliance by GPT-5, test that result across other models, analyse earlier-model errors, and qualify whether departures under an open-ended standard should count as legal errors. They also identify methodological, institutional and legitimacy limits to treating repeated model outputs as judges or replacing pluralistic human judging with AI.
