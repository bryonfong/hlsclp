Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper proposes a normative and testable framework for assessing generative AI by subtracting the cost of mandatory human verification from claimed efficiency gains. It argues that AI’s structural disconnection from factual reality and its opacity make verification unusually demanding in legal work, where professional duties attach to accuracy, integrity, clients, courts and the administration of justice.
