Corpus framing of this source: Jill Lepore’s critique of disruptive innovation as history, prediction and institutional prescription

Corpus synthesis of this source: In The Disruption Machine: What the Gospel of Innovation Gets Wrong (The New Yorker, June 2014), Jill Lepore challenges the evidence, predictive reliability and expanding reach of Clayton Christensen’s disruption theory. She combines historical interpretation, company counterexamples and criticism of disruption rhetoric, distinguishing technological novelty from improvement and commercial success from public obligations.
