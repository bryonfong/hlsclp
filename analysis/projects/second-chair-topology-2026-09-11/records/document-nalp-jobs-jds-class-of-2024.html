Corpus framing of this source: Record graduate employment in 2024, with warnings about hiring and class-size expansion

Corpus synthesis of this source: NALP’s 2025 selected findings, combining Nikia L. Gray’s commentary and Danielle A. Taylor’s statistical summary, document exceptional employment outcomes for the Class of 2024. The report distinguishes rising employment rates from total market capacity, explains changes in employer and salary distributions, and forecasts weaker BigLaw and federal hiring while urging caution about larger law-school cohorts.
