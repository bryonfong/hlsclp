Rule-based legal automation: Formalisation supports rather than replaces expert judgment

Corpus synthesis: Expert systems and executable rules can assist with bounded legal tasks, but their operation embeds interpretive choices and depends on expert knowledge, maintenance, workflow fit and explanation. The exemplars vary between practical system design and jurisprudential critique, while converging on narrow, expert-informed support rather than computational replacement of legal judgment.
