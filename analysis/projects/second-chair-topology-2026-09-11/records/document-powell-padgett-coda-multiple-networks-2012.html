Corpus framing of this source: Coda: Reflections on the Study of Multiple Networks

Corpus synthesis of this source: The authors develop a relational and historical framework for studying how people, practices, rules, skills and resources move across social domains. They distinguish ordinary interdependence from transformative transposition, explain network stability through reproductive fidelity and adaptive capacity, illustrate the argument with historical and life-science cases, and set out a research agenda on system vulnerability.
