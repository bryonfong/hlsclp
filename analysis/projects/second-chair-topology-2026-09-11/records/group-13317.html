Legal-AI effectiveness: Complete legal tasks and resolved problems outrank benchmark scores

Corpus synthesis: Legal-AI performance should be judged by reliable completion of realistic work, including sound reasoning, valid sources and the verification needed before reliance, rather than by average scores or partial credit. The exemplars vary from professional workflows and scoring rubrics to automation claims and everyday legal help, where lower cost or greater participation does not itself establish a remedy. They also show that conclusions remain sensitive to task design, prompts, grading standards and configuration.
