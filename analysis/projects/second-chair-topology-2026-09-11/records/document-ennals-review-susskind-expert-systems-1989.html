Open topic: Executable rules: Formalisation conceals interpretive choices

Corpus framing of this source: Grounding legal expert systems in jurisprudence and domain expertise

Corpus synthesis of this source: The review praises Richard Susskind’s jurisprudential account of legal expert systems for connecting logic programming with legal interpretation and professional understanding. It argues that domain expertise should guide system design, distinguishes legitimate concerns about rule-based representation from objections it considers misconceived, and tentatively envisages interdisciplinary education as a route to lasting applications.

Corpus framing of this source: Jurisprudence, domain expertise and the limits of legal expert systems

Corpus synthesis of this source: The review endorses Susskind’s jurisprudential foundation for legal expert systems and argues that legal expertise must guide their design and assessment. It reports his research agenda, identifies practical development difficulties, and leaves questions about professional adoption and disruption open rather than predicting definite outcomes.
