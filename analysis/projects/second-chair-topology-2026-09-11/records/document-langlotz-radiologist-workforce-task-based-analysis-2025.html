Corpus framing of this source: Task-level productivity gains need not reduce radiologist employment

Corpus synthesis of this source: Curtis P. Langlotz’s 2025 medRxiv preprint, not yet peer reviewed, models AI’s five-year effects on radiology tasks. It predicts substantial time savings but argues that rising imaging demand will probably absorb them. The analysis distinguishes high-impact delegation and report drafting from modest detection and triage gains, while acknowledging judgment-based assumptions, adoption barriers and unmodeled demand growth.
