Native topic findings

  1. AI coding assistance can accelerate task completion while creating substantial human checking and interaction work; these findings measure different aspects of productivity.
  2. The randomized Copilot trial finds a large but narrowly defined completion-time benefit.
  3. Acceptance is not completed verification, and interaction time is not necessarily wasted time.
  4. Experience may shape both benefits and checking difficulties, but the evidence does not establish a single novice–expert pattern.
  5. The practical design agenda combines less intrusive generation with support for continuous checking.
  6. Short studies, early tools and incomplete outcome evidence limit broader conclusions.
