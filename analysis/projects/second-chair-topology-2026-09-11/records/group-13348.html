Civil legal aid funding: Institutional survival can mask diminished capacity

Corpus synthesis: Federal preservation of the Legal Services Corporation does not by itself sustain its ability to meet civil legal need when appropriations and purchasing power decline. The evidence contrasts proposals for abolition, expansion and tighter control, while distinguishing funding effects on institutional capacity from intake demand, service forecasts and broader justice-gap estimates.
