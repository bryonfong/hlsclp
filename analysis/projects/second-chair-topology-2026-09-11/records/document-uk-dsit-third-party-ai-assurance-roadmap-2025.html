Corpus framing of this source: Trusted Third-Party AI Assurance Roadmap: Information Access for Effective Assurance

Corpus synthesis of this source: The roadmap identifies controlled access to AI-system information as a prerequisite for effective third-party assurance. It describes confidentiality, security, privacy and information-management barriers, then recommends secure technical access, transparency standards and government-backed information-sharing guidelines.

Corpus source-specific finding

Assurance requires access tailored to the service

Exact returned source excerpt

the **algorithm** that is used to generate the model, and its **parameters**  **oversight** and **change management mechanisms**, such as the monitoring of the AI system and steps taken to update, improve or repair it post-deployment  **documentation** that describes the management and governance processes surrounding the AI system Different AI assurance services will require different levels of access to information, ranging from full, white box access (that is, to the inner workings of an AI system) to minimal access (that is, to relevant documentation).

Corpus source-specific finding

Information-access barriers can undermine effective assurance

Exact returned source excerpt

Assurance providers often struggle to access the information they need to conduct effective assurance of AI systems.

Exact returned source excerpt

Firms who are using assurance services may have concerns about sharing commercially confidential information – such as training data – with third-party providers.
