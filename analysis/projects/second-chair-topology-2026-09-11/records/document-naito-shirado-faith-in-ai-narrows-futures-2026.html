Corpus framing of this source: Faith in AI can narrow the futures individuals consider

Corpus synthesis of this source: Aoi Naito and Hirokazu Shirado use four preregistered online studies based on Newcomb’s paradox to argue that perceived AI predictive authority can alter decision reasoning, induce people to forgo guaranteed rewards, persist despite predictive failure, and become self-fulfilling through behavioral adaptation. They attribute the effect to human psychology rather than actual algorithmic prediction, qualify its real-world generalisability, and call for attention to how perceived AI authority reshapes decisions.
