Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: Scott Stevenson on workflow-integrated legal AI and data protection

Corpus synthesis of this source: Stevenson draws on Rally’s adoption difficulties to argue that legal AI should augment lawyers inside familiar tools rather than require a replacement workflow. He treats sustained usage as more probative than positive demonstrations or stated intent, describes resistance to additional applications as a broader software problem, and outlines Spellbook’s optional-storage, encryption and de-identification controls. The security concerns themselves are raised by the interviewer; Stevenson responds with claims about Spellbook’s practices.

Corpus source-specific finding
