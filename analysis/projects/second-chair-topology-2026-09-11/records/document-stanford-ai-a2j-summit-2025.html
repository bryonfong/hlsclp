## Lessons Learned and Practical Strategies from the First Generation of AI+A2J Work A consistent “lesson learned” from Day 1 was that legal aid AI only works when it’s treated as *mission infrastructure*, not as a cool add-on.

Exact returned source excerpt

Leaders emphasized values as practical guardrails: put people first (staff + clients), keep the main thing the main thing (serving clients), and plan for the long term — especially because large legal aid organizations are “big ships” that can’t pivot overnight.
