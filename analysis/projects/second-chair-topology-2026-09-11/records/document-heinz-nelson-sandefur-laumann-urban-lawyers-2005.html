Corpus framing of this source: Corporate-client change and the restructuring of the urban bar

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied catalogue text and endorsements present Urban Lawyers as an empirical study of changes in Chicago’s legal profession. They attribute its central argument to changes in corporate clients and connect those changes to specialization, larger firms and professional stratification. The material reports the book’s findings through promotional descriptions and reviewers’ assessments rather than presenting its underlying data.
