Corpus framing of this source: Jason Barnwell — Agiloft (CLO, ex-Microsoft) on AI agents, declared protocols, and the rate of change in legal

Corpus synthesis of this source: Barnwell characterises law as social infrastructure built for scale but constrained by an obsolete scarcity model. He weighs institutional inertia against falling barriers to AI adoption, argues that buyer behaviour will determine the pace of commercial legal-market change, and uses an Agiloft workflow as evidence that previously difficult work is already becoming accessible. He predicts that agentic, parallel workflows will transform legal practice while acknowledging that substantive legal and compliance problems remain complex.
