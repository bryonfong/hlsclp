Native topic findings

  1. AI simulation offers a proposed replacement for lost practice opportunities, not a replacement for lawyers’ judgment.
  2. The proposed learning mechanism combines realistic decisions, variable scenarios and specific feedback.
  3. Early deployment shows adoption and favorable reactions, but does not establish improved advocacy.
  4. The central disagreement concerns how much simulation can substitute for consequential experience.
  5. Practical implementation centers on structured teaching and mentoring, not simply distributing software.
  6. Simulation could broaden access to rehearsal, but advocacy types require distinct training assumptions.
  7. Broader simulations and economic benefits remain a development agenda rather than established results.
