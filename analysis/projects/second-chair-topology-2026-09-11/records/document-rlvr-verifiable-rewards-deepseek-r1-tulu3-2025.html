Corpus framing of this source: DeepSeek-R1: Reinforcement Learning, Distillation, Evaluation and Limits

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents DeepSeek-R1-Zero and DeepSeek-R1 as evidence that large-scale reinforcement learning can elicit strong reasoning without supervised cold-start data, while a small cold-start dataset improves usability and performance. It reports benchmark gains, describes a multi-stage alignment pipeline, finds distillation especially effective for smaller models, documents unsuccessful process-reward-model experiments, and identifies practical limitations and future work.
