Corpus framing of this source: LEGIT: issue-tree rubrics for evaluating and improving legal reasoning traces

Corpus synthesis of this source: Lee, On, Han, Cohan and Hockenmaier introduce LEGIT, a 24,000-instance Korean legal-judgment dataset and evaluation framework that converts court judgments into hierarchical, issue-specific rubrics. The source tests rubric reliability with lawyers and LLM judges, diagnoses model failures, analyses their propagation through legal issue trees, and compares retrieval and reinforcement-learning interventions.

Corpus source-specific finding

The authors argue that final-answer accuracy alone cannot adequately assess legal reasoning and introduce LEGIT to evaluate issue coverage and issue correctness.
