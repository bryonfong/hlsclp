Corpus framing of this source: Building a Better Bar: Empirically defining and assessing minimum competence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source uses qualitative focus groups, participant accounts and prior practice studies to define minimum competence for newly licensed lawyers. It argues that competence extends well beyond doctrinal recall to professional judgment, client interaction, issue identification, research, communication, strategic perspective, workload management, resilience and continued learning, then proposes a licensing system combining more authentic written assessment with coursework and supervised practice.
