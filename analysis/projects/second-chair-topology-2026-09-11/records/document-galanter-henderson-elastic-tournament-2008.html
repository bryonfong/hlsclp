Corpus framing of this source: The elastic tournament: career-long competition reshapes the large law firm

Corpus synthesis of this source: In The Elastic Tournament: A Second Transformation of the Big Law Firm (2008), Marc Galanter and William Henderson argue that large firms have retained promotion tournaments but abandoned much of partnership’s former security. Drawing on firm rankings, partnership and mobility data, associate surveys and earlier scholarship, they explain how client purchasing, financial transparency and competition for rainmakers reshape growth, governance and careers. They distinguish observed changes from forecasts of market segmentation and professional consequences.
