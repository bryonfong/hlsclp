Corpus framing of this source: Erin Levine — Hello Divorce’s consumer-centred alternative to adversarial divorce

Corpus synthesis of this source: Levine presents Hello Divorce as a team-built effort to transform family law so consumers can leave unhealthy relationships without defaulting to prolonged, expensive legal conflict. The supplied passage also includes the interviewer’s account of the service’s expected benefits, which Levine does not state in her own words.

Corpus source-specific finding

Levine defines the long-term mission as disrupting the family-law industry and changing consumer expectations that divorce necessarily requires lawyers and conflict.
