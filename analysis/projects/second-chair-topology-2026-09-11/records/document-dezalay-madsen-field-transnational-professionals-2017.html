Corpus framing of this source: Transnational legal power as state-linked elite reproduction

Corpus synthesis of this source: Dezalay and Madsen develop a post-Bourdieusian explanation of transnational legal elites and a reflexive research strategy for studying them. Drawing on historical scholarship and earlier studies of arbitration, human rights and environmental law, they connect professional competition to state power, inherited resources and elite mobility rather than treating it as a contest over expertise alone.
