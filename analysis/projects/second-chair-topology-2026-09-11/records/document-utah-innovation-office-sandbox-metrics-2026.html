Corpus framing of this source: Utah legal-services Sandbox: access-to-justice focus and Phase 2 measurement

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Utah Office of Legal Services Innovation describes an adaptive regulatory Sandbox designed to inform future Utah Supreme Court policy on legal-service regulation and improve access to legal assistance. It reports tighter controls and scope, outlines revised data collection and reporting for Phase 2, and anticipates continued refinement with the Utah State Bar.

Corpus source-specific finding

The Office says the Sandbox has added front-end controls and narrowed its scope over time to advance its access-to-justice objectives.

Exact returned source excerpt

For example, additional front-end controls have been added over time, and the scope has been narrowed in the furtherance of the Sandbox’s access-to-justice objectives.

Corpus source-specific finding

The Sandbox aims to produce information that the Utah Supreme Court can use when deciding how to regulate legal practice to increase assistance for underserved Utahns.

Exact returned source excerpt

A goal of the Sandbox is to generate meaningful information that the Utah Supreme Court can utilize to make future policy decisions regarding the regulation of the practice of law in a manner that increases underserved Utahns’ access to legal assistance.** **As part of the implementation of Phase 2 of the Sandbox, the Legal Services Innovation (“LSI”) Committee will evaluate how to best collect and analyze data in a manner that advances the Court’s new guidance to focus on models with the most promise for addressing the access-to-justice gap in Utah while also addressing concerns raised by the Supreme Court and data team.

Corpus source-specific finding

The Office is replacing monthly reports during the transition with an annual report based on new metrics and calendar-year 2025 data.

Exact returned source excerpt

During this transition, we are suspending monthly data reports and will instead publish an annual data report that will reflect the new metrics and data gathered during calendar year 2025.** **The LSI Committee expects that fine-tuning will continue in the remaining two and a half years of the Sandbox and is excited to continue to be at the forefront of exploring legal innovation and regulatory reform in collaboration with the Utah State Bar.
