Regulators, courts & professional bodies · Adoption at scale (2025)

Corpus framing of this source: Bandla v SRA: evidential gaps defeat the explanation for a late appeal

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied judgment extracts reject an extension of time to appeal an SDT decision, distinguishing evidence of mental illness from evidence that illness prevented a timely appeal. They also reject procedural complaints and identify inconsistencies in the appellant’s accounts. The judge flags serious concerns about cited case law but expressly sets those aside in this analysis; the extracts do not supply the later ruling on those concerns or establish AI involvement.
