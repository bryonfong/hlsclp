Corpus framing of this source: Professional jurisdictional control shapes who performs digital legal work

Corpus synthesis of this source: Sako, Qian and Attolini develop a theory linking professions’ jurisdictional arrangements to the allocation of new digital tasks. They distinguish protective control, which retains tasks within a profession or delegates them to subordinate semiprofessionals, from connective control, which supports collaboration among equal-status professions. They report supporting evidence from online job postings across organisational, national and sectoral comparisons. The supplied material contains the cross-confirmed abstract and bibliographic notes, but not the article’s full text.
