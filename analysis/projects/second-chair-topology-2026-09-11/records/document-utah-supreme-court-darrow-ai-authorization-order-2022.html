Corpus framing of this source: Utah’s evidence-led regulatory framework for nontraditional legal-service models

Corpus synthesis of this source: The authorization packet presents the Utah Sandbox as a controlled route for nonlawyer ownership, fee sharing, technology platforms and nonlawyer providers to deliver legal services. It proposes comparative, service-model-based risk assessment; treats lawyer involvement as a provisional risk mitigant; and relies on standardized reporting, audits, disclosures and graduated enforcement to identify actual consumer harm. It anticipates more technology-enabled legal advice, acknowledges uncertainty about novel models and plans to refine risk categories as evidence accumulates.
