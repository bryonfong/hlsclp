Corpus synthesis of this source: Jon Campisi’s June 2026 American Lawyer feature reports how four firms use DepoSim to develop litigation skills. Corpus framing of this source: DepoSim as experiential deposition training for associates

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source presents DepoSim through comments from Littler and Brownstein personnel as a supplement to established training that gives junior lawyers realistic, interactive deposition practice. It attributes the tool’s value to filling gaps in live-case opportunities, providing feedback, building confidence and preparing lawyers for situations encountered in practice. Brownstein also treats adoption as part of its broader AI and career-development strategy. The supplied material reports positive firm experiences but provides no quantitative outcomes, independent evaluation or prediction.
