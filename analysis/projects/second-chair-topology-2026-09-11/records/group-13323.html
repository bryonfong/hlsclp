Professional-work evidence: Role structure and study design bound claims about AI effects

Corpus synthesis: Evidence about AI in professional work must be interpreted through how expertise, tasks and responsibility are organised, as well as through what a study actually measures. The exemplars range from occupational attitudes and a prespecified creativity experiment to citation sanctions and task-allocation analysis; they therefore support bounded claims about perceived expertise, proposed tests, continuing verification duties and deployment-dependent outcomes rather than general conclusions about substitution or productivity.
