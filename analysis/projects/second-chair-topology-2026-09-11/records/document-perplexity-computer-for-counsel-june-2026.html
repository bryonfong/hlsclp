# Perplexity Makes Its Move Into Legal, Unveiling Industry Features at 'Computer for Counsel' Event *Bob Ambrogi — LawSites / LawNext (Bob Ambrogi) / Law.com (2026)* ## Full Text — Bob Ambrogi, LawSites/LawNext, 25 June 2026 Perplexity, the AI search engine known for delivering sourced answers to user queries, is making an explicit move into the legal market, rolling out legal-specific features within its agentic platform, Perplexity Computer, and positioning the product as what it calls "Computer for Counsel." The company highlighted the legal push yesterday at an invitation-only event in New York aimed at in-house counsel, lawyers at small and midsized firms, and legal tech founders.

Exact returned source excerpt

The event marked Perplexity's most conspicuous effort yet to target legal as a specific vertical for product development, partnerships and go-to-market strategy.

Corpus source-specific finding

The company pitches a connected research, drafting and workflow layer, not a comprehensive legal database.

Exact returned source excerpt

Notably, Perplexity is expressly not attempting to build a full-fledged legal research database in the mold of Westlaw, LexisNexis or Bloomberg Law.

Exact returned source excerpt

Rather, it describes its platform as a research, drafting and workflow layer that can sit on top of the web, connected enterprise systems and specialized legal data sources, giving lawyers cited answers they can verify and use as the basis for further work.
