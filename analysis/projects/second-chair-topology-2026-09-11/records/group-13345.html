Automated hiring audits: Transparency exposes bias without ensuring fair screening

Corpus synthesis: NYC Local Law 144 uses audits, demographic reporting and disclosure to make potential discrimination in employment screening more visible. The exemplars differ in emphasis—coverage and employer responsibility versus audit scope, data and renewal limits—but both treat these requirements as procedural safeguards rather than proof of nondiscrimination or a remedy for bias, and they do not establish a right to nonautomated screening.
