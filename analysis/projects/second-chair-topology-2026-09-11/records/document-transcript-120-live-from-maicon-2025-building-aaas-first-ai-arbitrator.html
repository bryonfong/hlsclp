Corpus framing of this source: AAA’s phased, human-supervised AI arbitrator and AI-native dispute-resolution strategy

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bridget McCormack presents AAA’s AI arbitrator as an additional, human-supervised option designed first for bounded, documents-only business disputes. She grounds the design in AAA’s domain expertise, proprietary data, industry consultation and existing AI support tools; predicts lower cost and faster resolution; and argues that interactive claim clarification may strengthen procedural fairness by helping parties feel heard. She advocates preserving user choice and expanding reasoning capabilities case by case only where AAA has suitable expertise and training data. Jen Leonard separately reports OpenAI’s troubled opt-out rollout for likenesses and copyrighted characters as an example of misuse, rights-holder opposition, policy reversal and continuing public backlash.
