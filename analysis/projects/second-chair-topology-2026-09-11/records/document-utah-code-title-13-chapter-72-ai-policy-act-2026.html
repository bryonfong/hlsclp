Corpus framing of this source: Utah AI Learning Laboratory: Admission Standards and State Protections

Corpus synthesis of this source: The statute conditions access to regulatory mitigation or joint interpretation agreements on participant capability, resources, consumer-benefit potential, risk controls and proportionate testing. It permits expert consultation during application review and clarifies that participation neither carries state endorsement nor shifts participant-related liability to the state.

Corpus source-specific finding

The office must admit only participants that demonstrate responsible technical capability, adequate financial resources and potentially substantial consumer benefits that may outweigh the risks of mitigated regulatory enforcement.

Exact returned source excerpt

(1) To be eligible for a regulatory mitigation agreement or a joint interpretation agreement, a participant shall demonstrate to the office that: (a) the participant has the technical expertise and capability to responsibly develop, implement, and use or supervise the use of the proposed artificial intelligence technology; (b) the participant has sufficient financial resources to meet obligations during testing; (c) the artificial intelligence technology provides potential substantial consumer benefits that may outweigh identified risks from mitigated enforcement of regulations; (d) the participant has an effective plan to monitor and minimize identified risks from testing; and (e) the scale, scope, and duration of proposed testing is appropriately limited based on risk assessments.

Corpus source-specific finding

Participants must present effective risk controls and confine testing to a scale, scope and duration proportionate to assessed risks.
