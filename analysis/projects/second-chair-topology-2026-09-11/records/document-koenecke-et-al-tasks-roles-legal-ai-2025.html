Corpus framing of this source: Tasks and Roles in Legal AI: Data Curation, Annotation, and Verification

Corpus synthesis of this source: Koenecke, Stiglitz, Mimno and Wilkens frame legal AI as a high-potential but unusually high-stakes field whose responsible development depends on solving three connected problems: curating usable legal data, producing expert legal annotations and verifying outputs. They argue that output verification is the most important challenge, favour task- and user-specific deployment with human oversight, and recommend interdisciplinary collaboration, auditing and open release of data, models and code.
