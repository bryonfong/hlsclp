Corpus framing of this source: From Chemical to Social Networks

Corpus synthesis of this source: John F. Padgett develops a quasi-evolutionary model of how social or linguistic autocatalysis can build on chemical production autocatalysis. He treats language, communication, constitutive ties and repair as mechanisms through which distributed systems adapt, survive disruption and generate novelty, while identifying several conclusions as provisional pending computer simulation.

Corpus source-specific finding

Padgett models a staged transition from chemical production autocatalysis to social or linguistic autocatalysis, identifying language—not complexity or intelligence—as the key distinction.
