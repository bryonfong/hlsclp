# LawNext: From Roommates to Billionaires — Harvey's Founders Gabriel Pereyra and Winston Weinberg on Building AI Infrastructure for Law *Bob Ambrogi — LawSites (LawNext podcast) (2026)* [Gabriel Pereyra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabepereyra/) and [Winston Weinberg](https://www.linkedin.com/in/winston-weinberg/) started legal AI company [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/) in 2022 as roommates in a San Francisco apartment.

Exact returned source excerpt

Pereyra had been working on AI research at Meta and Google, while Weinberg was a first-year litigation associate at O’Melveny & Myers.

The founders identify model access and a major client win as early turning points.

In this episode of LawNext, Pereyra and Weinberg take us back to Harvey’s earliest days, when they were sending thousands of LinkedIn messages trying to get anyone to look at their product.
