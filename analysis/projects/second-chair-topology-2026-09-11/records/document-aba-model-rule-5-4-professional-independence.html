Corpus framing of this source: ABA Model Rule 5.4: restrictions protecting lawyers’ professional independence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The supplied excerpt sets out the ABA Model Rule’s restrictions on nonlawyer fee-sharing, partnerships, ownership, management and interference with professional judgment. It states normative requirements and exceptions, not empirical findings or predictions; it does not establish any particular state’s adopted rule.

Corpus source-specific finding

The rule prohibits sharing legal fees with nonlawyers, subject to four specified exceptions.

Exact returned source excerpt

5.4 — Professional Independence of a Lawyer *American Bar Association — American Bar Association (2026)* (a) A lawyer or law firm shall not share legal fees with a nonlawyer, except that: (1) an agreement by a lawyer with the lawyer's firm, partner, or associate may provide for the payment of money, over a reasonable period of time after the lawyer's death, to the lawyer's estate or to one or more specified persons; (2) a lawyer who purchases the practice of a deceased, disabled, or disappeared lawyer may, pursuant to the provisions of Rule 1.17, pay to the estate or other representative of that lawyer the agreed-upon purchase price; (3) a lawyer or law firm may include nonlawyer employees in a compensation or retirement plan, even though the plan is based in whole or in part on a profit-sharing arrangement; and (4) a lawyer may share court-awarded legal fees with a nonprofit organization that employed, retained or recommended employment of the lawyer in the matter.

Corpus source-specific finding

The rule bars partnerships with nonlawyers when any partnership activity consists of practising law.

Exact returned source excerpt

(b) A lawyer shall not form a partnership with a nonlawyer if any of the activities of the partnership consist of the practice of law.

Corpus source-specific finding

Lawyers must retain professional judgment when another person recommends, employs or pays them to serve a client.

Exact returned source excerpt

(c) A lawyer shall not permit a person who recommends, employs, or pays the lawyer to render legal services for another to direct or regulate the lawyer's professional judgment in rendering such legal services.

The excerpt restricts nonlawyer ownership and leadership in for-profit professional legal entities.
