Corpus framing of this source: Story Protocol and Poseidon: Licensed Real-World Data Infrastructure for AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Andrea Muttoni argues that access to specific, high-quality and legally cleared data increasingly constrains AI development. He presents Poseidon as a pipeline for sourcing and preparing private or newly created real-world data, while Story records IP metadata and licensing terms and uses smart contracts to control licensed access. He also outlines the project’s early-stage roadmap and explains how his agency experience shaped his preference for scalable products.
