Corpus framing of this source: The Tragedy of the Cognitive Commons: How AI Could Disrupt the Regeneration of Professional Expertise

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper develops a predictive “Cognitive Commons” framework linking HRD, commons theory, systems thinking and distributed cognition. It argues that AI can weaken profession-wide expertise regeneration by removing entry-level work or allowing AI-assisted performance without deep learning, while the resulting loss of independent domain mastery undermines substantive verification of AI outputs. It presents early labour-market and deskilling evidence, distinguishes those findings from its unobserved profession-level depletion prediction, and proposes a conditional research and governance agenda rather than universal prescriptions.
