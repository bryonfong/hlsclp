Corpus framing of this source: Ashurst’s GenAI trials: faster drafting, conditional value and adoption lessons

Corpus synthesis of this source: In Vox PopulAI: Lessons from a global law firm's exploration of generative AI, Ashurst reports early findings from global workplace trials. It identifies faster first drafts and everyday staff support as initial benefits, while stressing legal accuracy, human review, multidimensional quality assessment and sustained training. The report presents exploratory findings rather than definitive conclusions.

Corpus source-specific finding

Ashurst tested organisational capability, not just individual tools.

Exact returned source excerpt

From November 2023 to March 2024, Ashurst’s Office of the Chief Digital Officer (the CDO team) led three global GenAI trials involving 411 partners, lawyers and staff representing all of our practice areas and business services functions across 23 offices in 14 countries in our global network.

Exact returned source excerpt

The trials were designed to capture a mix of qualitative and quantitative assessment data through a blind study, controlled experiments with small groups of participants, and feedback surveys, giving our people a voice in how GenAI could benefit them.
