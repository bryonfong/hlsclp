Corpus framing of this source: Datasite–Legora integration promises permission-controlled AI diligence

Corpus synthesis of this source: In their May 18, 2026 press release, Datasite and Legora announce an integration for analysing data-room documents directly in Legora. They describe workflow and access-control features and predict shorter diligence cycles, rather than report measured performance results.

Corpus source-specific finding

The partnership targets the friction and security risks of moving deal documents between platforms.

Exact returned source excerpt

# Datasite and Legora platforms integrate to accelerate AI-powered diligence across the deal lifecycle *Legora, Datasite — Legora (newsroom) (2026)* # Datasite and Legora platforms integrate to accelerate AI-powered diligence PRESS RELEASE Datasite and Legora platforms integrate to accelerate AI-powered diligence across the deal lifecycle CATEGORY Press Release PUBLISHED May 18, 2026 Datasite, a leader in AI-powered solutions that enable private market investment and Legora, the collaborative AI platform for legal professionals, today announced a strategic partnership that enables deal teams to perform analysis on documents from Datasite’s data rooms directly in Legora, reducing workflow friction.

Exact returned source excerpt

Deal teams contend with thousands of documents under tight timelines, and leveraging AI often requires downloading files from the data room and uploading them to a third-party tool, which is not only cumbersome but creates a significant security risk.

Corpus source-specific finding

Datasite permissions constrain which documents users can access through Legora.

Exact returned source excerpt

Through the integration, Datasite permissions automatically carry into Legora so only documents that a user is permissioned to see can be pulled into the Legora platform, resulting in a smoother and more efficient workflow.

Exact returned source excerpt

A user on the Legora platform can only access and incorporate documents that they are already entitled to access in Datasite.

Corpus source-specific finding

The announced capabilities span selective document analysis and full-data-room diligence.

Exact returned source excerpt

Through the integration, Legora customers with access to Datasite data rooms will be able to: Select and open Datasite VDR files for analysis directly within Legora: Navigate Datasite folders directly in Legora and choose individual files, folders or, document categories to inform due diligence, legal analysis and, drafts.

Exact returned source excerpt

Run full data room analysis in Legora: Leverage Legora’s legal-specific AI across the data room to surface red flags, identify missing or inconsistent documents, use templates for drafts and triage diligence faster.

Corpus source-specific finding

The companies predict shorter review cycles without quantifying the expected savings.

Exact returned source excerpt

This functionality will compress review cycles that have traditionally consumed weeks of the deal cycle.
