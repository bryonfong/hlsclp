Corpus framing of this source: Deliberate practice as the primary mechanism of expert performance

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ericsson, Krampe and Tesch-Römer argue that expert performance develops chiefly through long-term, structured and domain-specific deliberate practice rather than mere experience or fixed innate talent. Their studies of musicians link accumulated practice to skill, while their broader review explains how feedback, early access, motivation, resources, recovery and physiological adaptation shape development. They qualify their argument by allowing that heritable differences may affect motivation or capacity for sustained practice and by acknowledging limits in separating innate from acquired characteristics after training begins.
