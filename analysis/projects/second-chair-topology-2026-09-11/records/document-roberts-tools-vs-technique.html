Corpus framing of this source: Tools vs Technique: The Capability Gap

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that effective AI adoption depends less on model choice than on human and organisational capability. Drawing on the author's experience introducing Claude Code in a willing team of fewer than ten, it presents AI-native fluency as a learned practice requiring workflow redesign, verification judgement, sustained use and hands-on support. It predicts that this capability gap will determine who captures AI's value and warns that large, high-stakes organisations face the greatest adoption barriers.
