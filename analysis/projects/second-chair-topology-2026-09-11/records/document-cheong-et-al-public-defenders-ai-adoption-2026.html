Corpus synthesis of this source: Cheong and colleagues use interviews with 17 US public defense professionals to map where AI might assist resource-constrained defense work. Interviewees prioritise evidence investigation over courtroom advocacy and strategy; the authors recommend collaborative interfaces, targeted research, defender-specific benchmarks and locally deployable models. The supplied substantive excerpts contain interview-based assessments and recommendations, not demonstrated productivity or client-outcome gains. Most remaining material consists of bibliographic references, which do not establish the authors’ own findings.
