Corpus framing of this source: Washington’s oversight framework for the entity-regulation pilot

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Washington Supreme Court order establishes a time-limited, data-driven pilot in which participating entities receive conditional authority to practise law under WSBA and Board oversight. It sets reporting, confidentiality, complaint, investigation and enforcement procedures, and provides for recommendations on continued authorization and possible entity regulation after the pilot.
