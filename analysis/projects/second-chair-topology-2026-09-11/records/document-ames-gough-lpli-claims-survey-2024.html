Corpus framing of this source: Ames & Gough: rising malpractice severity, emerging AI exposure and stronger risk controls

Corpus synthesis of this source: Ames & Gough’s 2024 professional liability survey reports broadly flat claim frequency in 2023 but exceptionally large payouts. It combines insurer observations with external lateral-hiring evidence and recommends conflict controls, ethical AI supervision, clear client communications and standalone cyber coverage. It distinguishes widespread concern about AI from the limited AI-related claims insurers had actually encountered.

Together, they provide insurance for more than 80 percent of the AM Law 250 firms.
