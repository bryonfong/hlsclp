Corpus framing of this source: The Rapid Adoption of Generative AI: widespread use, reported time savings and conditional productivity gains

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper uses nationally representative U.S. survey data from 2024 to document generative-AI adoption and estimate its early productivity potential. It connects self-reported time savings to an aggregate production model, compares its estimates with experimental and task-exposure research, and stresses that potential gains need not appear in measured output.
