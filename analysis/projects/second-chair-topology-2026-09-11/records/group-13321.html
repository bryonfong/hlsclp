Medical AI device inventories: regulatory breadth does not establish innovation or impact

Corpus synthesis: Regulatory histories and device listings document the spread of medical AI across specialties, tasks and approval pathways, but do not by themselves show clinical effectiveness, autonomy or workforce replacement. Successive versions, publication delays and product classifications can also inflate apparent product counts, so the inventories support device-level inquiry rather than broad claims about adoption or professional impact.
