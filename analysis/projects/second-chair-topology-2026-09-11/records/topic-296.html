Evaluation validity: Benchmark safeguards impose trade-offs

Corpus synthesis: Requires contextual quality, reproducibility, contamination control, agent diagnosis, cost accounting and independent safety testing.

This record retains the provider’s full available topic findings and source perspectives. It does not claim a new independent analytical review. Positions refer to this topic’s proposition: “Redirects” is not simple opposition, and “Unclear” is not support.

Native topic findings

  1. Benchmark scores support bounded comparisons, not automatic claims of professional competence or deployment safety.
  2. Legal AI evaluation must assess contextual quality and human–AI workflows, not isolated accuracy alone.
  3. Reproducibility, statistical validity and construct validity require separate checks.
  4. Contamination, overfitting and saturation weaken scores, but safeguards involve trade-offs.
  5. Agent evaluation needs execution-level diagnosis, cost accounting and independent safety tests.
  6. Benchmark credibility depends on incentives and institutional access as well as technical design.
  7. The main tension concerns how far benchmark reform can go, not whether evaluation matters.
  8. Practical proposals combine purpose-specific evidence, benchmark quality standards and independent oversight.
