Open topic: AI insurance: Coverage cannot replace professional governance

Corpus framing of this source: AI professional liability: verification failures, broad existing coverage and competitive constraints

Corpus synthesis of this source: Matthew Lerner’s Business Insurance article and sidebar report insurers’ and brokers’ assessments of AI-related professional liability. Interviewees distinguish existing court-sanctions incidents from still-limited malpractice claims, describe generally available coverage subject to policy exclusions, and explain why competition may delay tighter underwriting despite concerns about unverified output and undisclosed AI use.

Corpus source-specific finding

Interviewees identify inadequate verification and inappropriate tool selection as sources of professional liability.
