Corpus synthesis of this source: In Unilever’s February 2024 corporate interview, CLO Maria Varsellona describes an in-house legal transformation centred on three global hubs staffed by lawyers using generative AI. She reports operational and developmental benefits; the supplied excerpt offers a qualitative leadership account rather than quantified results or evidence isolating AI’s contribution.
