Corpus framing of this source: AVA and the Courts: lessons from Alaska’s court chatbot experiment

Corpus synthesis of this source: The NACM discussion presents AVA as a resource-intensive court service rather than a stand-alone technology build. Jeannie Sato, Aubrie Souza and Tom Martin stress curated legal content, bounded technical design, user testing, multidisciplinary collaboration and continuing human monitoring. They describe the extended development period as warranted quality work, qualify AI’s ability to accelerate legal-service delivery, and recommend planning for long-term staffing, funding and maintenance.
