Corpus synthesis of this source: Drawing on a global survey of 1,049 chief legal officers across 43 countries, ACC argues that the CLO has become a C-suite strategist, governance leader and technology-change agent. It documents stronger executive influence, a stabilising governance remit, rapid generative-AI adoption, mostly stable staffing, greater use of external expertise, emerging legal-operations maturity, shifting macro and regulatory risks, and persistent budget, litigation-cost and talent pressures. The report generally interprets survey responses as directional organisational trends; several causal explanations remain qualified inferences rather than measured findings.
