Open topic: Malpractice insurance: Reported AI claims remain hard to isolate

Corpus framing of this source: EPIC’s 2025 malpractice survey reports rising defense rates and concerns about oversight, cyber and AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: EPIC’s press release summarises insurer-reported malpractice trends and Eileen Garczynski’s explanations and recommendations. It provides numerical evidence on defense-counsel rate increases, identifies claim-generating practices, and reports concerns about AI and attorney oversight without quantifying AI-related claims.

Corpus source-specific finding

The evidence comes from 11 insurers with substantial large-firm coverage, not a direct survey of law firms.

Exact returned source excerpt

The survey examined key trends driving law firm malpractice exposures and claims by polling 11 leading lawyers’ professional liability insurance companies that, on a combined basis, provide insurance to more than 80% of the AM Law 100 and NLJ 250 firms.

Exact returned source excerpt

Full release text below; the survey itself is not held.* *The Lawyers Professional Liability Insurer Claims survey finds cyber and attorney oversight to be among the top concerns* WASHINGTON--([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com))--Law firms throughout the U.S.

Corpus source-specific finding

Most surveyed insurers reported higher defense-counsel rates.

Exact returned source excerpt

“Firms must remain hypervigilant to maintain their credibility and avoid the common errors that can prompt costly legal disputes.” Key findings of the survey include:** **The cost of defending malpractice claims continues to increase year over year.** Ten of the 11 insurers surveyed reported that the rates they pay defense counsel increased last year.

Exact returned source excerpt

Among them, three saw rates increase by 5% to 10%, and seven noted increases of 2% to 5%.

Corpus source-specific finding

Insurers identify three principal claim-generating practice areas and a growing share from insurance defense.

Exact returned source excerpt

**The three main practice areas generating claims.** Polled insurers named Trust & Estate, Business Transactions and Corporate & Securities as the three main practice areas generating claims.

Exact returned source excerpt

Insurance defense work is also seeing a growing percentage of legal malpractice claims.

Corpus source-specific finding

AI appears as an insurer concern alongside cyber and attorney oversight, not as a measured source of claims.

Exact returned source excerpt

When asked about specific issues keeping them up at night, the surveyed insurers named cyber and attorney oversight as top areas of concern, as well as AI risks.

Corpus source-specific finding

Garczynski warns that flexible working and merger growth may weaken oversight and urges vigilance.

Exact returned source excerpt

“As firms increase flexible work schedules and experience more merger growth the past several years, there is growing concern that some law firms may have blurred the lines of oversight for attorneys working from home, new joiners, and even those transitioning out of the firm,” added Garczynski.

Exact returned source excerpt

“This post-Covid phenomena may be leading to unchecked risks and lapses in accountability.” The participating insurers in the EPIC Law Firm Group LPL survey were Ascot, Berkley Select, BRIT, CNA, Crum & Forster, Liberty, Markel, QBE, Sompo, Swiss Re Corporate Solutions, and Travelers.
