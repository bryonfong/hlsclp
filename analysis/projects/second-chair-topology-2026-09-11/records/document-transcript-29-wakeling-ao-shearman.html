Corpus framing of this source: David Wakeling on broad AI adoption and the changing role of lawyers

Corpus synthesis of this source: Wakeling argues that AI differs from earlier legal technology because it is readily accessible to individual lawyers and is becoming part of ordinary organisational practice rather than remaining confined to specialist projects. He predicts that young lawyers will concentrate more on critical thinking, verification and proper application of information while using technology to focus more closely on core legal work. He also reports broad cross-sector and international client interest in AI.
