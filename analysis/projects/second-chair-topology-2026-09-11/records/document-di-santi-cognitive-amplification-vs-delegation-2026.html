Corpus framing of this source: Cognitive Amplification vs Cognitive Delegation in Human–AI Systems: A Metric Framework

Corpus synthesis of this source: The paper proposes a task-specific framework for distinguishing genuine human–AI amplification from high-performing but dependent collaboration. It combines four metrics with a stylized NetLogo simulation, AI-off assessments and robustness probes. Its experiments distinguish three reliance regimes but find no genuine amplification in the tested configurations; even eliminating modeled atrophy fails to produce positive collaborative gain. The authors recommend evaluating retained human capability and dependency alongside output, while qualifying the work as an abstract, initial validation rather than evidence for specific interface prescriptions or acceptable institutional thresholds.
