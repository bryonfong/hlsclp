Corpus framing of this source: DepoSim applies repeatable AI simulation and rubric-based feedback to deposition training

Corpus synthesis of this source: Bob Ambrogi’s LawSites report describes DepoSim’s launch, demonstrated capabilities and training rationale. It combines observations from a demonstration with company-reported pilot results and promotional endorsements, rather than presenting measured improvements in lawyers’ performance.

Corpus source-specific finding

AltaClaro presents unlimited, feedback-intensive practice as a solution to scarce deposition training.

Exact returned source excerpt

### The Problem It Aims to Solve Deposition training has long been a gap in associate development at large law firms, AltaClaro says, with he traditional options – hiring actors for role-play exercises or sending associates to off-site programs – being expensive, logistically cumbersome and infrequent.

Exact returned source excerpt

A junior associate may handle very few practice depositions before being put in front of a real witness, where the stakes are high and the client’s patience is limited.

Corpus source-specific finding

The demonstration shows configurable, live spoken practice rather than a static question script.

Exact returned source excerpt

DepoSim uses text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and large language model capabilities to voice the witness, opposing counsel and court reporter in real time.

Exact returned source excerpt

The user speaks their questions aloud; the witness and opposing counsel respond dynamically.

Corpus source-specific finding

The feedback system evaluates advocacy skills and can support partner coaching.

Exact returned source excerpt

In the demo, the feedback began streaming in within minutes of the deposition concluding and covered a detailed breakdown across multiple skill categories, including the opening introduction, witness identity confirmation, oath administration, ground rules, educational and work background development, exhibit handling, questioning technique, follow-up skill, witness management, time management and articulacy.

Exact returned source excerpt

For each category, the feedback identifies what went well, what needed improvement, and how to improve, along with specific transcript references, citing the point in the transcript where a particular issue arose.

Corpus source-specific finding

Early evidence establishes favorable participant reactions, not measured skill gains.

Exact returned source excerpt

The launch follows testing with a notable early-adopter cohort that participated in a five-week beta pilot.

Exact returned source excerpt

The cohort includes six Am Law 100 firms: Orrick, Herrington & Sutcliffe; K&L Gates; McDermott Will & Emery; Littler Mendelson; Taft Stettinius & Hollister; and Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck.

Corpus source-specific finding

The partnership joins training design with AI infrastructure and plans broader advocacy scenarios.

Exact returned source excerpt

### The AltaClaro-Verbit Partnership The two companies began their collaboration at Legalweek 2025 and formally launched a beta in fall 2025.

Exact returned source excerpt

The division of labor follows each company’s core competency: AltaClaro designed the pedagogy, the learner experience and the realistic deposition scenarios; Verbit built the underlying technology, including high-speed secure transcription and the AI agent capabilities that power the simulated witness and counsel.
