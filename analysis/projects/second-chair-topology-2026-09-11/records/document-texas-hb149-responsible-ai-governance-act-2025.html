Corpus framing of this source: Texas H.B. 149: Statewide AI duties, prohibitions, oversight and enforcement

Corpus synthesis of this source: H.B. 149 establishes a statewide Texas framework for artificial-intelligence governance. It defines covered actors, preempts local AI regulation, imposes disclosure and conduct rules, centralises enforcement in the attorney general, creates program oversight and an advisory council, and adds AI use to state-agency inventories and reviews. The supplied text contains statutory requirements and qualifications rather than empirical findings or forecasts.
