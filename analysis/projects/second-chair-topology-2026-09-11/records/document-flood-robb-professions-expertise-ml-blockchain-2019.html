Corpus framing of this source: AI, Blockchain and the Reconfiguration of Professional Expertise

Corpus synthesis of this source: Flood and Robb examine how AI and Blockchain affect professional knowledge, work and economic organisation, using law as their central case. They argue that complex legal tasks can be automated but that tacit and interactional dimensions of professional expertise constrain pure automation. They identify current legal-AI applications, explain law’s comparatively slow adoption, anticipate both substitution and augmentation, and locate lawyers’ durable value in the trusted-advisor role. They separately report labour-market theories and forecasts from Bostrom, Turner, Henderson and Deloitte.
