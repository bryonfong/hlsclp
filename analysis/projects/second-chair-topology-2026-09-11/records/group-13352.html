Disruption theory: Weak historical fit makes business-model displacement conditional

Corpus synthesis: The exemplars reject disruption as an automatic consequence of AI adoption or a reliable prediction of incumbent displacement. Historical cases may fit the theory only weakly or through retrospective adjustment, while credible disruption in practice requires deliberately separated experiments with independent resources and incentives.
