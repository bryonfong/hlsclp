Corpus framing of this source: In-house counsel’s rising authority and its uneven international diffusion

Corpus synthesis of this source: This 2012 article traces how U.S. general counsel gained purchasing power, professional prestige and corporate influence, then assesses preliminary evidence from Brazil, India and China. It distinguishes the movement’s successful claims to authority from uncertain cost-control outcomes and cautions that department size, stronger credentials and recruitment growth do not necessarily establish greater influence.
