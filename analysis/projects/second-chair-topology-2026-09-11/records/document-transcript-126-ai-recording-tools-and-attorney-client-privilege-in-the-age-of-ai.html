Corpus synthesis of this source: Bridget McCormack and Jen Leonard examine how AI recording tools add data-handling, confidentiality and professional-responsibility risks to longstanding recording-law concerns. They balance those risks against better note-taking and client recall, distinguish their interpretations from a cited New York ethics opinion, and recommend context-sensitive safeguards rather than uniform adoption.
