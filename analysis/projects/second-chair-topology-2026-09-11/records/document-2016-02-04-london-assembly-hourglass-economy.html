Open topic: Hourglass labour: Employment growth can block progression

Corpus framing of this source: London’s recovery concealed weaker job quality, stalled progression and widening low pay

Corpus synthesis of this source: The London Assembly Economy Committee’s February 2016 report examines London’s labour market mainly over 2008–2015. It combines commissioned GLA Economics analysis, official statistics, employer surveys and witness evidence to argue that employment growth alone cannot establish labour-market success. Its hourglass account concerns the erosion of mid-skilled occupations across London’s economy. It recommends mayoral action on skills, employment conditions, flexible hiring, living wages and devolution.
