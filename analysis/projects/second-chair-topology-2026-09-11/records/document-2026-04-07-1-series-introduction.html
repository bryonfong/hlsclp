Corpus framing of this source: AI as a systemic transformation of the legal profession

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anthea Roberts and David Wilkins present a provisional research framework linking AI, complex decision-making and law's structural role in business. They argue that AI changes professional judgment, competition and training—not merely tool adoption—and identify platform capture and weakened expertise development as unresolved risks. Their contribution draws on practical AI experience, teaching observations and research on the profession rather than presenting measured effects.
