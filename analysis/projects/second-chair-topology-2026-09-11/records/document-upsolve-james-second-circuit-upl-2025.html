Corpus framing of this source: Upsolve, Inc. v. James: standing, access to justice and First Amendment treatment of UPL rules

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Second Circuit situates the challenge in New York’s high-volume debt-collection system, holds that the plaintiffs have standing to seek preliminary relief, and reasons that the UPL statutes regulate protected communication as applied to their proposed legal advice while remaining content neutral. It also records and answers the Attorney General’s characterization of the statutes as regulation of antecedent professional conduct.
