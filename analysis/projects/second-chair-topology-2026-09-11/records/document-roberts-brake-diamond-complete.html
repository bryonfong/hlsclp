Corpus framing of this source: Diamond-complete expertise: combining depth, breadth and AI fluency

Corpus synthesis of this source: Anthea Roberts and Sue Brake (2026) offer a conceptual account of how AI changes the value of expertise. They argue that domain depth, cross-disciplinary breadth and the ability to direct and check AI must develop together. The supplied material presents an argument and predictions, not measured evidence of employment or productivity effects; its diamond describes individual competence, not firm staffing.

Corpus source-specific finding

The authors treat the structure of expertise as a better guide to AI exposure than job title, industry or seniority.
