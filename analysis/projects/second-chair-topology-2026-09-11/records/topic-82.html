Corpus synthesis of this source: Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices (AI-Enabled Medical Device List) contributes a regulatory inventory rather than a performance study or policy argument. It records devices across decades and clinical panels, links them to FDA decisions, and explains how publication timing and sponsor disclosures affect identification and future updates. The supplied material offers no comparative clinical outcomes, labour-market predictions or claims that AI replaces clinicians.
