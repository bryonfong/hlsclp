  1. Human accountability remains the governing principle for legal AI use.
  2. Hallucination cases establish concrete risks but do not measure general model reliability.
  3. Technical safeguards can reduce some errors, but verification remains broader than citation checking.
  4. Authorities disagree over whether ordinary rules need AI-specific disclosure or task restrictions.
