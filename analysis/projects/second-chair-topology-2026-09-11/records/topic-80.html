Corpus synthesis of this source: Despite the supplied source label referring to Fifth Circuit AI-use certification, these excerpts describe Judge Brantley Starr’s trial procedures. The repeated passages state courtroom requirements and recommendations, rather than competing views or empirical findings.

Corpus source-specific finding

Trial demonstratives generally are not evidence, with an exception for necessary charts and summaries.

Exact returned source excerpt

Generally speaking, the Court treats documents created for trial as demonstratives that get marked as exhibits for identification only.

Exact returned source excerpt

Such documents are not evidence and do not go back to the jury, but they may be published to the jury during trial.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court requires advance disclosure and resolves evidentiary disputes outside the jury’s hearing where possible.

Exact returned source excerpt

Exhibit Objections at Trial: If time allows, the Court will rule on exhibit objections at the pretrial conference.

Exact returned source excerpt

Time rarely allows.

Corpus source-specific finding

Preliminary rulings do not admit exhibits, and email contents require separate evidentiary support.

Exact returned source excerpt

The Court will reserve ruling on foundation or authenticity objections until trial when the witness sets the foundation, unless the foundation is set with a business records affidavit that complies with Federal Rules of Evidence 803(6) and 902(11).

Exact returned source excerpt

Note that with regard to emails, a business records affidavit from an entity like Google authenticating emails in a Gmail account overcomes hearsay and authenticity objections only for the header portion of the email and not the content of the email.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court limits examination scope and preapproves jury-selection questions.

Exact returned source excerpt

Scope of Cross Examination and Recalling Witnesses: Unless there is an agreement by the parties otherwise, the Court requires the scope of cross examination to not exceed the scope of direct examination (and recross to not exceed redirect).

Exact returned source excerpt

If the defense has designated and subpoenaed a witness also on the plaintiff/government’s witness list, counsel should confer before the pretrial conference on whether they will call that witness once and allow wide-open cross.

Corpus source-specific finding

Starr restricts objections that interrupt argument or expose jurors to legal debate.

Exact returned source excerpt

Objections During Opening and Closing: The Court highly disfavors objections during opening or closing, as attorneys frequently use them to disrupt the rhythm of opposing counsel’s argument.

Exact returned source excerpt

The Court will call for a sidebar following an opening or closing, at which point opposing counsel may seek a curative instruction or new trial at that time.

Corpus source-specific finding

Trial time limits reward efficient presentation while allowing justified extensions.

Exact returned source excerpt

Time Limits: The Court imposes a time limit on civil trials and some criminal trials.

Exact returned source excerpt

The Court will set the allowable time at the pretrial conference or written order and specify the number of hours of trial time per party.

Corpus source-specific finding

The guidance encourages early settlement and requires disclosure of settlements during trial.

Exact returned source excerpt

Settlements During Trial: Parties should strive to settle at least two weeks before trial.

Exact returned source excerpt

However, if parties settle during trial, the terms of the settlement agreement must be either filed on the docket in unredacted form or disclosed in open court for the case to be moot and the jury to be discharged.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court takes defendants on pretrial release into custody after a guilty jury verdict.

Exact returned source excerpt

Verdict in Criminal Cases: When a criminal defendant is on pretrial release and the jury returns a guilty verdict, the Court takes the defendant into custody.
