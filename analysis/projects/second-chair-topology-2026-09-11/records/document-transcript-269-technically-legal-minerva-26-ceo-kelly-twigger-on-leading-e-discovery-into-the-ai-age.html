Corpus framing of this source: Kelly Twigger on technology, AI and institutional change in e-discovery

Corpus synthesis of this source: Twigger argues that electronic evidence and specialist technology make discovery faster, leaner and more analytically useful than paper-based practice, but data volume, cost, lawyer reluctance, technical complexity and unclear procedural rules limit adoption. She describes Minerva 26’s research and generative-AI applications, stresses continuous human review and explainability, advocates early and economical case management, and predicts that AI will reduce review staffing substantially while acknowledging that core e-discovery AI remains immature.
