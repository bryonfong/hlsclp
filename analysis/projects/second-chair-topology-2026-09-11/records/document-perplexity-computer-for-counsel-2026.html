Corpus synthesis of this source: In its 2026 launch announcement, Perplexity describes Computer for Counsel as a multi-model system that connects legal research, institutional knowledge and workplace tools. It argues that automation can free lawyers for judgment and strategy, illustrates claimed workflows, and outlines verification, security and availability provisions. The material offers vendor claims and examples rather than measured legal-work outcomes.
