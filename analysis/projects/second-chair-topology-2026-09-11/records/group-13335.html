Professional authority: Innovation redistributes expertise within existing power structures

Corpus synthesis: The exemplars treat legal change as a contest over expertise, legitimacy and institutional power within an already divided profession, rather than as straightforward technological substitution. AI and specialization may fragment authority through clientele and repeat-player relationships, while organizational integration, political resources and selective access can preserve or renew elite power. They differ in emphasis between field-level competition, elite reproduction and the organization of specialist expertise.
