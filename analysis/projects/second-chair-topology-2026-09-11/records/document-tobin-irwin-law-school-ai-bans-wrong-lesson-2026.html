Builds onAI instruction: Timing divides educational reform

Open topic: AI instruction: Timing divides educational reform

Corpus framing of this source: Teach supervised AI judgment rather than impose default law-school bans

Corpus synthesis of this source: In their 2026 commentary, Donald B. Tobin and Samuel Irwin argue that law schools should integrate critical, supervised AI use into ordinary teaching. They challenge Berkeley’s default restrictions while acknowledging its exceptions, support targeted AI-free exercises, and recommend accessible proofreading tools. Their argument draws on professional competence duties, reported sanctions incidents and analogies to established legal practice, rather than a comparative study of educational outcomes.
