Corpus framing of this source: Proposed Rule 707: admissibility standards for machine-generated evidence

Corpus synthesis of this source: The September 2025 Standing Committee report advances public consultation on Rule 707 after three years of Advisory Committee consideration of AI evidence. The supplied excerpts document proposals, reasoning and committee decisions, rather than empirical findings about AI reliability.
