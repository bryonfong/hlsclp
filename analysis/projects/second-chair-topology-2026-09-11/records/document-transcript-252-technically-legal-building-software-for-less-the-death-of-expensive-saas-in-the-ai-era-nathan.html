Corpus framing of this source: Nathan Wenzel on AI-era software development and legal-software delivery

Corpus synthesis of this source: Wenzel argues that AI-assisted development lowers incremental software costs and expands feasible product scope, but does not eliminate the costs of secure, scalable operations. He distinguishes production engineering from casual “vibe coding,” describes a shift from handwriting code toward specification and testing, and predicts more demanding, responsive vendor-customer relationships.
