Displaced incumbents: Employment recovery can conceal lasting career harm

Corpus synthesis: Historical automation evidence distinguishes the later absorption of new cohorts from the unemployment, mobility and occupational downgrading experienced by workers whose jobs disappear. The exemplars vary between occupational-extinction cases, broader worker-exposure estimates and methodological validity checks, but consistently caution that stable aggregate employment does not establish recovery for displaced incumbents. Generalisation to AI and legal staffing remains limited by incomplete adjustment mechanisms, controls, attrition and study design.
