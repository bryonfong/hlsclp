Corpus framing of this source: TheAgentCompany: measuring autonomous work through a simulated software company

Corpus synthesis of this source: TheAgentCompany contributes a reproducible, 175-task benchmark of multi-step workplace activity. Its experiments find meaningful but limited autonomous capability: Gemini 2.5 Pro completes 30.3% of tasks. The authors explain failures, compare agent designs and costs, and qualify how far this simulated setting can support conclusions about real work.

Partial completion score To provide a more nuanced measure that rewards partial task completion while strongly incentivizing full task completion, we define partial completion score as: Spartial = 0.5 · Result Total + 0.5 · Sfull , where: “Result” is sum of awarded points across all checkpoints (including partial credit), “Total” is sum of the total points for all checkpoints, Result Total is fractional progress toward full completion, and Sfull is binary indicator equal to 1 when the task is fully completed.
