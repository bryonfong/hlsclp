Corpus synthesis of this source: Texas H.B. 149: Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act sets out statutory review criteria and information requirements, rather than empirical findings or predictions. The supplied excerpts address agencies’ own AI use, their oversight of others’ AI use, and AI inventories within a broader framework of effectiveness, public accountability and information governance.
