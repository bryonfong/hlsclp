Corpus framing of this source: Human oversight as a designed, multi-layered risk-control system

Corpus synthesis of this source: Keeping an Eye on AI proposes an interdisciplinary framework and documentation template for effective human oversight. It distinguishes operational work from oversight, specifies monitoring and intervention requirements, and illustrates their application in hiring, air traffic control, automated insulin delivery and radiology. The authors report practical insights from these applications while acknowledging that oversight can fail and that their framework does not resolve all governance or agentic-AI challenges.
