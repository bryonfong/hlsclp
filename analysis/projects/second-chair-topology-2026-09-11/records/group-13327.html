Early-career employment evidence: observed weakness does not identify AI displacement

Corpus synthesis: The exemplars examine whether weaker employment, hiring or job-posting outcomes for young and graduate workers can be attributed to AI. They use varying age, pay, exposure and task-effect comparisons, with some reporting age-concentrated associations or distinguishing automation from augmentation. Conflicting datasets, changing periods, proxy choices and incomplete causal tests leave entry-level displacement—and any resulting legal staffing effect—unproven.
