Corpus framing of this source: European Commission: risk-based AI safeguards, implementation duties and proposed simplification

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Commission’s overview, updated 27 January 2026, explains the AI Act’s rationale, differentiated obligations and phased implementation. It presents regulation and innovation support as complementary, and distinguishes the existing application timetable from proposed amendments. This is the Commission’s explanatory account, not the regulation’s text or an empirical assessment of its effects.

Corpus framing of this source: European Commission overview of the EU AI Act’s risk-based regulatory framework

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Commission presents the AI Act as a trust-building response to AI-specific risks that existing law does not adequately address. It outlines differentiated rules for prohibited, high-risk, transparency-risk, minimal-risk and general-purpose AI, together with oversight responsibilities, compliance support and phased application dates.
