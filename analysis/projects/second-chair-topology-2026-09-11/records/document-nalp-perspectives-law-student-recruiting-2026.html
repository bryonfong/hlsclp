Corpus framing of this source: NALP documents earlier, employer-led recruiting alongside smaller summer classes

Corpus synthesis of this source: NALP’s January 2026 release reports results from its 2025 law-student recruiting survey. It documents accelerated hiring for 2026 summer programs, employer-sponsored recruiting’s predominance, lower callback-to-offer rates and record offer acceptance. Executive Director Nikia Gray separately warns about recruiting’s pressure on the first-year curriculum and suggests widening recruitment pipelines.

Corpus source-specific finding

Employer-sponsored recruiting dominated hiring for 2026 2L summer programs.

Exact returned source excerpt

During the 2025 recruiting cycle for 2026 2L summer programs, 80% of offers resulted from employer sponsored recruiting, compared to just 20% through law school sponsored methods such as on-campus interviewing (OCI).

Exact returned source excerpt

2025 Recruiting for Summer 2026 Programs • For the second year in a row, direct application was the most frequently cited method by law offices to recruit 2L students (93%), followed by law school recruitment programs (e.g., OCI) at 71%, and resume collects (64%).

Corpus source-specific finding

Offer timing moved sharply earlier, especially through employer-sponsored channels.

Exact returned source excerpt

• In 2025, 85% of all offers were extended prior to July, compared to 34% of all offers in 2024.

Exact returned source excerpt

May 2025 was the most popular offer month (36%), followed by June (29%), and then April (15%).

Corpus source-specific finding

Callback-to-offer rates fell while acceptance reached another record.

Exact returned source excerpt

• In 2025, 47% of callback interviews resulted in offers for summer 2026 positions, a decrease of two percentage points from the previous recruiting cycle.

Exact returned source excerpt

Since 2023, offer rates have fallen below the historical range of 50-58% observed from 2014-2022.

Corpus source-specific finding

Aggregate offer volume stayed flat, while the median office extended a record-low number of offers.

Exact returned source excerpt

• Overall, total offer volume for summer 2026 2L programs was essentially flat, increasing by just 0.4% as compared to the prior recruiting cycle.

Exact returned source excerpt

• The median number of 2L 2026 summer program offers extended per office was four, an all- time low.

Corpus source-specific finding

Post-graduation offers to 2L summer associates remained strong.

• The offer rate from 2L summer programs (to return post-graduation as an entry-level associate at the firm) held steady at 97%, unchanged from 2024.

Exact returned source excerpt

The 2025 rate was 89.4%, down just slightly from the record high of 89.6% in 2024.

Corpus source-specific finding

Firms offered more 1L summer associates a return place, but acceptance weakened.

• The offer rate to 1L summer associates (to return for the 2L 2026 summer program) increased by 3.5 percentage points to 94.2%, an all-time high.

Exact returned source excerpt

The acceptance rate for those offers declined by three percentage points, dropping to 72.3%.
