Corpus framing of this source: Bourdieu and organizational analysis: NASA as a layered organizational field

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source applies Bourdieu’s concepts of field, capital and habitus to NASA’s Challenger launch decision. It uses a relational, historically grounded case study to explain how layered institutions, organizational pressures, positional power and normalized practices reproduced a flawed decision. It argues for greater attention to meso-level organizations and multiple social locations in Bourdieusian analysis, while stressing the framework’s complexity and proposing comparative research on organizational reproduction and change.
