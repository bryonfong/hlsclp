Corpus synthesis of this source: The Practice Note permits AI-assisted preparation of court documents within a risk-control framework applying to lawyers, litigants and witnesses from 14 May 2026. It presents access to justice as a potential benefit while treating fairness, accountability, privacy and accuracy as continuing responsibilities. The supplied text makes no predictions.
