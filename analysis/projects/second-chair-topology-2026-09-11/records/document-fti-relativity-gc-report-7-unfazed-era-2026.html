Corpus synthesis of this source: FTI Technology and Relativity’s 2026 General Counsel Report combines interviews and survey evidence to describe rapid generative-AI adoption, stronger technology planning and continued capability gaps. It also reports differing legal and technology leaders’ risk perceptions and respondents’ expectations for legal operations and outside providers.
