Native topic findings

  1. ROSS’s shutdown illustrates the claim that litigation can remove a legal-AI competitor from the market before courts resolve the merits.
  2. The underlying dispute pits protection of proprietary research content against ROSS’s allegations of exclusionary litigation.
  3. Operational survival and the capacity to litigate depended on different funding arrangements.
  4. The immediate customer consequence was service discontinuity, requiring data export and migration.
  5. Wider benefits for competition and access to justice were aspirations, not demonstrated results.
