Corpus framing of this source: Law as Code & the AI Power Shift: Fit-for-purpose legal AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: Scott Shapiro presents AI as a supplementary social technology for administering increasingly complex law, while rejecting a one-model-fits-all approach. He favours explainable formal reasoning for precise rules, specialised data-rich models and interdisciplinary research, but stresses human dissent, professional responsibility and jurisprudential limits. He predicts automation of some legal work and substantial access-to-justice benefits, subject to choices about data, institutional design and social purpose.
