Corpus framing of this source: The Input Layer: Prompt Engineering Isn't Dead. You're Just Doing It Wrong.

Corpus synthesis of this source: Zack Shapiro argues that effective professional AI use depends less on model outputs or generic prompting tricks than on humans precisely encoding context, constraints, analysis and judgment. Drawing on his AI-native legal practice, legal hallucination incidents, industry reports and experiments with reusable AI skills, he distinguishes AI-assisted execution from abdication of cognitive responsibility and recommends rigorous specification and verification.
