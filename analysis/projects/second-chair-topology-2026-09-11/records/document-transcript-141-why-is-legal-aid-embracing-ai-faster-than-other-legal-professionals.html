Corpus framing of this source: Legal aid’s comparatively rapid but guarded adoption of AI

Corpus synthesis of this source: In this 2025 Talk Justice discussion, Cat Moon, Joanne Sprague, Jennifer Zelnick and Angela Tripp interpret survey findings on legal aid’s AI uptake. They attribute adoption to legal aid’s established creativity, resource scarcity and concern that AI could widen the justice gap; distinguish individual experimentation from organisation-wide deployment; and recommend affordable access, ethical governance, legal-specific privacy safeguards and continuous accuracy evaluation. They remain cautious about survey comparability, present accessibility and future accuracy as tentative, and identify privacy, hallucinations and professional ethics as material barriers.
