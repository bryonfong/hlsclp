Corpus framing of this source: Practical, adaptive AI guidance for legal aid, consumer tools and regulation

Corpus synthesis of this source: Shellie Reid and Sam Harden argue for practical, rapidly adaptable guidance rather than quickly obsolete AI policies. They identify bias, hallucinations, privacy risks, inconsistent unauthorized-practice treatment and poor consumer-facing responses as central concerns, while viewing generative AI as a potential access-to-justice tool. Harden’s experiments suggest that actionable steps and disclaimers strongly shape legal professionals’ assessments of model answers. Cat Moon endorses proactive, risk-aware engagement but speaks as the host rather than for Reid or Harden.
