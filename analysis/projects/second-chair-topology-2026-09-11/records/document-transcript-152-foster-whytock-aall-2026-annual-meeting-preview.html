Corpus framing of this source: AALL leadership, professional identity and preparation for an AI-integrated future

Corpus synthesis of this source: Jenny Foster argues that AI will principally force legal information professionals to redefine and communicate their professional identity, rather than merely master new technology. She and Jessica Whytock present inclusive participation, transparent leadership pathways, education, advocacy and broader collaboration as AALL’s main tools for navigating that change. Foster grounds these proposals in current member leadership and her administration’s appointment practices; Whytock qualifies the outlook by stressing that the profession’s five-year trajectory remains uncertain. Other speakers separately credit Foster’s leadership and cite continuity in AALL’s advocacy.
