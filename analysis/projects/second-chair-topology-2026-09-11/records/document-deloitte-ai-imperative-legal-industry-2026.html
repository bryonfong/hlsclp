Corpus synthesis of this source: Drawing on a survey of more than 100 senior legal-department leaders across sectors, regions and department sizes, supplemented by law-firm interviews and wider ecosystem engagement, Deloitte argues that legal AI has moved rapidly into deployment but has not yet delivered its full value. It attributes the gap primarily to technology-heavy investment and insufficient attention to strategy, data, people, workflow redesign, governance and commercial-model change. The report predicts substantial changes in legal work, workforce composition, sourcing and law-firm pricing, and recommends a staged transformation programme rather than isolated technology adoption. It qualifies its forecasts by noting uneven maturity, limited realised provider benefits, uncertainty about sourcing models and the need for professional advice before acting.
