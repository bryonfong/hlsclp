Corpus framing of this source: The Pyramid and the Hourglass: redesigning work around human–agent teams

Corpus synthesis of this source: In his March 2026 essay, Somak Chattopadhyay argues that agentic AI reshapes corporate staffing rather than simply flattening hierarchies. Drawing on company examples, external forecasts and survey findings, he advocates outcome-driven human–agent teams while warning that removing entry-level roles and dispersing automated decisions can undermine talent development and human accountability.

Builds onWork redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Open topic: Work redesign: Jagged capability demands selective deployment

Corpus framing of this source: The Pyramid and the Hourglass: Goal-Driven Organizations in the Agentic-AI Era

Corpus synthesis of this source: The source argues that agentic AI will reshape organizational design from person-centred hierarchies toward fluid structures organized around outcomes, goals and accountability. It highlights unresolved liability, oversight, workforce-development and contextual-judgment risks, then recommends outcome-based management, deliberate entry-level development, hire-like scrutiny of AI agents and process instrumentation that preserves human inspection. Its support is conceptual and illustrative rather than empirical.
