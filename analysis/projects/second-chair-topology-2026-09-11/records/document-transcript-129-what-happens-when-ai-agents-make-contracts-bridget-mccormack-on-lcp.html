Corpus framing of this source: AI capability risks, evidentiary integrity and legal preparation for agentic commerce

Corpus synthesis of this source: The discussion identifies systemic risks from rapidly diffusing AI capabilities, deepfake documents and contracts formed by AI agents. The speakers ground these claims in observed market activity, release practices and illustrative risk scenarios rather than quantitative evidence.
