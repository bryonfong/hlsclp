Corpus framing of this source: Texas preliminary rules for licensed nonlawyer legal services

Corpus synthesis of this source: Preliminary Approval of Rules Governing Licensed Legal Paraprofessionals and Licensed Court-Access Assistants (Misc. Docket No. 24-9050) sets out a regulated model for limited legal services to low-income clients. The supplied excerpts specify eligibility, permitted independent and supervised work, client protections and disciplinary oversight, and begin a separate court-access-assistant framework. They present regulatory provisions, not empirical findings, forecasts or competing stakeholder views.

Corpus framing of this source: Texas Supreme Court proposal for licensed legal paraprofessionals and court-access assistants

Corpus synthesis of this source: The Supreme Court of Texas preliminarily approved and invited comment on rules permitting two categories of licensed non-attorneys to provide limited services to low-income people. The order grounds the proposal in unmet civil legal need and combines expanded representation with defined scope, disclosure, sponsorship, compensation, reporting and privilege safeguards.

Corpus source-specific finding

The court presents the rules as a preliminary access-to-justice measure rather than a final regime.

Exact returned source excerpt

24-9050 ════════════════════ Preliminary Approval of Rules Governing Licensed Legal Paraprofessionals and Licensed Court-Access Assistants ════════════════════════════════════════════════════ This Order invites public comments on proposed new and amended rules that allow licensed legal paraprofessionals and licensed court-access assistants to provide certain limited legal services to low-income individuals.
