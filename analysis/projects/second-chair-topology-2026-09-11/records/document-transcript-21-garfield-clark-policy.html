Corpus framing of this source: Education and adaptability in an automated workforce

Corpus synthesis of this source: In Greg Clark: From Middlesbrough To Minister, Clark endorses stronger workforce education as AI and automation change work. He draws on historical observations about mechanisation, rather than quantitative evidence, to argue for cognitive skills, training in machine interaction and preparation for changing tasks throughout a career.
