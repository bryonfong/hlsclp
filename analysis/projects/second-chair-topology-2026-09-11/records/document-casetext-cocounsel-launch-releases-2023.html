Corpus source-specific finding

Casetext uses testing scale and privacy assurances to distinguish CoCounsel from general-purpose models.

Exact returned source excerpt

"The power of this technology, deployed in a product that is secure and reliable, is a huge leap forward in what legal technology can do." Unlike generalized, publicly available large language models, CoCounsel is a product that offers the reliability and security critical for legal matters, which are specialized, complex, and confidential.

Exact returned source excerpt

Casetext's Trust Team, which has run every legal skill on the platform through thousands of internal tests, has spent nearly 4,000 hours training and fine-tuning CoCounsel's output based on over 30,000 legal questions.

Corpus source-specific finding

Casetext treats GPT-4’s reported bar-exam performance as evidence of reasoning capabilities relevant to professional legal AI.

Exact returned source excerpt

"The model's ability not just to generate text, but to interpret it, heralds nothing short of a new age in the practice of law." Casetext collaborated with researchers Dan Katz and Michael Bommarito from Stanford's Center for Legal Informatics to study GPT-4's performance on the UBE.

Exact returned source excerpt

Their forthcoming paper shows that GPT-4, however, passed the multiple-choice portion and both components of the written portion, exceeding not only all prior large language models' scores, but also the average score of real-life bar exam test takers.
