SCE058

A 2026 randomised experiment finds AI-assisted synthesis did not erode later unaided legal reasoning.

Our experiment provides some of the first causal evidence that AI use in legal work does not inevitably erode independent legal reasoning: when used to assist an initial synthesis task, AI improved participants’ intermediate work product and, in turn, their later reasoning performance even after AI was removed.
