What it is built from+

Second Chair is this project’s evidence base: 600 works on AI and the legal profession, 1953–2026 — scholarship, industry evidence, court materials and practitioner interviews — each read end to end. The Analysis section applies two published analytical lenses: topic mapping and actor mapping, with driver and dynamics mapping and scenario mapping in progress.

Momentum

Who is gaining ground, and on what evidence

This is a field in motion, so the first thing worth knowing about an actor is which way it is going. Each call carries its own dated evidence and a horizon — a funding round, a licence granted, a product shipped, a rule changed. Rising does not mean winning and declining does not mean irrelevant: several declining actors still hold the levers the response portfolio depends on. Select any card for the full reasoning.
