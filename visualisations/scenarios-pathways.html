What this shows+

The 2×2 answers where legal production can sit. This answers how it arrives: five gates in sequence, each a threshold on an accumulating quantity, each with three real options. A pathway is one coherent route through all five. Click any route for its full reading.

How to read it+
Each column is a gate and each row is an option. A pathway's height at a column is the option it takes there — so the lines part exactly where the futures do.
Ringed markers are gates with no measurable threshold. Three of the five. They cannot be watched, only recognized afterwards, and that is the set's largest inherited weakness.
Every duration is anchored on a prior cycle — expert systems, legal process outsourcing, predictive coding — because none of the 2×2's evidence predates 2024 and a time-based reading needs cases that finished.
