What this shows+

Two questions do the work. How deeply are direction and verification encoded into reusable systems rather than held in practitioners — and who controls the interface, the judged-work record and the operative criteria. Crossing them gives four settlements. Nothing here carries a probability: a curated corpus measures curation, not likelihood.
