What is carried instead of probabilities

WatchabilityThe nominal count is four per scenario. The WATCHABLE count — signposts carrying a sourced baseline that can be re-measured today — is lower for two scenarios, and both are at the standard's floor of three rather than above it.
ConsequenceBR-2 and BR-3 — the two branching conditions that matter most — are each watched by one sourced signpost and one unmeasured one. BR-1 alone is watched by two sourced signposts. Each unwatchable signpost now names the instrument that would close the gap.
What this shows+

This scenario set assigns no probabilities, so the register does the work a probability would: each signpost is a re-measurable indicator with a sourced, dated baseline and three threshold bands, chosen so that movement discriminates between settlements rather than merely confirming one.

How to read it+
The three rungs are baseline, amber and red. Click any signpost for the full band definitions and the basis for them.
Not measured marks a quantity that has no baseline anyone publishes. It is recorded as unknown rather than filled with an estimate.
Per-settlement sets are disjoint — no signpost is shared between settlements, so movement on one points somewhere specific. Three cross-cutting signposts are marked as non-discriminating.
